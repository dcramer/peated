Bowmore 2005 / 2x Ben Nevis 1996 (The Whisky Agency)

An introduction to three releases.

Ben Nevis 30 yo 1996 (43,4%, The Whisky Agency 2026, sherry butt)

Nose: Citrus, nuts, and mineral notes.

Mouth: Bright fruit with gentle spice.

Finish: Long and complex.

Ben Nevis 30 yo 1996 (45,3%, The Whisky Agency & Sansibar 2026, hogshead)

Nose: Creamy citrus and chalk.

Mouth: Green fruit and honey.

Score: 90/100

Bowmore 20 yo 2005 (48,8%, The Whisky Agency 2026, refill sherry hogshead)

Nose: Light smoke and sea spray.

Mouth: Tropical fruit and sweet malt.

Score: 91/100
