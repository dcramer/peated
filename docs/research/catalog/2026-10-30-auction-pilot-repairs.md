# Repairs after the auction recheck pilot — October 30, 2026

Where to find the facts behind Bottles that the auction recheck pilot created
from saved October 1 proposals (see the
[rollout guide](../../operations/auctions.md#rollout-status)). Those proposals
predate the current Brand, Series, and edition rules. Each section covers one
release. The identity rules are in `docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`.

Most facts here come from Scotch Whisky Auctions lot photos, read as
[Catalog Research](../../operations/catalog-research.md) describes.

## Ardnamurchan AD/05:15 CK.181

- The front label prints `AD/05:15 CK.181`, 58.8%, bottle number of 255, and
  "Exclusively bottled for The Good Spirits Co.'s 10th Birthday 1st June 2021".
  It is an official Ardnamurchan bottling with no bottler.
- Unknown: the age. The label prints none. Whisky Shop auction titles say 5
  years old, but a 2015 distillation bottled for June 2021 would be 6, so the
  age stays empty.

## Glen Garioch 1998 single cask 4633

- The label prints "Distilled 1998", single cask, cask 4633 filled November
  1998, bottled 30.11.12, 210 bottles, 61%, and "individually chosen on 7th
  September 2012 by World Duty Free Stansted, World of Whiskies".
  [Moodie Davitt](https://moodiedavittreport.com/wdfg-pioneers-regional-airport-bottlings-of-glen-garioch-whisky/)
  covers the airport cask series.
- Unknown: the printed age. The photo hides the digit after "the following
  1". Auction titles say 13 years old; November 1998 to November 2012 is 14.

## BenRiach 1997 single cask 85089

- The label prints "Limited 1997 Release", "Specially selected & bottled
  exclusively for the UK", cask 85089, aged 18 years, distilled 18/06/1997,
  bottled SEP/15, bourbon barrel, 167 bottles, 59.5%.

## Ballindalloch 2015 Vintage Release

- The label prints "2015 Distilled", "Vintage Release", "Bottled 2024
  Exclusively for UK", limited run of 3600, 46%. No age is printed; retailers
  say 8 years.
- Conflict: Whiskybase and some EU shops list a 2015 Vintage Release at 48.5%
  or 49.5%. These may be other markets' bottlings; only the 46% UK label was
  seen.

## Bruichladdich single cask GD01 (Grand Drams)

- Grand Drams' own artwork label prints the Grand Drams crest, "Bruichladdich
  Single Cask #GD01 First Fill Sherry", 15 years old, 280 bottles, 50.3%. It is
  an independent bottling, so Grand Drams is the Brand and bottler.
- Grand Drams' shop page is
  [granddrams.com](https://www.granddrams.com/shop/p/bruichladdich-15-year-old-single-sherry-cask-gd01-grand-drams).
  Whiskybase-derived listings give a 2023 bottling; the label photo does not
  show it.

## Bimber USA Edition (cask 400)

- [Bimber's home page](https://www.bimberdistillery.co.uk/home) names "The
  Spirit of the Game Collection": the USA, Mexico, and Canada Editions, single
  casks for the 2026 World Cup host nations. The site is a Wix page; a
  Shopify-style `products.json` read returns HTML.
- The label and tube print "USA Edition", single cask, cask ref 400, 247
  bottles, 56.3%, bottled June 2026. Bimber's 2021 Country Collection also had
  USA casks; they are separate releases.

## Craigellachie 14-year-old Flora & Fauna

- 43%, a 1990s United Distillers bottling in the Flora & Fauna range
  ([The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/1800/craigellachie-14-year-old-flora-fauna)).
  Flora & Fauna is a multi-distillery collection and already a Brand in the
  catalog. About two dozen older Flora & Fauna Bottles still use the
  distillery as Brand and put "Flora & Fauna" in the name.

## Glenfarclas The Family Casks

- Sherry hogshead 2132, distilled 2001, bottled 26 July 2016, 291 bottles,
  58.9% ([Scotch Whisky Auctions](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/224-the-175th-auction/856603-glenfarclas-2001-family-cask-2132/)).
- Trap: the catalog has two Glenfarclas Series for this range, "The Family
  Casks" and a later duplicate "Family Casks". Use "The Family Casks".

## Thompson Bros Invergordon 1990

- 32 years old, 51.1%, 1990–2022
  ([The Whisky Shop auctions](https://www.whiskyshop.com/auctions/a90027-invergordon-1990-32-year-old-thompson-bros)).
- Trap: the catalog has two Thompson Bros Entities, one used as Brand and one
  as bottler.
