# Bottles That Blocked The Generated Description Cleanup — October 29, 2026

Clearing the old model-written Bottle descriptions stopped on four products
whose stored records broke a catalog rule: one duplicate pair, and three
Bottles named only with their Brand. This record covers where the facts for
those four products live. It is not a full inventory of any of these Brands.

## Port Charlotte 10-year-old Second Limited Edition

Bruichladdich's official release, the follow-up to the first 10-year-old
Port Charlotte of 2012. It was entered twice, so the two records were one
Bottle.

- [Whisky Saga](https://www.whiskysaga.com/blog/port-charlotte-10-second-limited-edition-2016)
  and [Whisky Advocate](https://whiskyadvocate.com/bruichladdich-black-art-octomore-port-charlotte-laddie-10)
  agree on 50% ABV and 18,000 bottles. Whisky Israel's
  [tasting post](https://www.whiskyisrael.co.il/2017/02/28/port-charlotte-10-year-old-second-limited-edition/)
  and [WhiskyCast](https://whiskycast.com/?p=29100) add the cask mix
  (first-fill bourbon, sherry, Tempranillo, French wine) and 40 ppm.
- Dates conflict: one source gives a 2016-10-19 bottling, Whisky Advocate a
  March 2017 release, and a Japanese retailer a December 2016 launch. Release
  and bottling years stay unknown until a producer source settles them.
- One source says distilled 2006. No label image was checked for a printed
  `Distilled` line, so the vintage stays unknown.
- It is an official release, so it has no bottler.

## The Lost Distilleries Blend

A batch-numbered blended Scotch made from closed distilleries by The Blended
Whisky Company, Atom Supplies' sister label to That Boutique-y Whisky Company.
The marketed name is "The Lost Distilleries Blend"; the company is the label
it is released under, so under identity rule 4 it is the Brand and the
bottler, and the batch is the `edition`. Its sister product The Half Century
Blend already used that shape.

- [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/the-blended-whisky-company/the-lost-distilleries-blend-batch-10-whisky/?sku=67343)
  lists the product under The Blended Whisky Company.
- The [World Whiskies Awards 2016](https://worldwhiskiesawards.com/winner-whisky/gold-medal-10-world-whiskies-awards-2016)
  record Batch 5 at 50.4% (gold) and Batch 6 (bronze).
- [Whisky Notes](https://www.whiskynotes.be/?p=28361) gives Batch 10 at 51%
  with 1,041 bottles and its distillery list; Batch 6 was 534 bottles.
- No complete list of batches was found. Batches other than 4 and 6 are not
  in Peated yet.

## Kentucky Vintage

A Willett small-batch bourbon. The label reads "Kentucky Vintage – Straight
Kentucky Bourbon Whiskey", 90 proof, with batch codes such as `14-11`, and
names "Kentucky Bourbon Distillers, Ltd. d/b/a Kentucky Vintage Distillery".
Willett's sister brand Rowan's Creek is named the same way in Peated
(`Straight Kentucky Bourbon`).

- [Brian Haara's review](https://brianhaara.com/2015/07/08/sippn-corn-bourbon-review-kentucky-vintage-straight-kentucky-bourbon-whiskey/)
  quotes the label wording, proof, batch code, and bottler line.
- [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky_Vintage) places it in
  Willett's small-batch line with Noah's Mill, Rowan's Creek, and Pure
  Kentucky XO.
- Age is disputed: a UK retailer says 6 to 9 years, an importer listing says
  a minimum of 4 years per mash bill. Stated age stays unknown.
- The Whisky Exchange product page blocks fetching (HTTP 403).

## McFarlane's Reserve Kentucky Straight Bourbon

A low-cost Kentucky bourbon sold mainly through Total Wine. The trade and
reviews title the core bottle "McFarlane's Reserve Kentucky Straight Bourbon".

- [Total Wine](https://www.totalwine.com/spirits/bourbon/small-batch-bourbon/mcfarlanes-reserve-kentucky-straight-bourbon/p/231908750)
  and [Thirty One Whiskey](https://www.thirtyonewhiskey.com/whiskey-review-mcfarlanes-reserve-kentucky-straight-bourbon-whiskey/)
  use that title. Total Wine also lists a Reserve Select and a 13-year-old
  Cabernet Sauvignon barrel finish.
- [Drinkhacker](https://drinkhacker.com/?p=86626) says the label states an
  average age of 3 years, while Total Wine says a minimum of 4 years; the age
  stays unknown. Thirty One Whiskey traces bottling to Lodestone Beverages
  through TTB records; the distillery is not public.
