# Glenfarclas review resolution evidence

This check covers the three releases in Whiskyfun's October 16, 2026 article,
not the full Glenfarclas or Scott's Selection catalog.

- [Whiskyfun's source article](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2026/A-very-lovely-trio-of-Glenfarclas.html)
  identifies the anniversary release, the 1979/2014 Family Casks release for
  The Bow Bar and Shinanoya, and the 1965/2005 Scott's Selection release.
  Bottle totals in its titles describe release outturn, not multipacks.
- [Spink's original auction catalog](https://spink.com/lot/SFW21000122)
  names Scott's Selection, Glenfarclas 1965, a 2005 bottling date, 48.5% ABV,
  and Robert Scott & Co. as the independent bottling business. Under Peated's
  independent-bottler rule the label is the Brand and the featured distillery
  and printed vintage form the Bottle name. The title supports
  `Glenfarclas 1965`; the bottling date remains a separate fact.
- [Just Whisky's exact auction record](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/glenfarclas-1965-2005-scotts-selection-1372535)
  independently corroborates the vintage, bottling year, label and strength.
  Its approximate forty-year age does not establish a stated age.
- [Glenfarclas's anniversary announcement](https://glenfarclas.com/190th-anniversary-limited-edition/)
  establishes the official 2026 release and explains that its 90 British proof
  is 51.4% ABV. [Luvians' exact product record](https://www.luvians.com/shop/whisky/glenfarclas-190th-anniversary/)
  corroborates the 15,000-bottle outturn. Do not interpret British proof as US
  proof or use component ages as stated age.
- [Shinanoya's original product page](https://shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/item/000000002509?category_page_id=ct755)
  identifies its official 1979/2014 cask 8801 selection with The Bow Bar.
  [The exact auction record](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/5152410a/glenfarclas-1979-family-cask-8801-bow-bar-sapporo-and-shinanoya)
  corroborates the 2014 bottling date and 212-bottle outturn.
  [The exact catalog entry](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/66288/glenfarclas-1979)
  records the matching 50.8% strength. This is an official release selected for
  these businesses, not whisky independently bottled under their Brand.

The cited auction and review images have no established reuse license here.
These sources support identity research; this repair does not copy images.
