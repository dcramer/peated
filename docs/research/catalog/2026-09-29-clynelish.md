# Clynelish catalog sources

Research date: September 29, 2026. Scope: official Clynelish releases and
independent bottlings, including discontinued releases. This is a source guide,
not a claim that the historical catalog is complete.

## Broad historical indexes

- [Whiskyfun's Clynelish index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Clynelish.html) links
  dated reviews of official and independent releases, including recent releases
  through 2026. Follow the individual review: the index can contain transcription
  errors, approximate bottling dates, repeated reviews, and photographs explicitly
  described as a similar bottle.
- [Whisky-news' distillery page](https://www.whisky-news.com/En/distilleries/Clynelish.html)
  supplies another historical inventory and links individual reviews. Its dates
  and cask numbers sometimes disagree with other sources; matching age and ABV
  alone is insufficient.
- [Rare Whisky 101's Clynelish index](https://www.rarewhisky101.com/indices/distillery-specific-indices/clynelish-index)
  is useful for historical official bottlings and strength variants. It does not
  cover the independent-bottler catalog.
- Whisky Monitor searches surfaced additional historical releases, but direct
  access timed out during this pass. Whiskybase search results were discovery
  leads only; its catalog was not scraped or used as a bulk source.

Old Clynelish distilled before the new site's opening in 1967 belongs to the
original production site, later named Brora. Do not assign those releases to the
new distillery merely because the label says Clynelish. Repeated annual reviews
of the standard fourteen-year-old do not establish separate marketed releases.

## The Ultimate

[The producer's history page](https://ultimatewhisky.com/history.html) links a
[bottling-history PDF](https://ultimatewhisky.com/ult_hist.pdf). The Clynelish rows
cover bottlings from 1995 through 2019. Year headings can span PDF pages; retain
that context when extracting rows. The
[producer's introduction](https://ultimatewhisky.com/index.html) explains its
label information and non-chill filtration. A bottling year does not establish
a release date, and vintage plus bottling year does not establish stated age.

Cross-check apparent PDF errors against exact releases:

- The 2008 cask listed as `8000064` is `800064` on
  [Fine Drams' exact release page](https://www.finedrams.com/clynelish-8-year-old-2008-cask-800064-the-ultimate-whisky-company.html).
  It also supplies the eight-year age and bottle outturn.
- Likewise, `8000077` is `800077` on
  [the corresponding Fine Drams page](https://www.finedrams.com/clynelish-8-year-old-2008-cask-800077-the-ultimate-whisky-company.html).
- Cask `13216` has an implausible `4.0` strength in the PDF.
  [Whisky Gospel's contemporary review](https://whiskygospel.com/2014/06/04/whisky-review-van-wees-clynelish-1991-22yo/)
  and [Malt Fascination](https://maltfascination.com/2017/11/17/two-clynelishes-from-1991-by-the-ultimate/)
  identify the 1991/2013 release as twenty-two years old at 46%.
- The 2005/2012 row's `8000235` remains suspect. An exact label or another
  reliable source is needed before normalizing that cask number.
- Cask `1270`, distilled in 1980 and bottled in 1995, has conflicting age
  information: thirteen in the producer history and fifteen in
  [Whiskyfun](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2014/More-Clynelish.html).
  The review photograph is described as a similar bottle; it cannot settle the
  label's age statement.
- The 1991/2014 row lists `13213`, while
  [LA Whisky Society](https://www.lawhiskeysociety.com/whiskey/3958/Clynelish-Van-Wees-The-Ultimate-23-yo)
  records a vatting of `13213 & 13214`, twenty-three years old, 46%, with 507
  bottles. Check that combined release before creating two single-cask records.

## Scotch Malt Whisky Society

[The UK archive](https://smws.com/archive) uses a public bottle API. Its archive
request is `https://api.smws.com/api/v1/bottles?store_id=uk&parent_id=74&page=1&minPrice=0&maxPrice=0&perPage=1000`.
Follow every page, then select cask numbers beginning `26.` locally. During this
pass, a distillery filter returned an empty response despite matching archive
entries. This archive is a regional subset, not the complete Society history.

[The US tasting-note archive](https://smwsa.com/tasting-note-archive) links its
[public older-PDF folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1LaoRm6qgahOL-iNonfQEH5EY-HT_4PpU).
The normal Drive folder HTML exposed only an initial portion of the listing;
its public embedded-folder view exposed the Clynelish files, named `026.*.pdf`.
These original notes provide titles, cask codes, age, strength, distillation
month and year, and maturation. A US allocation is not the worldwide outturn.

For newer US entries, use [the website search](https://smwsa.com/search?query=26.)
and follow `data-next-url` until exhausted. Search matches other distillery codes
containing `26`, so inspect the complete cask code. The producer warns that older
product titles may not match the original tasting-note image. For example,
26.210 has conflicting titles in migrated pages. Do not replace its name from a
listing card. The original note for 26.117 identifies _Silky smooth sundowner_,
fourteen years old, distilled April 2001, 56.8%, finished in a second-fill
Sauternes hogshead; a conflicting review-index title is not sufficient to change
that identity. The US note for 26.39 uses _A Surprising Explosion_, while another
public name is _Spirited elan_; preserve the cask identity when reviewing aliases.

  identifies Clynelish Reserve at 51.2%. Packaging volume does not create a

- [Malts' Waxen Sun page](https://www.malts.com/en-us/products/clynelish-waxen-sun-single-malt-whisky-special-releases-2025)

  supplies its 51.6% strength. References saying 51.3% need correction or

- [Wealth Solutions' 1973 release at Whisky-Online](https://www.whisky-online.com/products/clynelish-41-year-old-wealth-solutions-html)
  identifies the forty-one-year-old refill hogshead, cask 16802, at 45.2%.
- [Spink's Casks of Distinction lot](https://spink.com/lot/SFW68000220)
  identifies the 1983/2018 thirty-five-year-old cask 2566 bottled for Dram Good
  Casks. Whiskyfun describes the same cask as selected for Aaron Chan.

Rare Malts, Flora & Fauna, Special Releases, Prima & Ultima, Casks of
Distinction, and Four Corners of Scotland are owner collections. Apply the
current identity model rather than reproducing a separate Series beneath each
distillery. Legacy Clynelish records also include independent releases assigned
to the distillery as Brand; the bottler abbreviation alone is insufficient to
identify their exact cask.

## Other independent bottlers

The linked individual reviews in Whiskyfun cover Cadenhead's releases from
the 1972 vintage through 1990s vintages bottled in the 2010s; Gordon & MacPhail
releases from the 1969 vintage through 2008 whisky bottled in 2025; and
Signatory releases from the 1971 vintage through 2008 whisky bottled in 2025.
Read each linked review rather than only the distillery index. Important traps:

- Cadenhead's 1990/2015 _Cask Ends_ needs evidence that it was a marketed bottle,
  rather than a visitor fill. The 1994/2015 twenty-year-old Sherry Cask has a
  legacy distillery-branded record and needs a Brand repair, not another Bottle.
- Gordon & MacPhail's 2005/2018 twelve-year-old at 55.1% appears under both batch
  `18/012` and cask `308764`. These identify one release. Conversely, `19/090`
  on the 2005/2019 TWE selection is a batch identifier, not reliable evidence
  of a cask number. The generic 1993 Connoisseurs Choice entry needs exact
  release identification before assigning it to the 2019 bottling.
- The 1980/1995 Signatory cask `1271` review describes a miniature. Its stated
  outturn therefore does not establish the total across bottle sizes. Do not
  infer a refill butt where a review merely says it was probably a butt.
- Signatory's 1995/2019 cask `2158` has an inconsistent twenty-year age in the
  index. Obtain its label before accepting that combination.
- The 2008/2019 eleven-year-old at 53% marketed by Whisky Sponge is already
  identifiable as Edition No. 3. Reviews calling it “Signatory for Whisky
  Sponge” do not justify a second Signatory Bottle. Compare the actual label.

[Gordon & MacPhail's distillery page](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/distilleries/clynelish/)
is useful context but does not prove its release history is complete. A search
result for the producer's product PDF numbered `14148` returned 404 when
requested; do not treat the search snippet as a replacement fact sheet.

[The Whisky Exchange's own Retro Label page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/26201/clynelish-18-year-old-sherry-cask-twe-retro-label)
identifies itself as the independent bottler of the eighteen-year-old sherry
release at 50.6%, released for its 2014 Whisky Show, without added colouring.

[Fruitful Spirits' current shop](https://fruitfulspirits.com/shop/) contains
two distinct Clynelish releases:

- [The Rich One, First Edition](https://fruitfulspirits.com/products/ever-curious-the-rich-one-first-edition-clynelish-70cl/)
  is branded **Ever Curious, by Fruitful Spirits** on the producer's front
  label. It is eleven years old, 48%, from refill hogsheads, without colouring
  or chill filtration. The readable label states December 2014 distillation,
  August 2026 bottling, and an outturn of 1,054. Ever Curious does not belong
  under Clynelish as a distillery-owned Series.
- [Fruitful Spirits' eleven-year-old at 55.2%](https://fruitfulspirits.com/products/clynelish-11-year-old-55-2-70cl/)
  is a separate single-cask release: December 2014 distillation, July 2026
  bottling, refill hogshead `326239`, and 299 bottles. The producer explicitly
  states cask strength, no colouring, and no chill filtration.

## Remaining identity questions

Distillers Edition needs exact vintage, bottling year, and label-code matching.
Generic legacy records for 1992 and 1997 cannot safely be assigned to one
yearly release. The 1993 vintage corresponds to the documented 2010 release. [The 2005/2020 auction record](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/lot-251939/clynelish-2005-distillers-edition-2020/auction-29)
is a useful exact anchor. The [historical Distillers Edition list](https://tjederswhisky.se/diageos-distillers-edition-series-an-attempt-at-a-complete-list/)
failed direct web retrieval but was readable through the browser. Its 2015
article documents the seven annual Clynelish releases from 2006 to 2012; it
is not evidence of the current complete range.

The Prima & Ultima 1996 release has conflicting 2022/2023 bottling-year claims;
consult its producer fact sheet or back label before changing either date.
The Ultimate's generic legacy 1991, 1996, and 2008 records need exact cask
identification before Brand repairs or merges.

Visitor hand-fills, including the Highland Festival 2019 hand-fill and a 2025
distillery hand-filled twelve-year-old, are outside the marketed-release scope
defined by the identity model. Milroy's Clynelish new-make spirit is also outside
the whisky catalog. Neither exclusion authorizes deleting existing records.

## Images and access

The Ultimate history and older SMWS notes establish facts but do not supply a
licensed bottle photograph for each release. Retailer and auction product
images require separate reuse permission; public access is not a reuse license.
No blanket image-reuse permission was established for those sites. LA Whisky
Society expressly reserves rights. Do not substitute another cask's image.

Some exact retailer pages failed through the web reader but remained available
as ordinary public HTML. Fine Drams and Whisky-Online were readable through
direct public requests. Keep temporary HTML, PDFs, API replies, and request
files outside the repository.

## Additional historical conflicts

- Cadenhead’s 1992 twenty-one-year-old Small Batch at 51.6% has 792 bottles,
  as recorded by [WhiskyNotes at release in 2014](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2014/clynelish/clynelish-1992-cadenhead-small-batch/)
  and a [contemporary tasting with Mark Watt](https://singlemaltwallonie.blogspot.com/2014/11/compte-rendu-ma-premiere-soiree-avec.html).
  Whiskyfun’s later entry says 592; Whiskyphiles says 2013. The contemporary
  sources support the 2014 release and 792 outturn.
- Adelphi’s 1995 ten-year-old is cask 12783, bottled in 2006 at 59.3%, as
  confirmed by [Whisky Magazine](https://whiskymag.com/tastings/adelphi-1995-cask-12783/)
  and the [Oger–Blanchet auction catalog](https://www.gazette-drouot.com/telechargement/catalogue?venteId=128658).
  Other historical lists have an incorrect cask or bottling year.
- Berry Bros’ US fourteen-year-old 1997 at 55.5% uses casks 4659–4661,
  documented by [a bottle-owner review](https://www.divingforpearlsblog.com/2013/07/single-malt-report-taste-off-four-1997.html)
  and [Chemistry of the Cocktail](https://cocktailchem.blogspot.com/2014/04/whisky-review-berry-brothers-rudd.html).
  Whiskyfun’s 4654–61 appears to be a transcription error. This vatting is
  distinct from the separately marketed single cask 4655.
- Berry Bros’ 1996 eighteen-year-old at 46% is listed as cask 6824 by
  Whiskyfun, while trade listings and contemporary tastings use 6424. Confirm
  the label before assigning the disputed cask identity.
- Malts of Scotland’s [warehouse-shop bottling MoS18026](https://www.whiskyauction.com/item/3706516)
  is a marketed 1997/2018 release, not a visitor hand-fill. Its 127-bottle
  outturn and 47.1% strength are recorded on the exact auction lot.
- Malts of Scotland’s [1998/2012 cask 12025 miniature](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/39265/clynelish-1998-single-cask)
  has a 48-miniature count. The full-size release is reported as 189 bottles;
  package counts must not be substituted for one another.
- [Milroy’s 1997 twenty-three-year-old cask 12359](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/64104/clynelish-1997-23-year-old-milroys)
  was bottled in August 2021. Whiskyfun’s 2012 is a transposition.

## Smaller bottlers and producer archives

[Maltbarn's own search](https://www.maltbarn.com/?s=Clynelish), its
[second page](https://www.maltbarn.com/page/2/?s=Clynelish), and the
[release archive](https://www.maltbarn.com/eigenabfuellungen/) preserve older
Clynelish releases, including numbered bottlings 11, 30, 36, 54, 143, and 147.
The archive has releases absent from Whiskyfun's distillery index. Its product
headings and specification blocks can differ in detail: do not calculate a
printed age when the producer omits it. The same archive establishes The 26 as
a marketed range. No image reuse grant was found.

[Càrn Mòr's Celebration of the Cask archive](https://www.carnmorwhisky.co.uk/celebration-of-the-cask)
preserves the 2020 Clynelish 1993 releases at 52.7% and 51.3%. They are different
casks. The [company history](https://scotchwhisky.com/whiskypedia/2259/morrison-mackay/)
connects Scottish Liqueur Centre to Morrison & MacKay; the former company name
on an older bottle does not imply a new bottler business.

Whiskyfun's full dated reviews, rather than its short index entries, cover
James MacArthur, Kingsbury, Dun Bheagan, Acorn, Svenska Eldvatten, The Whisky
Cask, The Whisky Cask Company, Archives, The Whiskyman, The Whisky Mercenary,
Riegger's Selection, and The Whisky Agency. Its narrative sometimes supplies
an omitted range, cask type, or explicit distinction between two strengths.
The Whisky Cask and The Whisky Cask Company are separate businesses. Do not
resolve an abbreviation such as TWCC solely from a reviewer's tentative guess.

[Liquid Library's contemporary review](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2011/clynelish/clynelish-1998-liquid-library/)
and [auctioneer range history](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/brands/liquid-library)
identify it as a Whisky Agency range. Full dated reviews also identify House
Malt and Private Stock. The 1989/2012 Perfect Dram entry lacks its strength in
the main Whiskyfun heading; the same page's summary supplies 49.7%, and the
[exact retailer page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/17504/clynelish-1989-23-year-old-the-perfect-dram)
is a lead for its further label facts.

## Wemyss release names and age corrections

[The Whiskey Reviewer's contemporary reviews](https://whiskeyreviewer.com/2014/05/wemyss-apple-basket-single-cask-scotch-review-2014_052914/)
connect Apple Basket, Toffee Glaze, and A Day at the Coast to their exact
Clynelish releases. Apple Basket was bottled in 2013 and released in 2014;
its old twenty-three-year age is wrong. [Difford's product entry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/4493/wemyss-malts-apple-basket-1997-clynelish)
provides the April release, sherry hogshead, and filtration/colour facts, but
calls it no-age-statement while describing sixteen years of maturation. Keep
its stated age unknown pending a readable label. A reviewer's subtraction of
years does not establish a printed age.

[Whisky Saga's contemporary Cayenne Cocoa Bean review](https://www.whiskysaga.com/blog/clynelish-1997-cayenne-cocoa-bean)
records the 2014 bottling, hogshead, and outturn. [1423's distributor magazine](https://www.1423noblespirits.se/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/1423-The_Spirit_Magazine.pdf),
printed pages 50–51, shows Redcurrant Jus and Waffles and Ice Cream alongside
their vintages, bottling years, strengths, and outturns. The Waffles figure is
274, not the adjacent Redcurrant Jus figure of 300. [The exact bottle review](https://maltrunners.com/2020/12/06/clynelish-18-year-1997-wemyss-malt-waffles-and-ice-cream/)
confirms eighteen years and cask strength. The magazine reserves all rights;
its bottle photographs are evidence, not a reuse grant. The web PDF screenshot
failed, but the downloaded PDF rendered locally.

## Elixir archive discrepancies

Whiskyfun lists Single Malts of Scotland cask 700050 twice, with 2020 and 2021
bottling years. [The exact Whisky Shop auction record](https://www.whiskyshop.com/auctions/a25883-clynelish-10-year-old-single-malt-of-scotland)
gives 22 October 2020, 57.5%, and 238 bottles. These are two reviews of one
release, not evidence for two bottles. Its 2010 cask 700051 neighbour is a
separate US release at 57.4%, with 228 bottles.

[The Whisky Exchange's Art of Whisky page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/38029/clynelish-21-year-old-art-of-whisky-distilling)
names The Whisky Exchange as bottler and connects the twenty-one-year-old to
the 2017 show. Reviews' shorthand “Elixir Spirit” is not a separate business.
[Thompson Bros' producer explanation](https://www.thompsonbrosdistillers.com/our-group/)
confirms that its independent bottlings have neither chill filtration nor
added colour. Its archive link goes to Whiskybase, which was not scraped.
[WhiskyNotes' 2026 review](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2026/clynelish/clynelish-2001-2013-2014-thompson-brothers/)
separates the 2013 Hogmanay bottling from the 2014 refill-hogshead release and
the Dornoch Castle Hungarian-oak anniversary selection.

[Scotch Drinkers Only](https://scotchdrinkersonly.com/products/woodrows-of-edinburgh-10-yo-clynelish-refill-hogshead-307820-54-0-alc-vol-700ml) confirms the ten-year age, 54% cask strength, natural colour and non-chill filtration. [Single Cask Nation’s archive](https://singlecasknation.com/pages/archive) identifies both Clynelish releases in its fifth US retail release: the twenty-three-year-old cask 8660 and nine-year-old cask 700038. [Diving for Pearls](https://www.divingforpearlsblog.com/2020/12/clynelish-23-year-old-1995-single-cask.html) gives the former’s September 1995–March 2019 dates, second-fill Oloroso butt, 580-bottle outturn and absence of colouring or chill filtration. [Spirit Radar’s exact cask entry](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/clynelish-2010-jwc-single-cask-nation-1nd-fill-bourbon-barrel-700038-58.1-percent-750ml/) supplies the latter’s 2010–2019 dates, first-fill bourbon barrel and 221-bottle outturn. ## Further producer and historical checks

[Decadent Drinks' Edition 38](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/clynelish-1995-whisky-sponge-edition-no38), [Edition 39](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/clynelish-1995-whisky-sponge-edition-no39), and [Edition 40](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/clynelish-1995-whisky-sponge-edition-no40) distinguish three 1995 refill-sherry casks. The [Third Secret Edition](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/clynelish-1995-whisky-sponge-third-secret-edition) combines equal portions of them and was reduced to 53%; it is neither a single cask nor a cask-strength release. Some archived gallery links return a missing-source-image error. [Edition 3](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/clynelish-2008-whisky-sponge-edition-no3) confirms the 2008 eleven-year-old bourbon-barrel identity. Auction sources give 254 bottles while contemporary reviewers give 250, so its outturn remains unknown.

[Acla's Secret Highland cask 194553 page](https://shop.acla-da-fans.ch/whisky/secret-highland-distillery-classic-serie-sherry-hogshead-194553) confirms the 2008/2023 fourteen-year-old at 53.4%, but explicitly says the distillery is unknown. A reviewer's placement under Clynelish does not resolve that attribution. By contrast, [Whisky AGE's Maltbarn Granville listing](https://whisky-age.com/en/product/maltbarn-granville-single-highland-malt-clynelish-22yo-sherry/) explicitly identifies Clynelish, the 1996/2019 years, twenty-two-year age, sherry maturation, 51.9%, and 177 bottles. The [full Whiskyfun review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2019/A-few-undisclosed-malts-session-two.html) also says the bottler personally confirmed the distillery; the introductory speculation alone misses that later confirmation.

[Milroy's own thirty-year-old product page](https://milroysofsoho.com/de/products/clynelish-30-year-old-1993-milroys-vintage-reserve-black-45-3-70cl-10461818) and label establish the 1993 Vintage Reserve Black release at 45.3%. The producer lists Vintage Reserve Black separately from Vintage Reserve. A [Korean review](https://labas24.tistory.com/2183) gives casks 11929 and 11322, whereas [The Central Whisky listing](https://thecentralwhisky.com/products/clynelish-30-year-old-1993-2023-bourbon-barrels-12929-11322-vintage-highland-reserve-by-milroys-single-malt-scotch-whisky) is indexed with 12929 and 11322 and a 2023 bottling date. The latter redirected away when opened. Cask numbers and bottling year remain unresolved pending a readable back label. The review's single-cask claim also conflicts with its two-cask heading.

[The contemporary Walloon review](https://singlemaltwallonie.blogspot.com/2014/10/quadriptyque-clynelish-volet-2-21-ans.html) documents Cadenhead's 1992/2014 twenty-one-year-old Christmas Bottling for The Nectar, Belgium, at 50.4%, with 222 bottles. [WhiskyNotes](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2014/clynelish/clynelish-1992-cadenhead-small-batch/) expressly distinguishes it from the general 51.6% Small Batch. Do not transfer the latter's outturn to the Christmas selection.

[Master of Malt's Signatory cask 12793 page](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/clynelish/clynelish-16-year-old-1995-cask-12793-ibisco-decanter-signatory-whisky/) confirms the 1995 sixteen-year-old at 43%, with 802 decanters. [Fine Spirits Auction's exact lot](https://www.finespirits.auction/uk/acheter-spi/B2264990-546-1-Bottle-Clynelish-1995-Signatory-Vintage-Cask-n12793-One-of-802-bottled-2012-The-Decanter-Collection-Brown.jsp) adds the 2012 bottling year and The Decanter Collection name. Two historical leads remain insufficiently settled. [The Whisky Store's March 1999 catalog](https://www.whisky.de/fileadmin/web_data/katalog/Alte_Preislisten/preis0399.pdf) indexes Connoisseurs Choice 1984 at 40%, but the PDF screenshot failed and direct retrieval returned 403; later sources disagree on whether fourteen years is a stated age. [The Clydesdale 1992 entry](https://maltmaniacs.com/bottle.jsp?bid=3507&bottling=Clynelish+14yo+1992%2F2006) is indexed at fourteen years, 60.4%, cask 0264/7164 and 290 bottles, but the full page could not be retrieved and the correct consumer-label Entity remains unsettled. Neither inaccessible page was bypassed. Producer and retailer photographs in these checks have no established reuse grant; they are evidence, not upload permission.

[Diageo's 2019 announcement](https://www.diageo.com/en/news-and-media/press-releases/2019/diageo-brands-earn-top-honors-for-exceptional-quality-at-19th-annual-san-francisco-world-spirits) uses the full name Game of Thrones Single Malt Scotch Whisky Collection and names releases across its distilleries. It is an owner collection Brand, with House Tyrell as the Clynelish release descriptor. The Manager's Dram follows the same owner-collection rule; it is distinct from The Managers' Choice. Correcting these relationships does not imply that generic Distillers Edition vintage references identify a unique annual bottling.
