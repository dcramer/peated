# Aberlour catalog sources, September 29, 2026

This scope covers marketed releases under the Aberlour brand: the current range,
older standard bottlings, vintage editions, numbered batches, travel retail,
commemorative releases, and official retailer selections. It does not cover
independent bottlers' Aberlour releases or Chivas Brothers' multi-distillery
Distillery Reserve Collection. Visitor hand-fills and private casks without
proof of a marketed release are excluded or unresolved.

The evidence spans 1960s vintages through September 2026 releases. No source
proves a complete historical inventory. In particular, old export labels,
White Oak vintages, and single-cask selections remain unevenly documented.

## Producer sources

- [The current range](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/) identifies the
  producer's A'bunadh Collection, A'bunadh Sherry Cask Collection, Distillery &
  Batch Releases Collection, Double Cask Matured Collection, and Travel
  Exclusives Collection. These headings describe Aberlour's own ranges.
- [Product information](https://www.aberlour.com/en/product-information/)
  links to Pernod Ricard e-labels. It covers core expressions, travel retail,
  French-market products, and some Distillery Reserve releases. The latter
  belong to the owner's multi-distillery brand. The e-label pages need a
  JavaScript browser; plain HTTP text can omit the actual strength and age.
- The individual [21-year-old Single Batch](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/21-year-old-single-batch/)
  and [25-year-old Single Batch](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/25-year-old-single-batch/)
  pages describe the September 2026 additions and their maturation. Do not
  treat a current page's publication date as the bottling date.
- The [50-year-old](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/aberlour-50-year-old/)
  page establishes its age and twenty-bottle outturn, but not a reliable
  strength. Search results elsewhere conflict; leave the strength unknown.
- The current range also describes the one-off 1967, 53-year-old _The Mouth of
  the Chattering Burn_. Its charity-auction release is distinct from a
  visitor's personal fill.
- [A'bunadh](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/abunadh-scotch-whisky/)
  establishes the no-age-statement, cask-strength, unchillfiltered Oloroso
  style. It does not give one strength applicable to every batch.
- [Alba](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/abunadh-alba-scotch-whisky/)
  establishes the American-oak branch of A'bunadh. Its numbered batches need
  their own strength evidence.
- [A'bunadh Limited Edition](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/abunadh-limited-edition/)
  and the [dated launch account](https://greatdrams.com/aberlour-unveils-a-new-limited-edition-abunadh-sherry-cask-collection/)
  identify Limited Edition 1 and its Oloroso/Pedro Ximénez finish. This is
  separate from the ordinary numbered A'bunadh batches.
- The [2007 vintage](https://www.aberlour.com/en/our-whisky/aberlour-18-year-old-2007-vintage/)
  page and [2004 vintage draw terms](https://www.aberlour.com/en/aberlour-20-year-old-2004-vintage-draw-terms-and-conditions/)
  establish those releases. Their ballot allocations are not proven total
  outturns. Do not turn the allocations into bottle counts.
- French pages are useful for [Forest Reserve](https://www.aberlour.com/fr-fr/le-whisky/aberlour-10-ans-forest-reserve/),
  [Non-Chill Filtered](https://www.aberlour.com/fr-fr/le-whisky/aberlour-12-ans-non-chill-filtered/),
  and [Triple Cask](https://www.aberlour.com/fr-fr/le-whisky/aberlour-triple-cask-2/).
  English-range navigation alone misses these products.

## Batches and historical archives

The [Aberlour collector's batch table](https://aberlour-collection.jimdoweb.com/a-bunadh-all-batches/)
provides the most useful historical A'bunadh sequence, especially batches 6–70.
Use its strengths with exact-batch corroboration where available. Its release
months are not authoritative. The first five entries describe early unnumbered
bottlings; do not invent printed batch numbers for them. The alternate Batch 30
label is described as a prototype, not another marketed release. Batch 43's
claimed private issue needs original marketing evidence.

The collector's Alba 002 strength conflicts with retailer and bottle-review
sources. [G. Town's exact listing](https://www.g.townwineandspirits.com/malts)
identifies Batch 002 at 58.7%; do not copy 57.1% from the collector table.
Later Alba evidence is spread across specialist shops and reviews:
[005](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2022/aberlour/aberlour-abunadh-alba-batch-005/),
[006](https://kensingtonwinemarket.com/product/881733/aberlour-a-bunadh-alba),
[007](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivemarch23-2-Aberlour-Strathmill.html),
[008](https://www.crombewines.com/en/products/abunadh-alba-batch-8-60-1-70cl),
[009](https://whic.de/aberlour-a-bunadh-alba-batch-009.html), and
[010](https://www.htfw.com/brands/aberlour). Searches for Alba 011 did not
establish a release in this pass.

For Casg Annamh, the [Whisky Auctioneer series archive](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/aberlour-casg-annamh?page=0)
is useful for older batch identities. Later examples appear at
[Dom Whisky](https://sklep-domwhisky.pl/product-pol-32838-Aberlour-Casg-Annamh-48-0-7l.html),
[Still Spirit](https://www.stillspirit.com/products/aberlour-casg-annahm-small-batch-010),
and [Delicando](https://www.delicando.com/en/products/40310-aberlour-casg-annamh-batch-release-011-48-vol-07l-in-gift-box).
A page's generic product title can omit the batch that its body identifies.
Searches for Batch 012 did not establish a release.

The collector's [vintage archive](https://aberlour-collection.jimdoweb.com/vintage-bottled/)
covers 1960s–1990s vintages, and its [older standard range](https://aberlour-collection.jimdoweb.com/the-standard-range-until-2010/)
is an entrance for old export bottles. Many entries are photographs with short
captions. Counts qualified as approximate must not become exact outturns.
Examples of stronger individual records include:

- [Sotheby's 1965 30-year-old](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2024/fine-rare-whisky-pf2490/aberlour-30-year-old-43-0-abv-1965-1-bt-70cl):
  two bourbon hogsheads finished in one Oloroso butt, not a single-cask release.
- [1966 30-year-old](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/10686/aberlour-1966):
  another vatting; the vintage and age do not imply a single cask.

  and [1980 22-year-old](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/84-the-47th-auction/58438-aberlour-1980-22-year-old/):
  useful exact vintage/age records without relying on approximate collector counts.
- [1990 Vintage Edition](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/107039/aberlour-1990-vintage-edition)
  and [1993 Vintage Edition](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/aberlour-1993-2003-vintage-edition-1-litre-1348085):
  the word “Vintage” does not establish an age statement.

  an age-stated 12-year-old A'bunadh exception. Later sales of leftover bottles
  do not establish a second bottling.
- [Centenary decanter](https://www.lionswhisky.com/whisky/aberlour_glenlivet_cryatal_decanter_centenary.php):
  the 1879–1979 commemoration is established; the often-repeated 1959 vintage
  remains uncertain.

## Retailer selections, older strengths, and Cellar Collection

[Whiskyfun's Aberlour index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Aberlour.html) is a useful
cross-check for historical strengths, cask numbers, selected-for names, and
outturns. Follow its dated tasting pages; an index is not a complete producer
inventory. It mixes official bottlings, independent bottlers, and visitor fills.
“OB” alone does not mean a publicly marketed release.

Useful dated pages cover [The Whisky Lodge cask 96352](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivejanuary17-1-Glenfarclas-Aberlour-Ardbeg.html),
[cask 36573](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archiveaugust18-2-Glenallachie-Mortlach.html),
[The Whisky Exchange cask 4738](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivemay16-2-Ledaig-Oban-Yamazaki-Lagavulin.html),
[LMDW/Jack Tar cask 3856](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivemarch22-2-Longrow-Glenfiddich.html),
and [LMDW cask 214040](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivenovember21-2-Ben-Nevis-Aberlour.html).
These remain official Aberlour releases; the retailer selection belongs in the
edition, not the bottler relationship.

The [2012 tasting archive](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archiveoctober12-2.html)
records both White Oak 2001/2012 and the 43% 16-year-old Double Cask. The
[43% 12-year-old auction record](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/lot-920912/aberlour-12-year-old-double-cask-matured/)
and [contemporary owner review](https://torontowhiskysociety.ca/2016/11/23/review-aberlour-12-double-cask/)
corroborate the corresponding 12-year-old strength. Do not overwrite a
40% release with these older 43% facts.

The [Cellar Collection announcement](https://foodanddrink.scotsman.com/drink/the-glenlivet-aberlour-and-scapa-release-rare-aged-collections-of-whisky-heres-whats-available/)

## Unresolved identities and facts

- **100 Proof St. Drostan's Well:** full-title, retailer, collector, and
  Whiskyfun searches find 57.1% no-age-statement bottles, but do not bind the
  existing age-stated record to the same label. A readable label is needed.
- **10-year-old Pure Single Highland Malt:** auction and standard-range searches
  show both 40% and 43% versions. The existing Highland Single Malt photograph
  visibly confirms 40%, but does not identify the separate Pure Single Highland
  Malt record or prove a duplicate.
- **12-year-old Sherry Cask:** both 40% and 43% litre versions appear in
  specialist sources. [A 2013 review](https://maltrunners.com/2013/04/04/aberlour-12-year-sherry-cask/)
  establishes the latter, but cannot identify a record with no strength or image.
- **13-year-old Single Cask:** multiple casks, including 34595 and 79212,
  exist. A generic record needs a cask number, strength, or selected-for name.
- **14-year-old A Dream of Scotland:** searches point to Brühler Whiskyhaus
  and a 54.3% release. Exact label identity and a separately approved Brand
  correction are needed before changing the existing record.
- **19-year-old Single Cask Sherry Butt:** cask 5938 is a candidate, but the
  generic stored name and import text do not establish it.
- **8-year-old Campbell's cube bottle:** the long packaging description fits
  more than one 43%/50% release. Collector and Whiskyfun searches do not settle
  its identity. Shortening it to an invented expression would hide the problem.
- **Millennium Contract 079/345:** secondary auction sale does not prove the
  original private-cask programme marketed labeled bottles to the public.
- **Early unnumbered A'bunadh / Batch 43:** the collector's ordering is not
  proof of printed numbering or public sale. Original labels and programme
  evidence are still needed.
- **1970 31-year-old:** the age, vintage, strength, and outturn agree, but
  sources disagree on 2001 versus 2002 bottling. Leave the bottling year unknown.
- **1975 casks 4556, 4577, and 4581:** the first has conflicting strength and
  outturn claims (49.2%/168 versus 50.2%/128); the second has conflicting
  bottling years; the third lacks sufficient exact-label corroboration.
  The [2005](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archiveseptember05-2.html) and
  [2006](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archiveoctober06-1.html) tasting archives are
  useful starting points, not a reason to resolve conflicts by arithmetic.
- **2002 cask 2575:** 62.4% in Whiskyfun conflicts with 62.6% in auction
  material. Inspect the exact label before choosing a strength.
- **Older export releases:** 8-year-old vintage cubes, “over 9 years” 70-proof
  bottles, 15-year-old Sherry Finish, Special Reserve decanters, and Dunnage
  Matured bottlings need further label-level matching. Packaging alone must
  not create a separate release.
- **White Oak:** individual vintages are documented unevenly. Do not generate
  missing years as a continuous sequence or convert a year difference into a
  stated age. Retailer databases sometimes fill unknown dates with January 1.

## Exclusions, images, and access limits

The collector's [Warehouse No. 1 history](https://aberlour-collection.jimdoweb.com/warehouse-no-1/)
documents visitor-filled casks from 2003 through May 2014, followed by Victorian
Room fills. The existing Warehouse No. 1 Sherry Butt, Warehouse No. 1 Bourbon
Cask, 1996 Warehouse No. 1, and 1997 Warehouse No. 1 records are outside the
marketed-release scope. Their dates do not justify calculated age statements.
A later auction appearance does not turn a visitor fill into a marketed release.

Independent bottler releases stay under their own Brands. A distiller search
therefore returns a wider set than this official-brand inventory. The same
applies to Chivas Brothers' Distillery Reserve Collection, even when Aberlour's
product-information page links to its e-labels.

No new Bottle image was copied in this pass. [Aberlour's terms](https://www.aberlour.com/en/terms-of-use/)
limit site material to personal use. The collector's images require permission;
retailer, auction, Whiskyfun, and community-review photographs do not carry an
established reusable license. Exact images on those pages help identify bottles
but do not supply permission to store them. Existing Bottle images were retained;
their exact-release identity and missing provenance remain a separate limitation.
The existing distillery photograph has Wikimedia Commons attribution and a
CC BY-SA 3.0 license; preserve that attribution.

Some archive image links did not open through the text fetcher. Pernod Ricard
e-labels required browser rendering. Search-index text helped locate auction
and retailer records, but cached snippets can differ from a live page. No
paywall or access control was bypassed.
