# Gordon & MacPhail named ranges

Research date: 22 October 2026. Scope: Gordon & MacPhail named ranges and
finite release sets, prompted by the 2026 Dram Takers Collection Volume 2
presenter. The [September guide](./2026-09-20-gordon-macphail.md) covers the
producer archive, label reading, and distillery-by-distillery evidence. This
guide does not claim every historical G&M bottling has been found.

## What the catalog was missing

Whole release sets were absent; some range names were stored only in
`edition`; Heritage and Heritage Collection were mixed; and Recollection had
two Series records (since merged). Expect the same kinds of gaps in other
bottler catalogs.

## Recollection

- The [2022 announcement](https://whiskyexperts.net/gordon-macphail-veroeffentlicht-recollection-series-seltene-whiskys-aus-geschlossenen-schottischen-destillerien/)
  covers the first series and its regional selections.
- The [2023 announcement](https://whiskyexperts.net/gordon-macphail-veroeffentlicht-zweite-recollection-serie-mit-18-whiskys-aus-lost-distilleries/)
  lists the 18 second-series whiskies.
- The [current Recollection page](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/the-recollection)
  shows the third series. Some of its section headings give years that
  conflict with the linked product pages (Convalmore, Glenlochy). Use the
  product page.
- Older Recollection product pages use `/our-whiskies/pc-<distillery>-<year>-<cask>`.
- Retailer pages that print the series number: The Whisky Vault (Glenlochy
  1979, Lochside 1981, Rosebank 1991), The Whisky Exchange (Glenesk 1984,
  Inverleven 1985), Grape & Grain and Liquor Kingdom (Imperial), Whisky-Online
  (Pittyvaich 1993, Convalmore 1975).

## Open ranges

These ranges are not finite. The pages show that a range exists and what its
labels look like, not every release in it.

- Range list and vault:
  [current ranges](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/ranges),
  [vault](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/our-vault) (Rare Old,
  Private Collection, Cask Strength, Rare Vintage),
  [Speymalt](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/ranges/speymalt).
- Connoisseurs Choice, Distillery Labels, Discovery: the range list. Modern
  single-cask Connoisseurs Choice releases with cask numbers and bottling years
  are easiest to confirm on The Whisky World, Fine Drams, Master of Malt, and
  Tyndrum Whisky product pages. The
  [G&M retail Connoisseurs Choice page](https://retail-gordonandmacphail.com/collections/connoisseurs-choice)
  lists current releases.
- Cask and Reserve: the
  [Het Whiskyforum guide](https://www.hetwhiskyforum.nl/_misc/GuideV44e_doc.pdf).
- Spirit of Scotland:
  [The Whisky Barrel mailing](https://milled.com/thewhiskybarrel-1/just-landed-gordon-and-macphail-single-casks-new-speymalts-w_8Dqj-IEfntxT4O).
- Centenary Reserve:
  [Bolaffi auction catalog 632](https://www.astebolaffi.it/pdf/auctions/632.pdf).
- The MacPhail's Collection (named distilleries): exact labels and the
  [2017 Vin og Vin distributor catalog](https://vinogvin.dk/images/design/upload/Minimesse%20generisk%20katalog_2017.pdf).
  This is not MacPhail's undisclosed-distillery whisky. An old producer URL
  containing `the-macphails-collection` now shows the undisclosed-distillery
  product, so the path alone does not prove the range.

## Conflicts and traps

- The Volume 2 presenter's cover and pages 5–10 say Volume 2; page 2 says
  Volume 1. Its "100 sets" is a set count, not each bottle's outturn. The same
  applies to the Vintage Moments set count.
- Book of Kells artwork on old labels (Strathisla 1977 casks 387 and 389,
  Clynelish 1972 cask 14301) does not make them part of the modern Dram Takers
  range. See
  [The Whisky Vault's Dram Takers article](https://www.thewhiskyvault.com/blogs/gordon--macphails-dram-takers-collection-a-legendary-label-returns).
- Imported reference text sometimes conflicts with producer evidence. Producer
  product pages and labels win.

## Image terms

Exact producer images exist, but the
[producer's terms](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/terms-conditions),
section 3.4, require written permission for online or software reuse. No
permission was found in this pass. G&M's public brand asset library holds
recent product images; see the
[Brora modern release images guide](./2026-10-22-brora-modern-images.md).
[Catalog Maintenance](../../operations/catalog-maintenance.md#review-images)
owns the rule for producer images.

## Unresolved

- **Port Ellen 1981, cask 290:** appears in both the 2023 second-series
  announcement and the third-series material. The current
  [product page](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/port-ellen-1981?hsLang=en-gb)
  gives 60 bottles; other records give 181. Is this one release or two?
- **Glenlivet 1943 at 40%:** a retailer
  ([The Whisky World](https://www.thewhiskyworld.com/glenlivet-1943-private-collection-40-p6848))
  calls it Private Collection, but the producer's
  [Private Collection Glenlivet 1943](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/private-collection-glenlivet-1943)
  is 70 years at 49.1% (see also
  [Sotheby's](https://www.sothebys.com/buy/14cb6d0d-6ecb-483d-83ad-41249f0a8324/lots/2a80c366-0a56-406b-baaa-5397d32b87bc)).
  Which range does the 40% label print?
- **North Port 1981 and Longmorn 1966:** some Private Collection records lack
  the cask and bottling year, so it is unclear whether they match the
  Recollection #3 North Port (42 years, 50.9%) and the Private Collection
  Longmorn cask 610. The Longmorn 1966 Anniversary Release is a separate
  product. Sources:
  [North Port at The Whisky World](https://www.thewhiskyworld.com/north-port-brechin-1981-private-collection-gordon-and-macphail-p16471),
  [Longmorn at The Whisky World](https://www.thewhiskyworld.com/longmorn-1966-private-collection-gordon-macphail-p13159).
- **Strathisla 1977 and Clynelish 1972 Book of Kells labels:** which range, if
  any, did G&M market them under? Sources:
  [Scotch Whisky Auctions, cask 389 for Japan Import System](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/227-the-178th-auction/871900-strathisla-1977-40-year-old-gordon--macphail-for-japan-import-system/),
  [The Whisky Exchange, cask 387](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/39299/strathisla-1977-40-year-old-twe-exclusive-gm).
- **MacPhail's Collection Glenturret 15 years, 46%:** named only by import
  references. Searches of the producer vault, retailers, auctions, and
  [Whisky Antique](https://www.whiskyantique.com/en/brands/glenturret) found
  other ages and strengths only.
- **Balblair 10 years, 43%:** sold as Distillery Labels
  ([Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/balblair/balblair-10-year-old-gordon-and-macphail-whisky/)),
  but a MacPhail's Collection version also exists
  ([Whiskyauction.com](https://whiskyauction.com/item/3461167)). Where is the
  boundary?
- **Glenrothes-Glenlivet 8 years, 40%:** an older white-label release
  ([Just Whisky](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/glenrothes-glenlivet-8-years-old-gm-1365932))
  and a later MacPhail's Collection release
  ([The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/13744/glenrothes-8-year-old-macphails-collection))
  share age and strength. Which records are which?
- **Connoisseurs Choice 1993 Bladnoch and Clynelish:** names with no age,
  strength, or bottler. No source identifies the exact release.
