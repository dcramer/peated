# Springbank, Longrow, and Hazelburn — September 6, 2026

The production review started with 268 Brand-attached Bottles: 197 Springbank,
46 Longrow, and 25 Hazelburn. It finished with 320: 197 Springbank, 82 Longrow,
and 41 Hazelburn. The operation created 52 exact release records, corrected 17
existing records, created four Series, and attached three licensed Bottle
images. It did not merge or delete records.

- The producer's [Springbank range](https://www.springbank.scot/whisky/springbank-range/),
  [Longrow range](https://www.springbank.scot/whisky/longrow/), and
  [Hazelburn range](https://www.springbank.scot/whisky/hazelburn/) established
  the current core releases, 2026 annual releases, maturation, strength, and
  outturn where published. The separate
  [Hazelburn Sherry Wood 2025 page](https://www.springbank.scot/whisky/hazelburn/sherry-wood-8yo-2025/)
  established that edition. The
  [Countdown introduction](https://www.springbank.scot/countdown-collection/countdown-collection-an-introduction/)
  established five annual 500-bottle releases; the four releases available by
  the review date were updated with that outturn.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Springbank list](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/distilleries/springbank),
  [Longrow list](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/brands/longrow),
  and [Hazelburn list](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/brands/hazelburn)
  were historical gap finders. Its
  [Longrow Red guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/longrow-red),
  [Longrow Open Day guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/longrow-springbank-open-day-bottlings),
  and [Hazelburn Open Day guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/hazelburn-springbank-open-day-bottlings)
  supplied bounded release families. Exact auction lots, readable labels, and
  specialist timelines were used when those guides omitted a year, strength,
  vintage, or outturn.
- Production Series `S0656`–`S0659` now hold Longrow Open Day (12 releases),
  Longrow Red (12), Hazelburn Sherry Wood (8), and Hazelburn Open Day (9).
  Newly created Bottles are `B53881`–`B53932`. The known annual Longrow 18 and
  21 releases, 2025–2026 100° Proof releases, Hazelburn's three 2005 first-edition
  labels, and the 2022–2023 Hazelburn 21 releases were also added.
- Existing Hazelburn Sherry Wood and Open Day records had wrong or missing ages,
  years, strengths, outturns, Series, or Springbank distillery relationships.
  These were corrected only where an exact label or page supported the value.
  The generic Hazelburn 2004 Oloroso record remains unchanged because it could
  mean either the 13-year 2018 edition or the 14-year 2019 edition.
- The Longrow Red Tawny Port record keeps bottling year 2022 but no release year
  because sources disagree about whether to call it a 2022 or 2023 release. The
  current Longrow 100° Proof page also gives conflicting red-wine cask details,
  so no maturation value was stored for the 2026 edition.
- The [Wikimedia Springbank bottle category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Springbank_bottles)
  contained four exact Bottle photos with verified reuse terms. Three were a
  safe identity match and were stored with their source page and `CC BY-SA 3.0`
  license: Springbank 15 (`B0872`), Longrow 10-year-old 100 Proof (`B1104`),
  and Hazelburn 8 First Edition – The Stills (`B53930`). Producer, retailer, and
  auction photos without stated reuse permission were not copied.
- No public source proved a complete list of every Society, duty-paid sample,
  cage, hand-fill, retailer, country, or private-cask release. Those families
  remain limited to exact records already in Peated or supported by a specific
  source. Generic Longrow Red, 18, and 21 records are possible merge candidates,
  but no merge was made without separate approval.
