# Talisker catalog sources — September 20, 2026

This review covers the prepared Talisker inventory: official releases,
Diageo collections, and independent bottlings that identify Talisker. It
includes historical export labels and releases marketed through 2026. The
September 18 work list supplied leads; the sources below were checked again
for this review. No source establishes a complete history of private casks
or independent bottlings.

## Official releases and Diageo collections

The [43-year-old Xpedition Oak producer page](https://www.diageorareandexceptional.com/us/rare-spirits/rare-whisky/talisker-xpedition-oak-43-year-old-single-malt-scotch-whisky)
establishes the named series and 49.7% strength. The
[Forests of the Deep auction lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/212-auction/792999-talisker-44-year-old-forests-of-the-deep-for-parley/)
records 44 years, 49.1%, and a numbered bottle from an outturn of 1,997.
Whiskyfun's index says 48.8%; that disagreement should not be propagated as
a second release without evidence of a different bottle.

The [Magma producer page](https://www.diageorareandexceptional.com/us/rare-spirits/rare-whisky/talisker-47-year-old)
gives 47 years, 48.8%, 622 bottles, and maturation in refill American oak
followed by new American oak toasted using volcanic rocks. The launch
article on BevNET encountered a security check during this review; the
producer page supports the product facts independently.

[Justerini & Brooks' own product page](https://www.justerinis.com/rare-whisky/scotland/whisky/talisker/talisker-21-year-old-justerini-and-brooks-exclusive/2004)
supports its exclusive 21-year-old at 58.7%, 252 bottles, and the sequence
of refill American oak, Port casks, then a puncheon. The merchant's
selection does not by itself make the merchant an independent bottler.

Use the current identity model for collection relationships. Special
Releases, Prima & Ultima, Rare Series, The Managers' Choice, and Casks of
Distinction are collection Brands. The Talisker distillery relationship
preserves their place in the Talisker catalog. Older preparation notes
proposed conflicting Brand rules and must not be used as policy.

The 2002 20-year-old has distinct 62% and US 59.7% versions. The Special
Releases guide documents both. An existing record without strength or an
exact source cannot safely be assigned to either one.

Numbered 25- and 30-year-old labels need individual checking. Their stated
year and outturn can identify a marketed annual bottling; a label slogan
alone cannot. Open outturn conflicts include the 2008 25-year-old
(6,708 versus 9,708) and 2010 30-year-old (3,000 versus 2,958). Preserve
existing values until stronger evidence establishes the correction.

The annual reduced-strength releases also appear in surviving retailer and
auction pages. [The Whisky Exchange's 2011 25-year-old](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/17387/talisker-25-year-old-bot2011)
records 5,946 bottles. [Whisky Magazine](https://whiskymag.com/tastings/talisker-25-years-old/)
confirms the 2012 25-year-old at 45.8%, but supplies no outturn.
[A 2013 auction lot](https://www.australianwhiskyauctions.com.au/lot-572178/talisker-25-year-old-2013-bottling)
records 5,772; [Whisky-Online's 2014 auction history](https://live.whisky-onlineauctions.com/pages/product-history-page?mproduct=talisker-25-year-old-2014-release&mproductId=6919404978340)
records 6,000. Exact Whisky.Auction lots establish
[2015 with 9,882](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/103033/talisker-25-year-old)
and [2018 with 14,058](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/82864/talisker-25-year-old).
[Scotch Whisky Auctions' 2017 lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/226-the-177th-auction/866780-talisker-25-year-old-2017-release-/)
records 21,498. These are numbered annual bottlings, not merely different
cartons for an undated expression.

For the 30-year-old, The Whisky Exchange documents
[2012 with 3,186 bottles](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/29166/talisker-30-year-old-bot2012)
and [2013 with 3,738](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/33763/talisker-30-year-old-bot2013).
[Just Whisky's 2014 lot](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/talisker-30-years-old-2014-limited-edition-1368677)
records 1,278. Luxauc supplies exact numbered lots for
[2015 with 3,912](https://luxauc.com/en/past_auction/130/detail/10579)
and [2017 with 9,078](https://luxauc.com/en/past_auction/130/detail/10578).
[Passie voor Whisky's 2022 page](https://www.passievoorwhisky.nl/nl/whisky/schotse-whisky/single-malt-whisky/islands/talisker-whisky-distillery/37264-talisker-30-years-bottled-2022.html)
confirms 49.6% and 3,195 bottles. Do not assign that edition to a record at
48.5% merely because its existing year says 2022.

## Distillers Edition

Start with the producer's [2022 product page](https://www.malts.com/en-gb/products/talisker-2022-distillers-edition-single-malt-scotch-whisky-70cl),
then use exact older labels to distinguish vintage from bottling year.
The year in a modern producer title does not authorize replacing every
older vintage-based title with an inferred release year.

The 1986 edition's bottling/release year remains disputed between sources.
The auction description confirms the vintage and 45.8%, but does not settle
that date. A general statement that Diageo launched Distillers Edition in
1997 does not prove when this particular bottle was released.

The 1991 and 1993 vintages each have more than one proposed bottling year.
Their generic legacy records lack enough facts to select one. In particular,
the auction page cited for a proposed 2006 assignment describes a 1993
vintage bottled in 2007. Keep those assignments unresolved until an exact
stored image, source reference, or other identifying evidence settles them.
An undated modern product photo also does not prove that an older record
whose name includes 2000 should become the ongoing modern expression.

## Independent bottlings: label checks that matter

- **Provenance DL11651, Whiskyschiff Luzern:** Whisky-Online describes a
  2008 vintage, six years old, bottled in 2017. Those years do not agree with
  the age. Its label clearly supports six years, the 2017 event, 51%, and
  126 bottles, but does not show a vintage. Do not infer 2008 or calculate
  a replacement vintage from the age.
- **Provenance DL10973 and DL11371:** the
  [cited 2008 seven-year-old auction lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/111-auction/151746-talisker-2008-7-year-old-provenance/)
  and its photo show DL11371, bottled August 2016, with 411 bottles. That
  photo cannot support DL10973. Whiskyfun separately lists DL10973 as a
  November 2015 bottling; use its own review and label for that cask.
- **Cadenhead's 1979 fifteen-year-old:** the
  [auction text](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/159-auction/451561-talisker-1979-15-year-old-cadenheads/)
  says July 1998, while the carton and bottle say July 1994. The label
  supports 15 years and 63.2%. The claimed 432-bottle outturn is not visible
  on that label and needs corroboration.
- **Fine Vintage cask 982:** [Bonhams](https://www.bonhams.com/auction/27720/lot/257/talisker-1989-27-year-old-982/)
  identifies a 1989 vintage, 27 years, 2016 bottling, 51.6%, and 250 bottles.
  Its description names Fine Vintage Cask Selection as bottler, while the
  front label uses Talisker trade dress without naming a separate business.
  Resolve the responsible bottler from stronger evidence before treating
  the label design as proof of an official release. Do not confuse it with
  cask 912, which has a different outturn.

## Access and images

Scotch Whisky Auctions worked in a browser after its age confirmation.
Read its pages sequentially. Whisky Auctioneer failed on initial browser
navigation, but some exact lots and their label photographs were available
later through indexed pages. Availability varies by URL. Do not work
around access controls or treat a search snippet as a label inspection.
Whiskybase and Distiller were not fetched, following the repository's source
access policy.

An exact label photo can prove facts without being available for reuse.
Do not publish auction watermarks or assume that producer packshots are
licensed. Diageo links its [conditions of use](https://legal.diageo.com/)
from its product pages. Reuse requires checking the terms for the actual
source and image. Three Commons file pages provide reusable alternatives:

  Keeezawa, CC BY-SA 4.0; current 10-year-old bottle and carton.

## Additional sources and resolved conflicts

[Whisky-news' 2026 tasting page](https://whisky-news.com/En/Tasting041.html)
resolves Berry Bros & Rudd's 1972 Isle of Skye cask 1102: its label explicitly
names Talisker. The review gives 1999 and 42.5%. An “Isle of Skye” title alone
would not establish the distiller; older Berry Bros labels must be checked
individually. Neither the vintage nor the bottling year supplies a stated age.

Historical G&M licensed bottlings need both strength units and label wording.
Imperial 100 proof and a later printed 57% are not interchangeable catalog
values. A “Cask Strength” statement does not by itself establish the named
Cask series. An importer credit, such as Intertrade, does not prove who
bottled the whisky. Similarly, the Wolverhampton & Dudley label says bottled
**for** the brewery, which does not establish it as bottler.

The [Cadenhead's 1979 eighteen-year-old lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/128-auction/244906-talisker-1979-18-year-old-cadenheads/)
has 62.2% on its label; the Whiskyfun listing's 63.2% should not override it.
The [1979 twenty-one-year-old Bond Reserve lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/102-the-64th-auction/106599-cadenheads-talisker-1979-21-year-old/)
identifies a named range. Miniature size alone does not prove membership in
a “Miniatures” series. [The Whisky Exchange's One Shot page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/6879/talisker-1992-one-shot)
provides another named range, for Jean Boyer's 1992/2006 Talisker.

Hart Brothers has two documented 1993 fifteen-year-old strengths:
[46% in an owner’s bottle review](https://whiskeryturnip.com/2024/08/18/2024-8-17-talisker-15-year-1993-hart-brothers/)
and [51.7% in Whiskyfun's March 2010 archive](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivemarch10-1.html).
Matching vintage, age, and bottling year do not make these one release.

Hedonism provides useful current product evidence, but two pages disagree
with themselves. Its [exclusive sixteen-year-old](https://hedonism.co.uk/products/talisker-16-year-old-cask-strength-exclusive-to-hedonism-wines)
is 59.2%, but mentions both 198 and 200 bottles; leave outturn unknown.
Its [Cadenhead's 1957 twenty-two-year-old](https://hedonism.co.uk/products/talisker-22-year-old-cadenheads-1957)
has 40% in the product information and 46% in the description; leave strength
unknown until an exact legible label settles it.

G&M's 1987 thirty-nine-year-old Connoisseurs Choice Heritage has an
unsettled outturn. The [July 2026 launch coverage](https://thewhiskywire.com/2026/07/09/gordon-macphail-launches-its-last-ever-release-to-feature-the-talisker-name/)
reports 451 bottles, while the existing catalog value is 300. The reviewed
material did not justify changing that stored value. An exact numbered
label or producer confirmation is still needed.

For early SMWS bottlings, supplement the distillery index with exact merchant
pages and dated owner reviews. [The Whiskyphiles' 14.3 review](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2015/03/10/14-3-talisker-17-years-old-1979-64-3-smws/)
provides the 1979 seventeen-year-old at 64.3%. SMWS release numbers belong
in their names; do not invent a tasting-note subtitle for a bottle whose
original title has not been established.

Private casks often survive on regional retailer pages rather than Diageo's
current site. [Kuva Whisky's DING’s selection](https://kuvawhisky.evershop.com.tw/product/600070727/)
shows the Talisker twenty-nine-year-old label and specifies 1990, cask 171,
50.6%, American oak, and 240 bottles. Its generic paragraph incorrectly says
Lagavulin; the exact title, product photograph, and product attributes identify
Talisker. Its page does not independently settle the bottling year.
[GourmetPool's own selection page](https://gourmetpool.de/talisker-30/)
is a direct source for the 1993 thirty-year-old, cask ZL01, at 60.4%.

## Remaining coverage questions

- **Unidentified legacy releases:** an undated official twenty-year-old
  cannot be assigned among the 2002 and 2003 Special Releases without strength
  or other identifying evidence. The 1981/2002 entry also lacks the facts
  needed to choose between the UK and US strengths. Generic twenty-five- and
  thirty-year-old slogan records may refer to numbered annual releases;
  their names alone do not justify merging them into ongoing expressions.
- **Distillers Edition:** resolve the 1991 and 1993 bottling-year alternatives
  and the record named 2000 before mapping the modern undated expression.
  Auction headings such as “2023 Release” or “2025” do not prove a separately
  marketed edition when the label is undated.
- **Old export bottles:** the claimed no-age 43% Distillers Agency Pure Malt
  still needs an exact legible label. The whisky indices and auction-title
  searches can instead surface the eight-year-old. Wolverhampton & Dudley's
  Rare Old Liqueur still lacks a verified bottler and dating evidence.
- **Cadenhead's and Hunter Laing legacy entries:** the bare “1979 Authentic
  Collection” and “2011 Old Malt Cask” names do not select a unique release.
  The proposed ages do not resolve the missing strength or cask number.
- **Intertrade 1970 sixteen-year-old:**
  [Pandolfini's exact lot](https://www.pandolfini.it/uk/auction-1227/talisker-1970-12023003434)
  helps with the bottle identity, but an Intertrade importer credit does not
  settle the bottler relationship. Do not import the prepared G&M attribution
  without stronger evidence.
- **SMWS 14.1:** specialist indices and searches for the 1976, 64.5% release
  did not establish a reliable exact label and bottling date. Conflicting
  eight-year age and later dates must not be reconciled by arithmetic.
- **The Speakeasy:** [My Annoying Opinions' own-bottle review](https://myannoyingopinions.com/2014/03/13/talisker-5-2008-speakeasy-douglas-laing/)
  and [Diving for Pearls](https://www.divingforpearlsblog.com/2017_02_06_archive.html)
  substantiate the 2008 five-year-old at 58.2%, selected for K&L. Attribution
  varies between Douglas Laing and The Laing Whisky Company; verify the
  responsible label and bottler before choosing stored Entity relationships.
- **Fine Vintage cask 982:** establish whether Fine Vintage was the bottler
  or a selector of an official cask. Its different outturn means it must not
  be folded into cask 912.
- **Small bottles and private releases:** the 2004 twenty-five-year-old 20cl
  at 58.2% and the Cadenhead's twenty-one-year-old 46% chess-set miniature
  still need evidence that each was a separately marketed release. Auction
  resale alone does not settle eligibility. Visitor hand-fills, staff-only
  Manager's Dram bottles, the Shanghai demonstration bottle, and whole-cask
  sales are outside this prepared retail-release backfill.

Unnamed malts attributed to Talisker by speculation, and blends containing
Talisker, are outside this single-malt scope. Secret Stills, Tactical,
Fascadale, and similar anonymous names should not acquire a Talisker
relationship from a reviewer's guess. Private casks have no complete public
archive; this review does not claim that every historical bottling is known.
