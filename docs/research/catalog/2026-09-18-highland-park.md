# Highland Park catalog sources — September 18, 2026

Where the information for Highland Park (Kirkwall, Orkney; owner Edrington,
earlier James Grant & Co and Highland Distillers) lives, found during the
September 18, 2026 catalog pass. Scope: every official Highland Park release
from the 1950s St Magnus labels to September 2026, including travel retail,
market and retailer selections bottled under the Highland Park label, the
numbered single casks, and the Viking Soul Cask firkins. Independent
bottlings were reviewed only where Peated already held a record with Highland
Park as distiller. Recheck each site before use.

Brand, Series, and bottler follow the six rules in
`docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`: Highland Park is Brand and
distiller with no bottler (rule 1); Valhalla Collection, The Warrior Series,
Viking Legend, Orcadian Vintage Series, Keystones, and Vintage Collection are
producer-named Series; retailer, importer, airport, club, and festival
selections carry the customer or program name in `edition` (rule 5). Numbered
casks are named `Single Cask` (labels before 2016) or `Single Cask Series`
(2016 onward) with `Cask No. N for <customer>` as the edition, because the
cask number and customer are the printed identity; Viking Soul Cask uses the
same shape.

## Conflicts settled

- The Dark launch: Whisky Advocate and retailers say 2017, whiskyfun 2018; the producer's December 2018 post says "last year". 2017 used, month unknown.
- 50 Year Old 2020 Release: named 2020 by the producer, launched March 2021 (just-drinks). Release date stored as March 2021.
- 54 Year Old bottling year: producer 2022 versus press 2023; left null.
- 56: producer page 46.9% and February 2025; press 47.1% and January 2025. Producer used.
- 1971 Orcadian Vintage ABV: producer 46.9%, whiskyfun label 46.7%; left null.
- 1968 Orcadian Vintage year: one producer blurb says 2008; product page, press release (November 2009), and archive say 2009.
- Saltire Edition 1 cask: producer page 2542, auction lot 6520; cask number left null.
- 21 Year Old 2024 Release cask count: page says 48 and 36 in two places; 48 used.
- DFS Changi European-oak cask: press "#284", label readings 384. Heathrow 2020 cask: press 64.4%, database 64.6%. Glasgow cask: lot title 5969, lot text 6569.
- Humble Whisky firkin: whiskyfun 300151, Spirit Radar 500151; one cask, recorded as 500151.
- 1973 cask 6194: Nuance Group (whisky-news) and 6194/A German Orkney Adventure (auction) may be one split cask sold to two customers; both recorded.
- Ambassador's Cask outturn No. 1: 270 (whiskyfun, Spirit Radar) versus 289 (Whisky Monitor); 270 used.
