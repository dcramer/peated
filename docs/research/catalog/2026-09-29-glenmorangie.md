# Glenmorangie catalog sources

Research date: 29 September 2026. Scope: Glenmorangie's own branded releases,
including travel retail, historical wood finishes, vintage releases, and publicly
marketed official selections. This is a source guide, not a claim that every
historical single cask has been identified.

## Dated launches and regional releases

[The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2018/01/glenmorangie-adds-cadboll-to-travel-retail-legends-collection/)
documents the original Cadboll, while its
[Tarlogan announcement](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2016/04/glenmorangie-to-release-tarlogan-tr-exclusive/)
anchors the Legends family. The
[2019 travel-retail announcement](https://prod-scotch-whisky.azurewebsites.net/magazine/latest-news/27482/glenmorangie-trio-heads-to-travel-retail/)
covers Accord, Elementa and Tribute, including the initial Dubai launch before
wider distribution. Later review dates should not replace that launch history.

For recent releases, consult the
[producer's Harrison Ford announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/harrison-ford-co-directs-his-own-limited-edition-glenmorangie-his-verdict-very-nice-302760347.html),
[The Thirty launch](https://www.dramface.com/news/2026/glenmorangie-30), and the
[Year of the Horse retailer listing](https://whisky.my/product/glenmorangie-23-year-old-2002-year-of-the-horse-2026/).
The retailer confirms an actual regional product beyond a proposed label filing;
its listing does not settle every release-date or outturn field.

The [Pride 1978 producer announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/glenmorangie-launches-their-most-exclusive-whisky-to-date-pride-1978-266625181.html)
and [Pride 1974 launch](https://scotchwhisky.com/magazine/latest-news/13250/glenmorangie-pride-1974-to-launch-globally/)
separate bottling dates, ages and public launches. For Grand Vintage, use dated
announcements such as [1990](https://harpers.co.uk/news/fullstory.php/aid/20169/Glenmorangie_unveils_new_vintage_collection.html),
[1989](https://scotchwhisky.com/magazine/latest-news/18425/glenmorangie-launches-grand-vintage-1989/),
[1991](https://scotchwhisky.com/magazine/latest-news/25141/glenmorangie-introduces-1991-grand-vintage/),
and [1998](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2022/glenmorangie-unveils-rare-23-year-old-limited-edition-whisky-glenmorangie-grand-vintage-malt-1998).
Do not derive stated age by subtracting calendar years.

## Historical label evidence

Auction photographs and transcriptions are useful for discontinued releases, but
lot titles often omit a cask number or invent an age from dates. Inspect the exact
label and distinguish a bottle number from a cask number.

- [1963 review](https://whiskycast.com/ratings/glenmorangie-1963/): original bottling and later rebottling history. Rebottling did not add maturation. Sources disagree on age, so the vintage alone is safe.
- [Culloden](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/2468/glenmorangie-1971-culloden) and [1971 anniversary](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/119-the-81st-auction/189365-glenmorangie-1971-150th-anniversary/): distinguish these from the ceramic Sesquicentennial Selection.
- [1974 millennium](https://www.whiskyshop.com/auctions/a81639-glenmorangie-1974-commemorative-millennium-bottling) and [plain 1974 vintage](https://www.artcurial.com/en/sales/3327/lots/1048-a): distinct from Millennium Malt 12-year-old. Conflicting inferred ages do not establish an age statement.
- [1975 Port Wood](https://live.whisky-onlineauctions.com/pages/product-history-page?mproduct=glenmorangie-1975-1994-port-wood-finish&mproductId=6921770893476), [1977 vintage](https://www.tyndrumwhisky.com/glenmorangie-1977-21-year-old.html), and [1979 vintage](https://www.whisky-online.com/products/glenmorangie-1979-1995-limited-edition): useful for exact vintage, strength and printed age where present.
- [1980 Japan release](https://waiter.jp/collection/bc6729/): age, strength and limited release evidence; bottling year conflicts with Whiskyfun and remains unsettled.
- [1982 Natural Cask Strength](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/glenmorangie-1982-natural-cask-strength-10-year-old-0) and [1987 cask 6813](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/16253/glenmorangie-1987-cask-strength): exact cask evidence. Ignore unrelated product recommendations embedded below the lot.
- [Cognac Matured 14-year-old](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/glenmorangie-cognac-matured-14-year-old?v=1535), [White Rum 18-year-old](https://www.nicks.com.au/products/glenmorangie-white-rum-wood-finish-18-year-old-single-malt-scotch-whisky-700ml), and [Premier Selection](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/2513/glenmorangie-premier-selection): discontinued official releases.
- [Christie's mixed historical lot](https://www.christies.com/lot/lot-6006785): Claret, Côte de Nuits and older finishes. A mixed lot does not make all its fields applicable to every bottle.

## Conflicts and questions to preserve

The producer's Allta page has conflicting strength text: its detailed description
and contemporary reviews support 51.2%, while a header says 46%. Bacalta, Allta,
Sonnalta PX, Tayne and Taghta should not acquire ages from maturation anecdotes.
Artisan Cask 1995 is not a 25-year-old whisky. Grand Vintage 1990 is 25-year-old,
and the 1977 vintage is 21-year-old; stronger release evidence overrides those
incorrect ages.

[The Polish selector](https://lozadzentelmenow.pl/selekcje/glenmorangie) and
[Distilia](https://distilia.com/products/glenmorangie-1991-2019-27yo-55-9-cask-201)
agree on the anniversary selection but conflict over strength, vintage text and
maturation. The shared age, vintage, bottling year and outturn can identify the
release; strength, maturation and cask number need label confirmation.

Pommard 25-year-old has conflicting 51.6% and 51.8% descriptions. Grand Vintage
1991 has conflicting age descriptions. The Lighthouse announcement's public-sale
allocation is not necessarily total outturn. Those disputed fields remain unknown.

The [Manager's Collection retailer](https://nothingbutwhisky.com/product/glenmorangie-distillery-managers-collection)
documents public sale of cask 475 after its staff-gift origin. Its remaining stock
allocation is not total outturn. Other staff-only gifts, such as Last Christmas at
Leith and 100 Best UK Companies, need evidence of a marketed release before
inclusion. Speakeasy cask 5448 is described as a visitor hand fill and falls outside
this marketed-release inventory.

SMWS releases retain their independent Brand. Anonymous Highland malts and
Westport blends cannot be assigned Glenmorangie identity merely because an index
places them on a Glenmorangie page.

## Access and images

The producer's [terms](https://www.glenmorangie.com/pages/terms-conditions) prohibit
automated scraping. Stop automated catalog retrieval once this restriction is
known; use permitted access and independently published evidence. Product and
auction pages vary in tool accessibility; search excerpts can locate a lead but
are not enough for a difficult label decision. No archive found in this research
proved complete coverage of historical casks.

No reusable image license was established for producer, retailer, review or auction
photographs. Public visibility is not permission to copy. Existing images were
retained; future replacements need an exact-bottle check, source page and verified
reuse terms.
