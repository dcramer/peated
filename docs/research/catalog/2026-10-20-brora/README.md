# Historical Brora releases — October 20–22, 2026

Production catalog follow-up for the request to backfill old Brora releases
without creating generic entries such as “Brora 30.” One marketed release is
one Bottle. Annual releases, bottling years, strengths, named ranges, and cask
numbers distinguish records; age alone does not.

## Results

- Created eleven exact Bottles: B67865–B67869 and B67875–B67880.
- Created Brand E367664, Le Clan des Grands Malts.
- Created Series S1326, Lost Legends, under The Whisky Connoisseur E5679.
- Updated B55658 with its documented 2003 bottling year.
- Unassigned four generic or conflicting import references and assigned two
  exact references.
- No merges, deletions, image uploads, or aliases.
- Final full distiller inventory: 255 records, including three outside this
  single-malt scope. The separate name search also returned B3241, a blend.
- Of the 252 in-scope records after these writes: eleven `create`, one `update`,
  237 `no change`, and three `unresolved`. The three unresolved records are
  preserved existing identities, not new placeholders.

[bottles.csv](bottles.csv) records every current Bottle inspected, its exact
identity fields, disposition, evidence basis, and image provenance status.
[references.csv](references.csv) records the six reference corrections.
[index-reconciliation.csv](index-reconciliation.csv) records the disposition
of every enumerated entry in the two additional historical indexes below.
`no change` means this follow-up found no supported correction; it does not
claim that every unknown fact or image license has been established.

## Scope and inventory

Fetched all three pages for Brora as distiller, all three pages of the separate
Brora name search, the Brora Brand inventory, relevant Brand/Series records,
and all Brora import references. There were 244 distiller-linked records
before the eleven creates. Re-fetched all three distiller pages on October 22
and confirmed that the saved identities and facts had not changed since the
October 20 writes.

Reconciled the current inventory against the
[September review and its family-specific sources](../2026-09-07-brora.md),
the [Whiskyfun Brora index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Brora-Index.html),
[Spirit Radar's Brora inventory](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/distilleries/brora/),
and exact auction, retailer, and label evidence below. These external indexes
are discovery aids, not proof that every historical release has been found.
The September report overstated completion. The first October pass found five
omissions; the deeper pass found six more. Excluding miniature listings simply
because they are miniatures would miss independently marketed releases.

Coverage includes old-stock Brora vintages from 1969–1983, independent releases
from the late 1980s onward, Rare Malts Selection, all 16 annual Special Releases
from 2002–2017, Prima & Ultima, Triptych, Distillery Collection, Casks of
Distinction, and named one-offs through the documented 2024 releases.
Independent families include Cadenhead's, Signatory, Gordon & MacPhail,
Douglas Laing, SMWS, Ian Macleod/William Maxwell, Lombard, Duncan Taylor,
First Cask, and the smaller/private-label releases listed in the earlier audit.
French club, US private selection, and Japanese-market releases were included.

Blends containing Brora, pre-1969 whisky marketed as Clynelish, cask samples,
whole-cask offers, and packaging/size variants of the same release were
excluded. Searches for the historical Ainslie/Glen Brora names did not establish
an additional Brora single-malt release within this scope.

  to a 2004 release at 56.6%, with one tasting attached. Its outturn of 1,515
  conflicts with the documented 3,000-bottle 2004 release and may be an
  individual bottle number. Its earlier generic 55.7% history conflicts with
  the new identity. The generic references were safely detached, but the
  Bottle and tasting were preserved. Resolving its identity and any merge
  requires review and approval; this task did not silently reinterpret it.
