# Brora: release sweep and images

Research date: 26 October 2026. This pass rechecked the Brora catalog after the
[September guide](./2026-09-07-brora.md), the
[October 20 audit](./2026-10-20-brora.md), and the
[modern image guide](./2026-10-22-brora-modern-images.md). It looked for
releases since 2023, rechecked the historical indexes, settled two duplicate
questions, and searched every producer and bottler for usable images.

## Scope

A Brora release is a bottle sold as Brora, from the 1969 rename onward.
Whisky made at the same site before 1969 was sold only as Clynelish, and
collectors, Whiskyfun, and auction houses file it as "old Clynelish." It
is out of scope for a Brora pass (maintainer decision, October 2026). Its
distiller is still the original site, stored as the Brora Entity; see the
[Clynelish guide](./2026-09-29-clynelish.md). Treat it as part of a Clynelish
pass.

Blends that feature Brora are not cataloged here. Each one that names Brora
should list Brora among its distillers.

## Historical indexes rechecked

The Whiskyfun index, Whisky Monitor, Spirit Radar (170 links), Whisky Hunter
(30 bottles), and Whisky-Online's Brora collection (Shopify JSON, 19
products) add nothing beyond size and label variants of stored releases. Two
whisky-news rows are errors: "Brora 1970 Platinum 32 YO 52.4%" is the
Douglas Laing Brechin 1970 strength, and "Rare Malts 1977 21 YO 59.6%" and
"Special Release 2015 50.9%" match no label.

## Duplicates settled

- **Rare Malts 1975, 20 years, 59.1%:** one release with two market labels.
  The 75 cl label ("59.1%vol 75cl", carton) and the US label ("59.1% Alc/Vol
  (118.2 proof) 750ml") share one bottle-number run: the 75 cl bottles found
  run to about 7,300, and the US bottles start above 8,800. See Scotch Whisky
  Auctions lots
  [36381](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/68-the-37th-auction/36381-rare-malts-brora-1975-20-year-old-75cl)
  and
  [107560](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/102-the-64th-auction/107560-rare-malts-brora-1975-20-year-old-75cl/).
  Neither label prints a bottling year. Compilations
  ([Buxrud](https://www.buxrud.se/raremalt.htm),
  [Whisky.Auction Magazine](https://magazine.whisky.auction/how-to/how-to-read-a-rare-malts-selection-label/))
  list "1995, USA" and "1996, 75 cl" as two rows; that split is not printed,
  so the year is stored as unknown. A "South Africa 750 ml" index entry is
  another market label.
- **2004 Special Release, 30 years, 56.6%:** every source lists one 3,000-bottle
  release. A stored outturn of 1,515 was a bottle number; auction lots show
  numbers such as
  [1446 of 3000](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/104-the-66th-auction/114795-brora-30-year-old-2004-release/).

## Image sources

The producer-image rule in
[Review Images](../../operations/catalog-maintenance.md#review-images)
applies. Diageo sites grant no reuse license, so producer images are stored
with `© Diageo`.

- [Diageo Rare & Exceptional](https://www.diageorareandexceptional.com/)
  has 1080 × 1080 packshots for the Prima & Ultima Second, Third, and Fourth
  Releases (product pages under `/us/rare-spirits/rare-whisky/`).
- [malts.com Brora visit page](https://www.malts.com/en-gb/brora/visit)
  serves a 4800 × 4800 front render of the Prima & Ultima 1977 Fourth Release
  and copies of the other two.
- The malts.com site of 2016–2019 kept product pages under
  `/en-us/our-whisky-collection/brora/`. Wayback captured only one Brora
  image from it: the 2016 Special Release (38 years) bottle and box,
  from the
  [Wayback capture of the 2016 product page](https://web.archive.org/web/20190130054344/https://www.malts.com/en-us/our-whisky-collection/brora/brora-38-year-old-2016/).
  The 2019 pages for the 2002–2006, 2012, 2014, 2015, and 2017 releases link
  to `/media/<id>/` packshots that Wayback never captured and the live site no
  longer serves.
- The malts.com site of 2010–2015 left only 150 × 210 thumbnails for the 2008,
  2009, 2011, and 2012 Special Releases, with no captured page. Too small to
  use.
- The 2004–2009 malts.com pages sat behind an age gate and were never
  archived.
- [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:That_Boutique-y_Whisky_Company_-_Brora_Batch_1.jpg)
  holds the Boutique-y Batch 1 label artwork under CC BY-SA 2.0, uploaded by
  Master of Malt for That Boutique-y Whisky Company, with a Commons permission
  ticket. The file is CMYK; convert it to RGB before upload. It prints 47.1%
  but no batch number; 47.1% matches Batch 1 only.
- The Whisky Exchange's image server (`img.thewhiskyexchange.com/900/...`)
  answers direct requests even while the product page is behind Cloudflare.
  The largest Whisky Show 2011 image is 540 × 720. The Whisky Exchange is
  that release's Brand, so its own photo qualifies; its photos of other
  bottlers' releases do not.

## Unresolved

- **Images stored without a reuse grant:** the Rare Malts Selection Brora
  photos have no source and look like auction photos, and four Special
  Releases (2012, 2013, 2014, 2017) and two First Cask and Signatory bottles
  use retailer photos. All show the right release. The maintainer chose to
  keep them for now (October 2026); replace each when a producer image or
  reuse grant turns up.
- **Prima & Ultima Second Release strength:** the Rare & Exceptional page text
  says 49.2%; the label on the same page and Diageo's release document say
  49.4%. 49.4% is kept.
- **Chieftain's Choice 1981, 23 years:** Ian Macleod's 2006 page lists a
  sherry-butt Brora at 48%. Stored Chieftain's 23-year-olds are 46% from
  exact label records; the 48% may be Dun Bheagan cask 1512. Needs a label.
- **Duncan Taylor cask 1426:** Duncan Taylor's 2010 page says 23 years;
  labels photographed at auction say 22 years, bottled 2004. 22 kept.
- **Winston Churchill Collection "Private Cask" Brora 1982, 40 years, 47.3%,
  bottle 46 of 50:**
  [Whisky.Auction lot 199056](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/199056/brora-1982-40-year-old-winston-churchill)
  and the same house's results write-up name Hunter Laing as bottler, but the
  label photos show none. Is it a second release or a relabel of Hunter Laing
  Old & Rare cask HL19390 (47.4%, 50 decanters)? Single source.
- **Sotheby's 2022 cask 480 (1982):** sold as a cask with bottling allowed
  until 2027. No bottle seen yet.
- The open items in the [October 20 audit](./2026-10-20-brora.md#unresolved)
  other than the two duplicates above remain open.
