# Dalmore catalog sources

Research date: 20 September 2026. Scope includes official Dalmore releases and
independent bottlings distilled at Dalmore. This is a source guide, not a claim
that the historical catalog is exhaustive.

## Producer sources

- [Dalmore collections](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/) covers
  the current Principal Collection, rare age statements, travel exclusives,
  Luminary, and Distillery Select. Check regional sites: the
  [US 14-year-old](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-collections/the-principal-collection/the-14/)
  is absent from the global Principal Collection index.
- [Limited editions](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/limited-editions/)
  retains Luminary editions from 2022 onward and recent Distillery Select
  releases. A page URL containing a year does not alone establish a release
  date. Cask Curation and Portfolio include sets; establish independent sale
  before treating each component as a Bottle.
- [Travel exclusives](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/travel-exclusives/)
  covers the Anthology range and Portfolio. Historical travel ranges require
  other sources. Wine age and vintage describe the finishing wine, not
  necessarily the whisky.
- [Cadenhead's Dalmore archive](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/?distillery=dalmore)
  preserves selected releases from the 2010s. Its
  [March 2026 club release](https://www.cadenhead.scot/special-releases/cadenheads-club-march-2026-dalmore-16yo/)
  is on a separate news page. The archive is not a full historical list.
- [Hart Brothers archive](https://hartbrothers.co.uk/our-archives/) retains
  distinct Dalmore 11-year-olds bottled in 2018 and 2019. Pages distinguish
  American oak, Port, Sherry, and the Swiss Cardeira finish. The unsuffixed
  Dalmore page failed in the web cache but was readable by a normal public
  HTTP request.
- SMWS has separate [UK](https://smws.com/archive) and
  [EU](https://smws.eu/archive) archives. Their public catalog frontend uses
  `api.smws.com/api/v1/bottles`, with `store_id`, `parent_id`, `page`, and
  `perPage`. The archive category is 74 for UK and 60 for EU. Read every page
  and match exact cask codes locally. A guessed distillery filter returned
  no bottles despite known matches. Neither regional archive establishes
  coverage of the earliest Society releases.

## Historical evidence and discovery

- [Constellation launch details](https://www.scotchmaltwhisky.co.uk/dalmoreconstellationcollection.htm)
  and [Sotheby's complete collection lot](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2022/21-the-dalmore-constellation-collection/the-dalmore-constellation-collection-21-bt70)
  establish the original 2012 collection, spanning vintages 1964–1992.
  Together they give ages, strengths, cask numbers, and outturns. Some exact
  distillation days conflict; years are safer than unsupported exact dates.
- [Whiskyfun's Dalmore index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Dalmore.html) links
  dated reviews of official and independent releases, including discontinued
  expressions and older casks. Review dates and approximate tasting years
  are not bottling or release dates. Follow the review for label photographs
  and corroborate transcription conflicts.
- Spirit Radar has separate [brand](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/brands/dalmore/)
  and [distillery](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/distilleries/dalmore/)
  indexes. The distillery index is substantially broader and includes
  independent bottlers. Entries repeat sizes and packaging. Use them as
  leads; generated descriptions can confuse age, vintage, and release year.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Rivers guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/dalmore-rivers-collection)
  distinguishes the original 2010 Dee Dram from the seasonal Rivers releases
  in 2011 and 2012. Check season text against bottle photographs and dated
  listings, rather than inferring the season from auction dates.
- [The Whisky Exchange's Dalmore catalog](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/b/40/dalmore-single-malt-scotch-whisky)
  retains discontinued product pages and independent bottlings. Product
  facts are useful, but its historical distillery introduction contains
  obsolete ownership information.
- [Whiskybase's distillery index](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/distillery/81/whiskies)
  appeared in search results but returned HTTP 403 on direct opening.
  Some individual bottle facts were available in indexed results. This
  does not constitute a complete inventory of that database.

## Conflicts and identity traps

- The original Cigar Malt exists at 40% and 43%; Cigar Malt Reserve is a
  separate 44% release. Retailer pages sometimes reuse the shorter name for
  the newer whisky.
- The [2016 Fortuna Meritas announcement](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2016/06/the-dalmore-revamps-tr-exclusive-line/)
  explicitly identifies the range as having no age statement. Regalis is
  not an 18-year-old. The announcement also describes changed finishes
  relative to the 2014 range; do not assume all historical versions share
  one maturation recipe.
- Cadenhead's own 2001 vintage, 18-year-old page says 51.7%; Whiskyfun's
  index says 51.8%. Prefer the bottler for that release, and do not create
  another Bottle from the discrepancy alone.
- Annual rare releases need their own edition evidence. A current generic
  30-year-old page does not establish the strength of the 2021 release.
- Ordinary bottle sizes, gift boxes, artist packaging, and sample containers
  do not establish different whisky identities. Names such as King Alexander
  III and 1263 King Alexander III require duplicate review.

## Images and remaining coverage

Dalmore's [terms of use](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/legal/terms-and-conditions/)
require permission for public reuse of its material. Producer and auction
photographs are useful for identification, but no public reuse license was
established for catalog uploads. Preserve image provenance and obtain reuse
permission before uploading them. Do not substitute another cask's image.

The early SMWS catalog, private casks, older Cadenhead's and Signatory
releases, owner collections such as Stillman's Dram, and country-specific
releases need continued reconciliation. Current producer pages and the
accessible collector indexes do not prove that every historical Dalmore
release has been found.

## Independent bottler archives and regional sources

- Gordon & MacPhail's [sitemap](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/sitemap.xml)
  exposes discontinued Connoisseurs Choice pages. Direct HTTP requests can
  return 403 while normal browser navigation works. The pages identify
  vintage, strength, bottling date and maturation; some render empty outturn
  and cask-number fields. Do not turn URL batch codes into marketed editions
  without label evidence. Several 2005 casks share the same strength.
- The Norwegian importer Daracha keeps dated release books, including its
  [2021 collection](https://content.daracha.no/little-red/Darachas-little-red-2021-1-final.pdf)
  and [2022 release sheets](https://content.daracha.no/Siorsan/Daracha-Siorsan-2022-03-H.pdf).
  These provide exact A. D. Rattray casks, dates and outturns. The producer's
  Dalmore 2003 page truncates its bottling year; the importer supplies 2021.
- [Just Whisky](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/) and
  [The Grand Whisky Auction](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/) retain
  exact Society lots with dates, cask codes and bottle counts. Just Whisky
  pages that fail the fetch tool can work in a normal browser.
- Dated regional menus supplement Society releases absent from the UK
  shop: [Park Hotel Tokyo, January 2019](https://parkhoteltokyo.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2019/05/SMWS-Menu_201901-1.pdf),
  [Garleton Lodge, autumn 2019](https://garletonlodge.co.uk/Menus/Garleton-Lodge-Whisky-List-New-Style-%28Autumn-2019%29.pdf),
  and [SMWS Korea, April 2026](https://fliphtml5.com/fubtv/iqoc/2026_SMWS_APR_OUTTURN_TASTING_MAT_QR/).
  A menu date proves availability, not the initial release date.
- [Cask Code's Dalmore archive](https://caskcode.co.uk/code/13/archive/)
  provides links back to Society pages, primarily for 2021 onward. It
  explicitly disclaims completeness and official affiliation. Its open-data
  license does not establish permission to reuse producer photographs.

## Additional source conflicts

- [Master of Malt's Dalmore 1992 page](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/dalmore-1992-whisky/)
  currently says bottled 2006 in the title but 2004 in its description.
  Both statements remain present in a normal browser. This page alone cannot
  distinguish those releases.
- For Gordon & MacPhail's 2005 Kirsch cask, the
  [importer's announcement](https://whiskyexperts.net/pr-neu-bei-kirsch-import-duncan-taylor-dingle-gm-bb-rudd-puni-hammerschmiede-und-teeling/)
  gives cask 16600212 and 254 bottles. Whiskyfun transcribes the cask as
  1600212 and gives 255 bottles; prefer the importer.
- The 1999/2014 Connoisseurs Choice has conflicting American-hogshead and
  sherry-hogshead descriptions. Leave its maturation unresolved until the
  exact label settles it.
- SMWS 13.84 has conflicting 59.0% collector data and 59.5% bar-menu data;
  strength remains unknown pending an exact readable label. SMWS 13.91 also
  has a 60% UK-page versus 62.5% regional-data conflict.
- [Chieftain's cask 94721](https://www.htfw.com/dalmore-chieftain-s-single-pomerol-wine-cask-94721-1996-19-year-old-whisky)
  is called Pomerol in the retailer title and Monthélie Les Riottes in its
  description. Its identity is clear, but the wine finish needs label review.
- Chieftain's casks 94221/94222 have 46% on The Whisky Barrel and 43% in
  contemporary Highland Herold release listings and collector records.
  The contemporary 43% evidence is stronger; do not create a second release
  from the isolated retailer discrepancy.

### Further independent labels

[Whisky Hammer's Chieftain's 1994/2005 lot](https://www.whiskyhammer.com/item/154633/Dalmore/Dalmore---10-Year-Old-1994-Chieftains.html) independently establishes the ten-year-old, two-hogshead release and exact outturn. [A contemporary Chinese-language tasting](https://pomerol82.blogspot.com/2012/04/) corroborates those facts. The blog's name “pomerol82” is not evidence of a Pomerol finish; the auction specifies only hogsheads. Whisky Hammer's direct image route presents Cloudflare, so the accessible lot description supports the record without importing an image.

[Whisky Auctioneer's Dun Bheagan 1997 lot](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/10711/dalmore-1997-dun-bheagan-14-year-old) confirms age, strength, bottling year, butt maturation and outturn. [Classicdram's Ian Macleod table](https://classicdram.com/whisky-belong-to-malt.html?bottler=Ian+MacLeod) supplies the corresponding cask number and also retains several early wine- and rum-finished Dalmores. Its individual bottle pages fail in the web fetch tool even when its table is readable.

[Master of Malt's Wemyss archive](https://www.masterofmalt.com/distilleries/wemyss-malts-whisky/)
retains Mocha Spice, Spiced Figs and Gooseberry Marmalade under their tasting-note
names. The [2010 Tropical Spice review](https://alcoholandaphorisms.wordpress.com/2010/10/11/wemyss-malts-dalmore-1990-tropical-spice/)
and [Whisky Auctioneer's Spiced Figs entry](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/dalmore-1990-wemyss-malts-18-year-old-spiced-figs)
help distinguish these from similarly named releases from other distilleries.
Master of Malt's January 1 dates are year placeholders; do not store those days.

[Càrn Mòr's 2000 Strictly Limited release](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/dalmore/dalmore-14-year-old-2000-strictly-limited-carn-mor-whisky/)
combined two bourbon barrels. The [Halls 2022 auction catalog](https://www.hallsauction.com/PDF/Auction382.pdf)
records the 1990 Bequest release for BC Liquor Stores, while
[Whisky News](https://www.whisky-news.com/En/Tasting036.html) records the 1991
Celebration of the Cask bottling for World of Whisky. Halls incorrectly calls
the independently bottled Bequest release distillery bottled.

[Alambic Classique's 2025 Dalmore](https://www.whisky-erlebnis-ol.de/p/dalmore-2005-2025-sherry-single-cask-25022-54-9-alambic-classique)
has a detailed retailer record. Earlier 2005 bottlings appear in Whisky.de,
Whisky Auctioneer and the Highland Herold archive. For the Fino cask,
[Highland Herold 29](https://highland-herold-files.s3.amazonaws.com/documents/TheHighlandHerold_29.pdf)
reports 167 bottles but Whiskybase reports 276; the outturn remains unknown.

[Atom Brands](https://www.atombrands.com/our-brands/darkness) identifies Darkness
as its sherry-octave label. Master of Malt retains separate Dalmore 14-year-old
pages at 49.1% and 55.9%; Hard to Find Whisky also retains the 16-year-old at
54.6%. These different strengths and ages identify distinct releases.

The producer's [UK travel shop](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-gb/our-collections/travel-exclusives/)
offers each Portfolio expression separately. Its
[2025 artist introduction](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-world/the-making-of-the-dalmore-portfolio-series-2025-introducing-artist-gabriella-marcella/)
explicitly says the same three parcels are released annually with new artwork;
keep the three liquids distinct without creating duplicates for packaging years.
The same article confirms natural colour and no chill filtration. Per-year
packaging outturns should not be treated as the total outturn of the liquid.

The producer also retains individual Trio, Quartet and Quintet pages with their
cask recipes. [The 2019 launch announcement](https://moodiedavittreport.com/whyte-mackay-unveils-travel-retail-exclusive-the-dalmore-collection/)
gave July 2020 as planned availability; do not turn that plan into an exact
release date without confirming actual availability. The Ensemble name appears
in the producer's packaging and later travel-retail coverage.

[Meidi-ya's July 2008 importer announcement](https://www.meidi-ya.co.jp/news/20080704.html)
is useful contemporary evidence for Gran Reserva and the redesigned core range.

SMWS regional sites preserve useful alternate titles. The American
[13.102 page](https://smwsa.com/products/cask-no-13-102) calls it “A Broadway
smash”; the [South African product page](https://smws.co.za/products/bubbling-brown-sugar)
uses “Bubbling Brown Sugar” and supplies the distillation date and worldwide
outturn. These are the same cask. The American allocation is not its total
outturn. The [Sherry Showdown comparison](https://smwsa.com/products/distillery-dive-13-sherry-showdown)
confirms the shared distillation year and different sherry maturation for
13.100 and 13.101. The [13.112 page](https://smwsa.com/products/cask-no-13-112)
identifies the Burns Night 2025 release.

For McGibbon's Provenance, compare [Classicdram's bottler index](https://www.classicdram.com/whisky-belong-to-malt.html?bottler=Douglas+McGibbon),
[The Whisky Exchange's 1992 release](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/81031/dalmore-1992-12-year-old-provenance),
and Master of Malt's surviving listings. The label on its
[cask 9501 page](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/dalmore/dalmore-12-year-old-2000-cask-9501-provenance-douglas-laing-whisky/)
reads McGibbon's Provenance even though the retailer categorizes it under the
parent company, Douglas Laing. Master of Malt's cask 6879 prose says 2013,
while its facts and [Fine Spirits Auction](https://www.finespirits.auction/uk/spirit-prices/212750------Bottle-Highlands-Dalmore-11-years-1999-Douglas-McGibbon-Provenance-Spring-Distillation-DMG-Ref-6879-bottled-2011-Special-Selection-Brown.jsp)
say 2011; use the latter. The 1989 release and 1999 cask 6566 still need
independent corroboration beyond collector indexes. Exact-name, vintage,
cask-number and Provenance searches did not produce an accessible exact
retailer or auction record. The 1991 release has conflicting cask numbers:
[Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/dalmore-1991-provenance-14-year-old-winter)
says 1695 and 1696; Whiskybase says 1995 and 1996. A readable label is needed.

Master of Malt retains detailed [Montgomerie's Rare Select listings](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/dalmore/dalmore-23-year-old-1991-cask-12174-rare-select-montgomeries-whisky/),
including vintage, age, bottling year and cask number. Its 1986 cask 3093 page
also gives the outturn; [WhiskyNotes](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2014/dalmore/dalmore-1986-montgomeries/)
provides a contemporary cross-check.

Some retailers retroactively attribute old Old Malt Cask releases to Hunter
Laing. For the [1999 cask 7096](https://www.thewhiskybarrel.com/products/dalmore-12-year-old-1999-old-malt-cask),
the 2011 bottling predates that business. [Delia Whisky](https://deliawhisky.de/en/products/art-nr-fehlt-dalmore-12-jahre-1999-2011-old-malt-cask-50-0-7l)
also mixes the two names, but describes Douglas Laing in its prose and gives
the DL cask reference. The pages disagree on March versus April; retain only
the bottling year. Do not infer cask strength from that retailer's generic
prose for the 50% range.

### Further Cadenhead and Duncan Taylor sources

[Passion for Whisky](https://www.passionforwhisky.com/en/whisky/scotch-whisky/single-malt-whisky/highlands/dalmore-whisky-distillery/32878-dalmore-20-years-1990-2010-4318.html) retains detailed old stock records, including Cadenhead's 1987/1999 and 1989/2005 Authentic Collection bottlings and Duncan Taylor's 1990/2010 Rare Auld cask. [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/39683/dalmore-1992-25-year-old-cadenheads) covers Cadenhead's 1992/2017 release; its [1976/1996 page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/85159/dalmore-1976-19-year-old-authentic-collection-cadenheads) distinguishes the 58.7% bottling from other releases of the same vintage and age. [MaltKlaus's contemporary review](https://maltklaus.net/dailydram/2016/05/24/tasting-dalmore-1992-22-years-by-cadenheads/) and Fine Spirits Auction corroborate the 1992/2014 bottling.

[Shinanoya's March 2014 announcement](https://sakedori.com/s/shinanoya/blog/7936.html) is primary evidence for the Japan-exclusive Cadenhead Small Batch cask 338. Its preorder date is not necessarily its shipment date. [Master of Malt's Duthies listing](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/dalmore/dalmore-11-year-old-duthies-wm-cadenhead/) identifies the 11-year-old release and states its filtration, colour, and outturn.

Exact searches for Cadenhead's 1992/2006 at 62%, 1989/2003 at 58.2%, and the purported 1984 Bond Reserve for Limburg did not find independent confirmation beyond collector indexes. Whisky Magazine's old Cadenhead tasting page mixes a 12-year-old title with 13-year-old facts and mislabels a vintage as a bottling year; it cannot resolve the 1987/2000 release.

[Whisky.Auction's 2006/2022 Octave lot](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/168999/dalmore-2006-16-year-old-the-octave) records the exact cask and outturn. For Octave cask 1027524, Master of Malt says 90 bottles while [Whizita](https://www.whizita.de/de/product/dalmore-2004-the-octave-551/) and the collector index say 92. Leave the outturn unresolved until a readable label settles this. The Kirsch Import price list identifies this as Octave Premium.

### Smaller independent labels and archival gaps

[The Ultimate's bottling history](https://www.ultimatewhisky.com/ult_hist.pdf) confirms the 1986 and 1990 casks. It omits cask 9422, which [Passie voor Whisky](https://www.passievoorwhisky.nl/nl/whisky/schotse-whisky/single-malt-whisky/highlands/dalmore-whisky-distillery/32944-dalmore-19-years-1990-2010-9422.html) documents separately. The producer archive does not state ages for those old entries. [Classicdram](https://classicdram.com/whisky-belong-to-malt.html?bottler=van+Wees) corroborates the age of cask 9420; iDealwine has a detailed record of cask 12/894. Neither historical cask 3082's stated age nor cask 9420's exact outturn was independently established. The producer's current website documents the range's non-chill-filtered policy.

[The Whiskyphiles' Duthies review](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2015/01/26/dalmore-11-years-old-duthies-cadenheads/) quotes the Cadenhead collection's history. Its bourbon maturation and small-batch interpretation are expressly assumptions, so they should not become facts. [Alexander Murray's own Dalmore page](https://alexandermurray.com/vintage-malts/dalmore-1999/) confirms that release is a vatting of bourbon hogsheads and sherry butts, rather than a single cask.

[The Whisky Company's Adelphi listing](https://www.thewhiskycompany.com.au/product/dalmore-2007-aged-14-years-single-malt-scotch-whisky-adelphi-3065-700ml/) provides dates, cask and outturn for the 2007 release. For the 1998 cask 8217, [Blue Cask](https://www.blue-cask.com/en/products/dalmore-1998-2019-21-jahre) says refill sherry, but [a report of an Adelphi tasting](https://theoldmanofhuy.blogspot.com/2020/05/06052020-london-whisky-club-adelphi.html) says the printed sherry statement was wrong and the cask was bourbon. Maturation remains disputed.

[Whisky Auctioneer's Whiskybroker lot](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/276964/dalmore-2007-whisky-broker-13-year-old) explains how cask 702099 was divided into four Oloroso octaves and recombined. A cask number alone would have concealed that process. Exact searches for Whisky Chamber cask 2128 found inconsistent vintage and strength in Whisky-news; its Hogmanay cask 800463 still lacks independent confirmation beyond collector lists. Whiskybroker's 2013 eight-year-old is independently listed by Whisky Auctioneer, but its collector-only cask and outturn need a readable label.

[Deer, Bear & Moose's producer archive](https://www.deerbearandmoose.com/) lists the Dalmore edition, actual release date and outturn. [Whisky Time's exact review](https://whiskytime.pl/2021/07/recenzja-whisky-dalmore-2004-flaviar-sherry-butt-6529/) supplies age, cask, filtration and colour facts. [Words of Whisky](https://wordsofwhisky.com/dalmore-5-years-dailuaine-7-years-torsten-paul-whisky-company/) covers Scotch & Tattoos' first releases and its unusual ex-Aultmore Oloroso octave finish; [The Single Cask](https://www.thesinglecask.co.uk/pages/scotch-tattoos-brand-page) identifies the label and its founder.

[World Wine & Whisky](https://worldwinewhisky.com/products/dalmore-30-years-old-1966-1996-cask-6868-the-bottl) quotes The Bottlers' untreated cask-strength policy and gives dated facts for cask 6868. Its approximate outturn is not an exact bottle count. Flaviar and auction catalogues mix cask 6869 at 51.7% and 54.1%; a readable label is needed to settle the cask/strength combinations. [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/41201/dalmore-1978-33-year-old-scotia-royale) and Bonhams establish Scotia Royale's 1978 release. [Whisky Antique](https://www.whiskyantique.com/en/dalmore-1980-1996-70cl-50-moon-import-dovr-toutes-mares-cv2003) and Sylvie's auction catalogue cover Moon Import's Dovr-Toutes-Mares Dalmore.

The [Distillers' Charity catalogue](https://whiskyexperts.net/wp-content/uploads/Distillers__Charity_Auction_Catalogue.pdf) explicitly dates Sirius's bottling to late 2011, contrary to collector entries saying 2012. [Farr Vintners](https://www.farrvintners.com/spirits/spirit.php?spirit=36230) corroborates the narrative facts; its header mistakenly says 70% while the tasting text correctly says 64.3%. Exact English searches for Shieldaig cask 8215 and Or Sileis cask 9432 found only collector-index facts. Their identities still need a primary release page, label, or independent exact lot.

### Japan exclusives and newer independent labels

[Shinanoya's Dalmore archive](https://www.shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/category/ct850) retains sold-out releases and readable labels. The browser works where fetch tools return errors. It covers Old Particular's Asia Pacific Friendship cask, Old & Rare selections for Shinanoya and Osaka bars, the Toyosu shop selection, and The Sixth Sense. For [Toyosu's cask 2167](https://www.shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/item/000000017756), the label says “Abeyhill”; auction descriptions recognize this spelling as Abbeyhill. Do not confuse it with the unrelated English company of the same spelling. The [Sixth Sense label](https://www.shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/item/000000016654) explicitly establishes single-cask, cask-strength, uncoloured and non-chill-filtered status. Old & Rare's [Minmore House/Aiba selection](https://www.shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/item/000000019905) has a precise cask and outturn. Shipping announcements are not used as actual release dates.

The [Oceans 2007 product](https://www.shinanoya-tokyo.jp/view/item/000000012458) contradicts itself: cask 3073 in its title, 3076 in the specification table. The cask remains unknown pending a readable label. [Whisk-e's Blossoms page](https://whisk-e.co.jp/products/blossoms_dalmore1991/) supplies the 1991 release's exact cask and announcement date.

[No Nonsense Whisky's release announcement](https://www.nononsensewhisky.com/blog/actual-no-nonsense-whisky) and [The Dramble](https://www.thedramble.com/tastings/dalmore/dalmore-2011/) establish its first release. [The Single Cask's Dalmore history](https://www.thesinglecask.co.uk/blogs/distilleries/dalmore-distillery) links its two seven-year-old releases: cask 800147 finished in tawny port and 800147A in rum. Their different finishes, strengths and outturns identify separate liquids. [Fadandel's own bottling table](https://fadandel.com/ratings-on-fadandel-dk-bottlings/) documents the 2009 Oloroso-octave finish, including precise distillation and bottling dates.

### Anthology and Select Edition naming

Dalmore's [Anthology 13](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/travel-exclusives/the-dalmore-anthology-aged-13-years/), [16](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/travel-exclusives/the-dalmore-anthology-aged-16-years/), and [19](https://www.thedalmore.com/en/our-collections/travel-exclusives/the-dalmore-anthology-aged-19-years/) pages establish the collection and bourbon-to-PX maturation. [Moodie Davitt's February 2026 report](https://moodiedavittreport.com/whyte-mackay-partners-with-ospree-duty-free-to-bring-the-dalmore-anthology-to-mumbai-airport/) dates the collection's launch to the preceding October.

The producer's US [Select Edition 2007](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-collections/limited-editions/select-edition-2007-18-year-old/) page specifies Apostoles and Amoroso sherry, while secondary reviews say Matusalem. Prefer the producer for that release. Its [2010 page](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-collections/limited-editions/select-edition-2010-15-year-old/) does specify Matusalem and Amoroso, and explicitly confirms natural colour and no chill filtration. US release announcements may follow earlier international availability; they do not establish the first worldwide release date.

The producer's [Select Edition 2006](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-collections/limited-editions/select-edition-2006/) and [2009](https://www.thedalmore.com/en-us/our-collections/limited-editions/select-edition-2009/) pages explicitly confirm age-bearing titles, natural colour, no chill filtration, and Matusalem/Amoroso maturation. [The Spirits Business's 2024 announcement](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2024/11/dalmore-expands-vintage-collection/) calls Select Editions the US name for Vintage Collection, but describes a different finish for the 2009 Vintage. [WhiskyExperts' 2026 announcement](https://whiskyexperts.net/neu-the-dalmore-select-editions-2007-2010/) expressly distinguishes Select Editions from similar-looking Vintage Editions. Do not merge releases, assign one range to the other, or transfer first-release dates on this evidence alone. Producer confirmation of the two ranges' liquid identity remains needed.

### Corrections found in existing independent records

The readable front and back labels at [Whisky Erlebnis](https://www.whisky-erlebnis-ol.de/p/dalmore-2015-2026-1st-fill-bourbon-barrels-52-7-james-eadie) resolve James Eadie's ten-year-old release: the back label says distilled in 2015, bottled in 2026, and matured in two first-fill bourbon barrels. The shop's own table says 2016, and Master of Malt repeats that error. The printed label takes precedence. The label also establishes the total outturn and natural-colour/unfiltered status.

[Decadent Drinks' archived product page](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/dalmore-1991-whisky-sponge-edition-no10) retains large label art for Edition No.10. It proves The Patersponge Collection membership, outturn, vintage, bottling year and refill bourbon barrel. The producer's prose confirms no caramel or filtration. [Whiskyfun's 2014 review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2014/Four-very-unusual-Dalmore.html) and [Booze Dancing's contemporary review](https://boozedancing.com/2013/09/25/whisky-review-single-cask-nation-laphroiag-cask-119-dalmore-cask-695/) establish Single Cask Nation's 12-year-old cask 6951 and its ten-month PX finish.

The Duchess's 2013 release has a cask-code conflict: [WhiskyNotes' product review](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2026/longmorn/duchess-longmorn-2009-dalmore-2013/) and collector listings say 9002541, while [Long Pour Amour](https://longpouramour.com/the-duchess-new-releases/) and WhiskyNotes' category index say 90025-II. The accessible front label does not settle this. The [producer's collection archive](https://duchess-spirits.com/our-collection) is readable in a browser but did not yet list Dalmore. Exact searches for the Best of Wines product did not find an accessible listing. Keep the cask code unresolved pending a back-label image or producer specification.
