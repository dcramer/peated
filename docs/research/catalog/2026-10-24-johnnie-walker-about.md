# Johnnie Walker About example — October 24, 2026

This file supports the brand example in
`apps/server/src/lib/entityAbout/examples/johnnieWalker.ts`, the second style
example in the About writer's prompt. Johnnie Walker is E1093 in Peated, a
brand. No production records were changed.

## Where the facts live

- Diageo's [Johnnie Walker page](https://www.diageo.com/en/our-brands/scotch-whisky/johnnie-walker)
  is the owner's own account: the 1820 grocer's shop, the 1908 Striding Man,
  120 countries by 1920, and Johnnie Walker Princes Street in 2021. It is short
  and has no dates for the owner changes.
- Wikipedia's [Johnnie Walker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johnnie_Walker)
  page has the family history, the 1860 square bottle, the 1865 Old Highland
  blend, Cardhu in 1893, the 1909 label colours, the Distillers Company in
  1925, Guinness in 1986, Diageo in 1997, and the March 2012 Kilmarnock
  closure. The text is CC BY-SA, so the example quotes it briefly and writes
  its own sentences.

## Traps

- The Striding Man: Diageo and Wikipedia's marketing section say 1908, when
  the advertisements began. Wikipedia's history section ties it to the 1909
  rebranding. The example stores 1908 and keeps the 1909 citation, so the page
  shows "Sources disagree".
- The cartoonist is "Tom Browne" on Wikipedia and "Tom Brown" on Diageo's page.
  The example does not name him.
- The brand began as a grocer's shop, not a distillery. John Walker's own
  blends were sold as "Walker's Kilmarnock Whisky" before the Johnnie Walker
  name; Wikipedia gives no year, so the example leaves it out.
- 1997 is a merger. Guinness and Grand Metropolitan formed Diageo; the example
  records Diageo as the new owner that year.

## Unknowns

- When the Johnnie Walker name first went on a label.
- Exact dates for the 1925 and 1986 owner changes.
