# FEW whiskey, independent bottlings, and collaborations

Researched September 20, 2026. This guide covers FEW-branded whiskey, releases
by independent bottlers using FEW spirit, and the Four Kings collaboration.
It includes core and discontinued expressions, music collaborations, and
identifiable retailer and importer selections. Gin and unaged spirits are
outside the whiskey catalog.

The sources establish releases from the early 2010s through the 2024 launches,
with continuing SMWS distribution in 2025–2026. They do not establish a complete
register of private barrels. Searches for `FEW` alone often treat the name as an
ordinary word; use the full producer and expression names. A later regional
launch or renamed SMWS subtitle does not establish a different release.

## Core range and later launches

- [FEW's product page](https://fewspirits.com/products/) establishes the core
  bourbon and rye, American Straight Whiskey, Single Malt, Cold Cut, Bottled in
  Bond Bourbon, and several music collaborations. It explains that American
  Straight Whiskey blends bourbon, rye, and cherrywood-smoked malt whiskey.
  It is not a historical release register and does not date every expression.
- Distiller's pages for [American Straight Whiskey](https://distiller.com/spirits/few-american-whiskey),
  [Cold Cut](https://distiller.com/spirits/few-cold-cut-bourbon), and
  [Single Malt](https://distiller.com/spirits/few-single-malt-whisky) supply proof
  and production details. American Straight Whiskey launched in September
  2018; Cold Cut followed in November 2019. Case allocations are not bottle
  outturns unless the case size is established.
- [Bottled in Bond Bourbon](https://distiller.com/spirits/few-bottled-in-bond-bourbon)
  documents the November 2021 launch. The
  [producer's rye announcement](https://www.breakingbourbon.com/bourbon-whiskey-press-releases/few-spirits-announces-few-rye-whiskey-bottled-in-bond)
  gives October 2022 availability. Both are 50% ABV and aged at least four years.
  A minimum maturation claim does not by itself establish a printed age statement.
- [Bevvy's interview about Immortal Rye](https://bevvy.co/articles/few-spirits-immortal-rye-whiskey-tea/18655)
  establishes the 2020 release, 46.5% ABV, and proofing with The Tea Spot's Eight
  Immortals oolong. **Eight Immortals is a tea name, not an eight-year age.** The
  starting whiskey is cask strength; the finished product is diluted with tea.
- [The tenth-anniversary announcement](https://thewhiskeywash.com/bourbon/new-few-spirits-four-grain-bourbon-celebrates-decade-of-making-spirits/)
  covers the September 2021 four-grain bourbon. The whiskey spent six years in
  American white oak; the tenth anniversary refers to the producer.
- [Heaven Hill's Chicago Smokeworks announcement](https://heavenhill.com/news-and-notes/with-chicago-smokeworks-whiskey-few-spirits-commemorates-the-great-chicago-fire-looks-ahead-to-2024-tailgating-season/)
  gives August 2024 availability and 46.5% ABV. It explicitly names malted barley
  **and rye grain**. Do not classify it as single malt from its title alone;
  leave the category unknown without a supported fit to Peated's categories.
- [The Wee Dram's Triple Smoke listing](https://www.weedram.co.uk/product/few-triple-smoke/)
  identifies an official FEW single malt made with barley smoked over three
  woods. It does not establish which woods, peat use, or an initial release date.
- [Unicorn Auctions' November 2023 tasting brief](https://www.unicornauctions.com/unicorn-review/tasting-brief-rye-and-american-whiskey)
  identifies Cereal Killer Straight Rye and its use of koji with rye and wheat.
  [Main Street Liquor](https://mainstreetliquor.com/products/few-cereal-killer-straight-rye-whiskey)
  is a further label lead. ABV, launch date, age, and outturn remain unknown;
  the review date is not a release date.

## Collaborations, 2015–2024

- [Tales of the Cocktail's custom-blend interview](https://talesofthecocktail.org/custom-whiskey-blends-and-customers-who-love-them/)
  records the July 2015 Bloodshot Records bourbon and its bottle allocation.
  [Please the Palate's founder interview](https://pleasethepalate.com/startup-stories-of-spirits-entrepreneurs-few-spirits/)
  corroborates the record label's 21st-anniversary collaboration. Proof and
  whiskey age remain unknown.
- [The Brainville launch announcement](https://www.barbizmag.com/spirits/few-spirits-flaming-lips-whiskey-collaboration/)
  gives October 2016 availability, 80 proof, new charred American oak, and the
  limited bottle run. July is the announcement month, not the planned release.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Delilah's catalog](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/few-spirits-delilah-23rd-anniversary)
  documents the 2016 anniversary bottling at 50% ABV, drawn from 23 casks.
  [Whisky Advocate's White Lightning announcement](https://whiskyadvocate.com/kentucky-owl-rye-new-hillrock-estate-bourbon-few-white-lightning/)
  independently identifies Delilah's as a blend of bourbon, rye, and single malt.
  That article also dates Bloodshot Records White Lightning to September 9,
  2017: five kinds of malted barley, briefly rested in used FEW whiskey barrels,
  at 50.5% ABV. Despite the name, it is not simply unaged corn spirit.
- [Whisky Advocate's November 2017 releases](https://whiskyadvocate.com/lagavulin-8-cotswolds-isaac-bowman-whisky-whiskey/)
  cover Italia bourbon and both Copper & Kings finishes, with strengths and
  outturns. [The Manual's Italia review](https://www.themanual.com/food-and-drink/few-italia-bourbon/)
  corroborates the Donnafugata Sicilian wine finish. Its 2020 publication date
  does not supersede the 2017 launch.
- [FEW's product page](https://fewspirits.com/products/) dates the first Alice in
  Chains collaboration to 2019. The existing Batch 19H19 identifier is preserved;
  it is not enough evidence to infer a precise bottling date or apply the full
  first-release outturn to that batch.
  [Whisky Magazine's return announcement](https://www.whiskymag.com/articles/few-spirits-releases-whiskey-with-alice-in-chains/)
  identifies a separate September 28, 2023 release. Both use a six-month tequila
  cask finish and 50.5% ABV.
- [Motor Oil's announcement](https://whiskyadvocate.com/few-spirits-motor-oil-the-macallan-m-collection-more-new-releases/)
  gives July 1, 2022, 50.5% ABV, and the original limited run. This Black Rebel
  Motorcycle Club collaboration blends two finished bourbons with smoked wheat
  whiskey. Its three components are not separate FEW retail releases.
- [Heaven Hill's Icons of Rock announcement](https://heavenhill.com/news-and-notes/a-very-rock-n-roll-holiday-few-spirits-releases-icons-of-rock-collection-featuring-whiskey-collabs-with-legends-of-modern-rock/)
  covers Smashing Pumpkins bourbon with Midnight Rose tea and the 2024 bundle
  with Motor Oil and All Secrets Known. The bundle does not establish new
  liquids or separate 2024 editions of every included bottle. No new Series was
  inferred from the gift collection.
- [A dated Distiller tasting](https://distiller.com/tastes/752778) corroborates
  Sarah Shook & the Disarmers Blue Corn Bourbon by late 2019. Searches of producer
  and trade material did not recover a launch announcement or readable label
  sufficient to establish further facts. Existing identity and strength were
  retained; age, exact launch date, cask details, and outturn remain unknown.

## Retailer and importer selections

These are official FEW bottles. The selector belongs in the edition, not in the
Brand or independent bottler field.

- [Breaking Bourbon selection 37](https://www.breakingbourbon.com/single-barrel-club/sbc-37-few-bourbon-the-strong-the-proud-the-few)
  documents The Strong - The Proud - The FEW, sold through De Wine Spot in
  December 2020. It explicitly distinguishes a two-year **label statement** from
  the barrel's actual age of more than three years. Use the label age.
- [PB Express Liquor's selection](https://pbexpressliquor.com/products/few-single-barrel-pb-express-liquor-bottled-in-bond-bourbon-whiskey-exclusive-store-pick)

  identifies another selection. S1B28 is a selection identifier; do not assume
  it is a printed cask number. Ambiguous shop date fields were not converted
  into vintage or bottling dates.
- Clos des Millésimes documents the Antipodes selections for Whisky Live Paris
  2022: [bourbon](https://www.closdesmillesimes.com/27991-clos-des-millesimes-few---bourbon---single-cask-n-170914---5050.html)
  and [rye](https://www.closdesspiritueux.com/27992-clos-des-millesimes-few---rye---single-cask-n-172723---5050.html).

  [Whiskyfun's bourbon review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2023/A-wild-bunch-of-American-whiskies-part-tres.html)

- [Rhum Attitude's Foundations rye](https://www.rhumattitude.com/en/cave/few-7-ans-straight-rye-sauternes-cask-finish-foundations-465/)
  establishes seven years, 46.5%, a single cask, and three years in new oak
  followed by four in Sauternes wood.
  [Whiskyfun's 2024 review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2024/The-return-of-American-whiskies-on-WF.html)
  corroborates the importer collection. A review year alone is insufficient
  to establish the exact release date.
- Whisky Auctioneer identifies the British Bourbon Society's
  [bourbon Batch 13-0494](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/few-spirits-bourbon-batch-13-0494-70cl-british-bourbon-society)
  at 64.38%. Its [related-bottle catalog](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/few-spirits-delilah-23rd-anniversary)
  also identifies the society's single-barrel rye 628 at 59.5%. A full rye label
  is still needed to establish the complete barrel code and any dates.

  [61.5% Master of Malt selection](https://maltfascination.com/2013/11/12/few-bourbon-cask-strength-61-5/).
  The latter review is from November 2013; that is an availability bound, not
  the exact launch date. Neither source establishes the earlier cask number.
- [Maverick Drinks' producer page](https://www.maverickdrinks.com/few-spirits)
  and [downloadable product resources](https://www.maverickdrinks.com/few-resources)

  60.57% ABV. Spirit Radar associates this strength with Batch 17I14, but the
  reviewed label does not show the batch. Keep that code unconfirmed.

## Independent bottlings

### Scotch Malt Whisky Society

The relevant codes are B4 for bourbon and RW1 for rye. The checked sources cover
B4.1–B4.11 and RW1.1–RW1.5 plus RW1.7–RW1.12. Searches of the producer archives,
auction records, and exact code/title combinations did not establish RW1.6,
B4.12, or RW1.13. A gap in numbering is not evidence of a marketed bottle.

- [Abbey Whisky's B4.1 entry](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/products/few-3-year-old-bourbon-whiskey-2012-smws-b4-1-aw01479)
  documents Comforting coconut, bountiful banana. The
  [Australian producer announcement](https://smws.com.au/a-year-of-firsts-announcing-b4-1/)
  corroborates FEW as the distiller and single-cask, cask-strength bottling.
  Its local allocation is not the global outturn, and its displayed timestamp
  does not agree cleanly with the October release wording.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's B4.2 record](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/few-spirits-smws-b42-3-year-old)
  has a readable label proving the subtitle, 2012 distillation, three years,
  61.3%, and global outturn. Its label says new charred barrel; a collector
  description of toasted wood does not supersede that label.
- [Malt Fascination's B4.3 review](https://maltfascination.com/2018/03/29/few-3yo-2013-2016-63-4-smws-b4-3-full-flavour-behaviour/)
  establishes the 2013–2016 identity.
  [Caves Damiani's January 2017 tasting](https://www.caves-damiani.com/club-de-whisky/club-de-whisky-degustations-2017/)
  corroborates its age, proof, new charred oak, and outturn. The Whisky Barrel's
  2003 distillation year conflicts with both this evidence and FEW's founding.
- SMWS's US product URLs use `https://smwsa.com/products/cask-no-b4-7` and
  `https://smwsa.com/products/cask-no-rw1-2` patterns. The UK archive uses title
  slugs, such as [Tropical cheesecake](https://smws.com/tropical-cheesecake/)
  and [Sweet, punchy and everlasting](https://smws.com/sweet-punchy-and-everlasting/).
  These cover the existing later bourbon and rye entries. Most pages do not
  give global outturns or first release dates. US allocation must not be used
  as total outturn; the RW1.5 page even has conflicting US allocation figures.
- The Swiss producer shop documents
  [B4.9 Hoisin duck skin](https://shop.smws.ch/pi/en/Whisky/B49-HOISIN-DUCK-SKIN.html)
  and [RW1.9 Mint leaf marmalade](https://shop.smws.ch/pi/en/Whisky/RW19-MINT-LEAF-MARMALADE.html),
  including global outturns, distillation dates, ages, ABVs, and cask types.
  These pages worked in a browser when text fetching failed.
- Regional names can differ. The US
  [B4.11 Sweetness and like](https://smwsa.com/products/cask-no-b4-11)
  matches the UK Like a grown-up in a candy store. The US
  [Windy City Whiskey collection](https://smwsa.com/products/windy-city-whiskey)
  identifies B4.10 as All things bright and bold, matching Tropical cheesecake,
  and dates RW1.5's distillation to June 2017. Keep proven alternate subtitles
  as aliases. RW1.7's older Char's in their ryes alias remains an evidence gap;
  the current producer page uses Full-throttle bottle.

### That Boutique-y Whisky Company

- [The producer's FEW archive](https://www.thatboutiqueywhiskycompany.com/whiskies/few)
  contains the early bourbon batches. Batch 3's bottle photograph proves three
  years, 50.1%, and its small outturn. Batch 4's page mistakenly says three years;
  the producer's own label artwork says two years, corroborated by
  [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/spirit/few-spirits/few-that-boutiquey-bourbon-company-spirit/).
  Use two years. The producer page dates Batch 4 to June 2018.
- [The Whisky Exchange's Batch 1](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/27302/few-bourbon-batch-1-that-boutique-y-whisky-company)
  establishes 48.4%, the outturn, and a wheated bourbon mashbill.
  [Whisky Auctioneer's Batch 2](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/few-spirits-boutique-y-whisky-company-2-year-old-bourbon-batch-2)
  supplies the two-year, 51.8% identity and a readable outturn on the label.
- [Batch 5 in the USA Series](https://www.thatboutiqueywhiskycompany.com/usa-baseball-cards/few-spirits)
  is six-year-old straight rye with a Port finish, 63.6%, and **398** bottles.
  A retailer transposes the outturn to 389. The producer's claim that its 2014
  first release was rye also conflicts with the actual Batch 1 bourbon label;
  do not invent a separate early rye batch from that sentence.
- [Batch 6 in the Cinema Series](https://www.thatboutiqueywhiskycompany.com/tbwc-at-the-movies/few)
  is four-year-old straight rye finished in a Kyrö rye cask, at 50.7%.
  [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/72980/few-4-year-old-batch-6-that-boutique-y-whisky-company)
  provides its outturn. Its generic bourbon category conflicts with its own
  description and the producer's explicit rye category.
- The labels use Whisky Company, Bourbon Company, and Rye Company wording
  within the Boutique-y range. The umbrella bottler's own archive establishes
  the continuous FEW batch sequence. The Unquenchables label illustration is
  not the producer's Series name; Batch 6 belongs to Cinema Series.

- [Single Cask Nation's archive](https://singlecasknation.com/pages/archive)

  FEW cask was found in that archive. It proves FEW is the distiller; The

- [Master of Malt's Heart Cut #5 entry](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/few-spirits/few-spirits-6-year-old-rye-2016-the-heart-cut-5-whiskey/?sku=135452)

- [Lost Lantern's 2024 Single Cask #3](https://www.lostlanternwhiskey.com/product/2024-few-bourbon-singlecask/)
  is five-year-old FEW Spirits Illinois Straight Bourbon, 62.3%, with natural

  [The producer's guest post](https://www.lostlanternwhiskey.com/few-spirits-guest-post-sydney-jones-on-distilling-in-the-midwest/)

  not evidence of another FEW release.

## Four Kings, 2014–2020

Four Kings is an official collaboration label for Corsair, FEW, Journeyman,
and Mississippi River Distilling Company. Attribute all four distillers. It
is not a FEW private pick or a separate independent bottler.

- [Time Out's April 2014 announcement](https://www.timeout.com/chicago/food-drink/4-midwest-distilleries-team-up-to-make-a-new-whiskey)
  documents the first bourbon and its outturn.
- [Drinkhacker's 2015 rye review](https://www.drinkhacker.com/2015/07/30/review-four-kings-rye-2015-craft-collaboration/)
  and [the contemporary launch report](https://whiskeyreviewer.com/2015/06/craft-whiskeys-four-kings-return-with-rye_060915/)
  establish the next collaboration. Sources disagree on 80 versus 101.4 proof;
  leave ABV unknown until an exact 2015 label resolves it.
- [Journeyman's 2016 announcement](https://www.journeyman.com/press/new-american-whiskeys-for-2016/)
  identifies the malt-whiskey collaboration. American malt whiskey alone does
  not prove an all-malted-barley recipe, so do not assume single or blended malt.
- [The Whiskey Reviewer's 2017 account](https://whiskeyreviewer.com/2016/02/qa-with-dave-sweet-whisky-live-usa-and-canada-022316/?random-post=1)
  describes the corn, rye, wheat, and malt blend at 101.4 proof. The search-index
  URL is a mismatched random-post redirect; its URL date is not evidence.
- [Mississippi River's March 2018 newsletter](https://myemail.constantcontact.com/MRDC-March-Newsletter.html?aid=hmxXLGPesYk&soid=1103863233642)
  is primary evidence for the bourbon's nine-month tequila-cask finish,
  101.4 proof, and March 22 launch.
- CaskCartel's [2019 rye](https://caskcartel.com/products/four-kings-2019-craft-collaboration-rye-whiske)
  has an exact label showing the brandy finish, 50.7%, and outturn. Its generic
  claim of 500 bottles every year is contradicted by this label and the first
  release's contemporary announcement; do not copy that claim.
- Its [2020 malt](https://caskcartel.com/products/four-kings-whiskey-collaboration-2020-finished-in-ex-ardbeg-barrels-craft-whiskey)
  label says Scotch-barrel finish and 50.7%, while the page says 50.5%. Use the
  label. [The 2020 Blendaggedon list](https://whiskymarketing.org/2020-blendaggedon-list/)
  independently corroborates the release. The exact Scotch distillery and
  outturn need stronger evidence.

## Remaining identity and evidence gaps

- Generic Single Barrel Bourbon and Single Barrel Rye listings still lack
  enough context to separate them from named private picks. The identified
  cask-strength releases above do not resolve every generic auction entry.
- [Spirit Radar's producer index](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/distilleries/few-spirits/)
  and collector search results are discovery leads, not a complete register.
  Unresolved bourbon casks are 16-0410, 16-0402, 16-0251, 16-1119, 16-0566,
  and 16-0565. Rye leads include 16-0358, 16-0112, 16-0109, and 16-1684.
  Exact-code searches, alternate punctuation, retailer and auction searches
  did not produce independently verifiable labels. Whiskybase was not scraped.
- De Wine Spot 17-1699, Binny's rye, the 2023 San Francisco Whisky Bourbon and
  Scotch Society bottled-in-bond pick, and Bounty Hunter's 60.4% bourbon remain
  leads. The index does not settle their exact identities or overlap with other
  selections. In particular, Binny's may overlap rye 16-1684. The Bounty Hunter
  code 13-382 remains collector-only evidence.
- A four-year-old 46.5% Straight Bourbon listing needs a readable age statement
  and evidence that it is a separate marketed expression. Searches mostly
  returned Bottled in Bond or the tea-proofed Smashing Pumpkins bourbon.
- Boutique-y Rye Batch 6 and Rye Port Finish Batch 5 follow the independent
  bottler rule: That Boutique-y Whisky Company is Brand and bottler, FEW Spirits
  is distiller, and Cinema Series and USA Series are the respective ranges.
  The producer pages explicitly identify those ranges; the film-inspired label
  on Batch 6 does not establish a separate Unquenchables Series. Immortal Rye
  belongs to FEW Brand, with FEW Spirits retained as its distiller.
- Existing import references remain attached to the same exact releases.
  Repeated brand names, automatic fact suffixes, and the Eight Immortals tea
  name must not become new public aliases or new releases.

## Access and images

Whisky Advocate sometimes required the trailing slash; Breaking Bourbon worked
with its `www` hostname. Clos des Millésimes needed a browser when text fetching
failed. BevNET's Immortal Rye page returned an access challenge; it was not
bypassed. The founder interview at Bevvy supplied the needed tea evidence.

The existing core, Cold Cut, American, Immortal, and Triple Smoke images were
visually checked against their products. Their stored source and license fields
do not establish provenance. No new image was uploaded or existing provenance
invented.

[Heaven Hill's terms](https://heavenhill.com/terms-and-conditions/) restrict
reproduction and commercial reuse; a public announcement is not an image
license. Whisky Advocate states all rights reserved. The Manual credits an
image used with permission, which does not transfer that permission to Peated.
No suitable reuse grant was found on the FEW product, Distiller, auction, or
retailer pages reviewed here. Seek producer permission or separately licensed
photographs before filling image gaps.

The Boutique-y archive, SMWS branches, Maverick Drinks, and auction sites expose
useful label images but did not provide a clear grant for storing them in
Peated. Maverick's downloadable assets still sit under all-rights-reserved
terms. Labels were inspected for evidence; those downloads were not uploaded.
CaskCartel's product-page image disclaimers mean each label must be checked
against the named year and release. Its text is not a reliable substitute for
the actual proof or outturn printed on a bottle.
