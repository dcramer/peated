# Glenlivet catalog sources

Research date: September 30, 2026. This pass covers The Glenlivet's own branded
releases, including historical, travel-retail, Japanese, and named single-cask
bottlings. Independently branded bottlings were used to check identity, not
treated as part of the official brand range. No source below proves a complete
release history. In particular, private selections, early export bottles, and
batch-specific Nàdurra releases remain incompletely documented.

## Producer sources

- [Global collection](https://www.theglenlivet.com/en/whisky-collection/): current
  core range, travel exclusives, Small Batch, Eternal Collection, and newer
  limited editions. Follow individual product pages for strength and maturation;
  the collection page alone does not distinguish older recipes.
- [Japanese archive](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/): retains discontinued
  Original Stories, Nàdurra, Code, Winchester, and Japan-only single casks that
  are absent from the global navigation. Japanese launch dates and allocations
  are not necessarily global release dates or total outturns.
- [Original Stories: Illicit Still](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-12is.html)
  and [Licensed Dram](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-12ld.html) establish
  distinct 12-year-old releases. An import name explicitly saying Illicit Still
  must not resolve to the ordinary 12-year-old.
- Japanese single-cask pages identify
  [2021 #16809](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-sc2021.html),
  [2022 #37819](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-sc2022_24.html),
  [2022 #41799](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-sc2022_26.html), and
  [2023 #18103](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-sc2023_20.html).
  The last page's HTML title incorrectly repeats the preceding release; its
  visible heading and article identify the 2023 release. The text does not give
  all strengths. Hospitality-only distribution still concerns labeled bottles
  offered for sale, unlike a visitor's hand-fill.
- [Code](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/le-code.html) gives the eventual
  maturation disclosure. The Nàdurra pages for
  [Oloroso](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/nd-o.html),
  [First Fill](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/nd-ffs.html), and
  [Peated](https://www.theglenlivet.jp/our-whisky/nd-pwcf.html) distinguish
  maturation and mention travel-market strengths. Do not copy one strength onto
  every batch.
- [30-year-old Cellar Collection](https://www.theglenlivet.com/en-gb/whisky/30-year-old-cellar-collection/)
  and [33-year-old Cellar Collection](https://www.theglenlivet.com/en-gb/whisky/33-year-old-cellar-collection/)
  are separate from the older vintage-led Cellar Collection releases. The older
  30-year-old bottled in 2001 is also a different strength from the newer one.
- [Pernod Ricard's brand page](https://www.pernod-ricard.com/en/brands/the-glenlivet)
  supports the distillery's founding year and current group affiliation. Its
  nutritional tables cover several strengths and cannot establish the strength
  of a particular old Bottle.

## Historical discovery and exact-release checks

[Whiskyfun's Glenlivet index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Glenlivet.html) separates
official releases from independent bottlings and links dated firsthand tastings.
It is useful for older export whisky, Nàdurra batch codes, Cellar Collection,
and named single casks. Approximate dates attached to ordinary core bottles are
often tasting-era estimates, not marketed editions. Sample codes are not cask
numbers. Open the linked review: an index can omit uncertainty expressed there.

[Rare Whisky 101's Glenlivet index](https://www.rarewhisky101.com/indices/distillery-specific-indices/glenlivet-index)
is a useful independent collector inventory for commemoratives, vintage
releases, and the alphabetical single-cask range. It provides bottling dates
and identifying facts but not a complete history. Several strengths differ from
other sources, so it should not silently override a readable label.

Useful checks beyond these indexes:

- [2012 single-cask announcement](https://www.scotchmaltwhisky.co.uk/glenlivetsinglecaskeditions.htm)
  covers Guardians, Helios, Josie, Inveravon, Kilimanjaro, and Legacy, with
  production details and allocations. It also names the earlier alphabetical
  releases. [Difford's Helios entry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2594/the-glenlivet-helios-single-cask-20-year-old)
  and [Legacy entry](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2597/the-glenlivet-legacy-single-cask-16-year-old)
  provide additional dated checks.
- Exact auction records distinguish
  [Eclipse #12549](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/3612/glenlivet-eclipse-cask-12549),
  [Gallow Hill #142608](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/144818/glenlivet-16-year-old-gallow-hill),
  [Quercus #9345](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/85-the-48th-auction/61020-glenlivet-quercus-single-cask-edition-17-year-old/),
  and [Castleton of Blairfindy #59245](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/8157634/glenlivet-18-year-old-single-cask-59245-castleton-blairfindy).
  Use the lot's facts and label, not boilerplate distillery history.
- [Quercus retailer announcement](https://www.whiskyintelligence.com/2013/04/the-glenlivet-quercus-is-now-in-stock-a-whisky-shop-exclusive-scotch-whisky-news/)
  distinguishes its launch from its earlier bottling date.
- [Minmore retailer selection](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/18156/glenlivet-18-year-old-minmore-single-cask-22378)
  and [Lost In Time selection](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/79725/glenlivet-1990-32-year-old-cask-27277-lost-in-time-series)
  identify official bottlings selected for a retailer. A retailer selection does
  not by itself make the retailer the bottler.
- [Cellar Collection 1980](https://www.diffordsguide.com/beer-wine-spirits/2044/the-glenlivet-cellar-collection-1980-bottled-2011)
  supplies the bottling year, strength, casks, and global outturn. The
  [1983 French Oak listing](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/15182/glenlivet-1983-bot2003-french-oak-finish-cellar-collection)
  and [exact auction lot](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/42092/glenlivet-1983-cellar-collection)
  establish bottling in 2003. They do not support a 37-year age statement.
- Contemporary Cigar Aficionado articles cover
  [1959](https://www.cigaraficionado.com/article/the-glenlivet-1959-limited-edition-2710)
  and [1964 at 44.7%](https://www.cigaraficionado.com/article/straight-from-the-barrel-the-glenlivet-cellar-collection-1964-limited-edition-1985).
  The latter's proof conversion is inconsistent; use the expressly reported
  ABV, corroborated by the matching Whisky Advocate review, not the conversion.
- [1969 Vintage auction](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/145-the-104th-auction/355547-glenlivet-1969-vintage/)
  and [1972 Vintage auction](https://www.skinnerinc.com/auctions/2904B/lots/333)
  identify 1998 bottlings. Do not confuse these with later Cellar Collection
  versions or treat their sample codes as single casks.
- [Alpha's contemporary review](https://scotchnoob.com/2013/06/05/the-glenlivet-alpha/)
  records the labeled strength and subsequent producer disclosure. It supports
  correcting an unsupported 50.9% value to 50%.

## Conflicts and open questions

- **Winchester 1967:** the Japanese producer archive reports 155 bottles; UK
  coverage and auction catalogs report 150. Leave total outturn unknown until
  the allocation difference is explained. Bottling in 2018 and release in 2019
  are different events.
- **Josie:** the 2012 announcement and collector index give 57.5%, whereas
  Whiskyfun reports 54.5% for the same named cask. Identity, age, and cask are
  supported; strength needs a readable label.
- **Travel-exclusive 16-year-old:** the current global producer page gives 40%,
  while the existing catalog has 43%. Establish whether there are two versions
  before changing that record or adding another.
- **Cellar 1959:** contemporary US coverage gives 42.8%; the collector index
  describes 42.19%. The documented 42.8% release is supportable. The other
  variant needs an exact label and bottling identity. Sources also disagree on
  the stated age, so do not calculate one from calendar years.
- **Cellar 1969 and 1973:** existing ages need stronger matching evidence.
  The 1969 release appears at 50.8% and 50.7% in different bottlings. The 1973
  has conflicting outturn claims. Generic vintage references cannot choose a
  variant. The pre-release 1973 Whiskyfun review itself cautions about its
  sample's strength.
- **Nàdurra:** generic 16-year-old, First Fill, Oloroso, Peated/Islay, and Triumph
  records do not reliably identify batches. The indexes expose 0407C, 1007D,
  0911P, FF0714, FF0117, OL0314, OL0614, OL0818, 0809A, and 0310B, among others.
  Confirm label spelling of O/0 in codes and reconcile existing imports before
  creating batch records. The existing Triumph age is unsupported by the
  reviewed material; its exact batch remains unknown.
- **Generic single-cask ages:** a bare 14-, 15-, or 16-year-old title cannot
  identify a named cask. Existing image provenance suggests Conglass and Valiant
  for two records, but images alone do not settle all linked imports. The
  unspecified 16-year-old lacks a reliable cask identity.
- **Early releases:** Atlantic, Blairfindy, and Cairn Daimh are named in the
  2012 announcement. Exact auction searches resolve
  [Atlantic #5318](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/164-the-122nd-auction/482988-glenlivet-40-year-old-single-cask-atlantic/)
  and [Carn Daimh #2860](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/glenlivet-20-year-old-single-cask-2860-carn-daimh).
  Searching the announcement's spelling "Cairn Daimh" mainly found walking
  routes; "Carn Daimh" found the Bottle. Searches for the earlier Blairfindy
  mostly found the later Castleton of Blairfindy and unrelated Glenfarclas
  bottlings, so its exact identity remains open. The collector/tasting indexes
  also expose the 150th Anniversary,
  Silver Jubilee Reserve, Queen Mother's 80th birthday, early Baretto exports,
  and Peter Dominic selections. Resolve printed names, proof conventions, and
  whether each was a marketed release before writing those records.
- **Additional selections:** Navigate World Whisky #9477, Jack Tar/LMDW China
  #9480, Or Sileis #16611, and other named casks appearing only in discovery
  indexes still need exact source and label checks. Chivas Brothers Cask
  Strength and Distillery Reserve bottles require the owner-collection Brand
  rule, rather than an invented Glenlivet Series.
- **Existing modern entries:** Small Batch 2.3 versus Orchard Fruitiness,
  Groundbreaker chapter naming, Jamaica and Provence titles, and First Fill
  American Oak selections need exact-label reconciliation before renaming.
  Generic Archive 21-year-old records need batch checks before merging.
- **Excluded from the official-brand backfill:** independent bottlers' releases,
  the visitor hand-filled batches, cask samples, and individual Spectra tasting
  components without separate sale evidence. Existing records were retained;
  a scope decision is not permission to delete them. Independents incorrectly
  assigned to the producer Brand need separately approved Brand corrections.

## Images and access

The producer's [terms](https://www.theglenlivet.com/en/terms-and-conditions/)
reserve reproduction rights. Product pages can establish facts without granting
permission to store their photographs. Retailer and auction images also lack a
verified reuse grant in this pass. New Bottles therefore remain without images;
existing image licenses require a separate rights check. The Cognac Cask
Selection image provenance points to a travel-exclusive 14-year-old and needs
replacement with a licensed exact image.

The existing distillery photograph by Kkonstan is documented on
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Glenlivet_distillery.jpg)
under CC BY 3.0, with the photographer already credited in Peated.

Direct fetches intermittently failed for Japanese archive pages, Cigar
Aficionado, The Whisky World, and some contemporary news pages. Browser access
worked for the Japanese 26-year-old single cask and the 1964 article. Search
snippets are discovery aids, especially when retailer copy contains copied
facts or inconsistent dates. The global producer range, Japan archive, collector
index, and tasting index should be checked together on a future pass.
