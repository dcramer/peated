# Sources for auction pilot bottle identities

This guide covers three releases inspected during the first Scotch Whisky
Auctions matching pilot. Read the source label alongside its description.
A saved model transcription can be wrong even when the catalog photo is right.

## Whisky Sponge SideBurn Edition No 95

[Decadent Drinks' original product page](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/whisky-sponge-ed95-sideburn-31-years-old)
identifies Edition No. 95 as a 31-year-old blended malt, made by marrying a
1992 second-fill barrel and a 1993 first-fill barrel. Its product gallery and
the existing catalog label distinguish this release from other SideBurn bottles.
The label prints 404 bottles and 51.4% ABV.

The [later Decadent Drams SideBurn](https://decadent-drinks.com/shop/decadent-drams-sideburn-31-years-old)
is a different release. Do not transfer its details to Whisky Sponge Edition
No. 95 merely because both names contain SideBurn and the same age.

The [September 2026 auction occurrence](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/232-the-183rd-auction/897061-sideburn-31-year-old-blended-malt-whisky-sponge-edition-no95/)
supplies the source title and sale result. The producer page establishes the
category; it is not a single malt. The inspected catalog photo is correct.

## Ardbeg Fèis Ìle 2024 cask 3771

The [older Scotch Whisky Auctions occurrence](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/205-the-157th-auction/751232-ardbeg-2009-14-year-old-single-cask-3771-feis-ile-2024/)
records a 2009 distillation, October 2023 bottling, second-fill Pedro Ximénez
sherry butt, cask 3771, 59% ABV, and 633 bottles. The
[Whiskyfun release review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2024/Ardbeg-from-2009-to-1989.html)
independently corroborates the cask, strength, and outturn for Fèis Ìle 2024.

The catalog photo shows the same cask and bottle count. Its handwritten label
can be misread by a model. An apparent transcription conflict is a reason to
inspect the image, not proof that the image or stored cask details are wrong.

## Aberlour 12 year old Sherry Cask

The [September 2026 auction occurrence](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/232-the-183rd-auction/898330-aberlour-12-year-old-sherry-cask-1l/)
has the producer's label and describes full sherry-cask maturation at 40% ABV
in a one-litre bottle. It explicitly distinguishes this from the standard
12-year-old release.

[Whisky Auctioneer's release catalog](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/aberlour-12-year-old-sherry-cask-matured-litre)
classifies this discontinued release as a distillery bottling, with Aberlour
as both Brand and distillery. This supports removing an independent-bottler
relationship. It does not justify applying the litre release's strength to
every older record with a similar name. Aberlour's current producer range lists
Double Cask; that page alone cannot establish this discontinued release.

## Access and photo reuse

The [September 2026 Ardbeg occurrence](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/232-the-183rd-auction/893368-ardbeg-2009-14-year-old-single-cask-3771-feis-ile-2024/)
and older occurrence were inspected on October 1, 2026. Their lot IDs differ
(`893368` and `751232`), as do their individual bottle numbers (`573 / 633`
and `596 / 633`). Both publish cask 3771 and 59% ABV in factual paragraphs.
Neither inspected page exposed a reusable bottle identifier in its inputs,
data attributes, or embedded scripts. The performance graph contains auction
numbers and prices, not a product ID. This is evidence about these pages, not
proof that the auction house has no internal bottle catalog. Do not create a
product mapping from a lot ID or graph values.

Scotch Whisky Auctions requires an age confirmation in the browser. Keep
requests at least 30 seconds apart and within the collector's 120-per-hour
limit. Its index pages already include photo links, so retaining those links
does not need a separate detail-page crawl.

No photo reuse license was established for these source galleries. Use them
to verify facts and retain attributed source links; do not copy their photos
into the public catalog without a separate reuse basis. No catalog image was
replaced during this research.
