# Glen Ord pass

Date: 2026-10-19, recorded 2026-10-22

## What Happened

A Glen Ord catalog pass ran in production on October 19, 2026, from 16:32 to
19:45 UTC, without a research record. The Bottle change history
(`GET /v1/changes?type=bottle`) is the only account of it. The pass:

- renamed independent Glen Ord and Ord bottlings from names such as
  `Glen Ord 2011 8-year-old - #800313` and `Ord 1962 20-year-old` to a bare
  `Glen Ord` or `Ord`, and moved cask markers out of the name;
- changed editions, cask numbers, Series, and release dates on many of the
  same Bottles;
- created a few Bottles, among them Singleton of Glen Ord Hand Filled
  Distillery Exclusive casks.

The sources it used were not recorded. The
[Bottle naming repair](./2026-10-22-bottle-naming-repair.md) restored the
vintage, and the age where a same-vintage sibling exists, using the names from
before this pass.

## Sources

- [Whiskyfun's Glen Ord index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Glen-Ord.html) groups
  reviews by bottler and covers about 2004 to 2026. Header lines give age,
  vintage and bottling year, bottler, strength, cask type, and often the cask
  number and bottle count. It includes older labels under other names:
  `Glenordie 12 yo (40%, OB, 1980's)` and Cadenhead `Ord 20 yo 1962/1983`
  "dumpy" bottlings.

## Names To Search

Glen Ord releases appear as Glen Ord, Ord, Glenordie, and The Singleton of
Glen Ord. Older Cadenhead and other independent labels used `Ord`. Keep the
name the label prints; do not change `Ord` to `Glen Ord`.
