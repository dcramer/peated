# Jack Daniel's — September 6, 2026

The production review started with 48 Brand-attached Bottles and finished with 155. It created 107 exact Bottle records and ten Series, corrected the distillery
or bottler relationship on 34 pre-existing Bottles, assigned 20 pre-existing
releases to documented Series, and assigned 39 exact or safely normalized Bottle
References. It did not merge or delete records. The complete create manifest,
including the exact evidence page and stored facts for every new Bottle, is in
[Jack Daniel's catalog evidence](./evidence.json).

- The producer's [sitemap](https://www.jackdaniels.com/sitemap.xml),
  [current product pages](https://www.jackdaniels.com/whiskey), and
  [press-room product list](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/products/) exposed
  both current releases and preserved pages for historical decanters,
  commemorative bottles, Gold Medal releases, Holiday Select, Before & After,
  the saloon bottles, and other discontinued releases. The default English
  pages were used; duplicated localized paths were not treated as separate
  Bottles.
- Official announcements established the Aged Series: the
  [2021 10-year launch](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-launches-first-age-stated-whiskey-in-over-100-years/),
  [2023 10- and 12-year release](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-introduces-new-12-year-old-tennessee-whiskey-in-its-annual-aged-series-release/),
  [2024 batches](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-launches-new-batches-of-12-year-old-and-10-year-old-tennessee-whiskey/),
  and [2025 10-, 12-, and 14-year release](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-introduces-14-year-old-tennessee-whiskey-for-the-first-time-in-over-a-century/).
  The producer's current [14-year page](https://www.jackdaniels.com/whiskey/14-year-old-tennessee-whiskey)
  and a syndicated [2026 release announcement](https://www.breakingbourbon.com/bourbon-whiskey-press-releases/jack-daniels-introduces-new-batches-of-award-winning-aged-series-tennessee-whiskeys)
  established the 2026 batches. Series `S0688` contains 11 exact releases,
  including the pre-existing 2026 10-year Batch 5.
- The collector's [complete Bottle index](https://jackdanielsbottles.com/bottles/)
  and [Series index](https://jackdanielsbottles.com/series/) supplied a bounded
  chronology for releases that the current producer site omits. Exact child
  pages supplied the release year and marketed name. This completed the renamed
  Tennessee Tasters/Distillery Series selections 1–17 (`S0685`, 17 records),
  Scenes from Lynchburg bottles 1–12 (`S0689`), and the annual
  [Ducks Unlimited releases](https://jackdanielsbottles.com/bottle/ducks-unlimited-series/)
  from 2006 through 2017 (`S0701`). The separate
  [Eric Church Single Barrel page](https://jackdanielsbottles.com/bottle/eric-church-single-barrel/)
  established the 2020 94-proof release, now `B54392`.
- A second exact-name and Series reconciliation corrected 23 pre-existing
  records. It restored Distillery Series #16 (`B43936`) to Tennessee rye with
  its 2025 date and 111.4 proof, restored the word `Straight` to selections
  8–12 where marketed, normalized 10-year Batch 5 (`B46425`), completed the
  producer-defined Bonded (`S0348`, three records), Legacy (`S0122`, three
  records), and Single Barrel (`S0019`, seven records) ranges, and left the
  conflicting 91/92-proof Barrel Reunion #2 value unknown.
- Exact collector pages also established six previously absent marketed
  releases: Gentleman Jack Commemorative Bottle (`B54450`), Gentleman Jack
  Timepiece (`B54453`), the Paula Scher Label (`B54454`), and the Japan, UK,
  and Singapore Master Distiller Bottles (`B54455`–`B54457`). The three
  country releases belong to the separately documented International Master
  Distiller Bottles Series `S0709`.
- Official pages and announcements established the 2022 American Single Malt,
  2023 travel-retail American Single Malt, 2023–2025 McLaren labels,
  [2026 Halo MK1](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-debuts-design-forward-halo-mk1-tennessee-whiskey-with-mclaren-mastercard-formula-1-team/),
  and the permanent
  [2025 Single Barrel Heritage Barrel](https://pressroom.jackdaniels.com/jack-daniels-single-barrel-heritage-barrel-tennessee-whiskey-launches-as-permanent-expression/).
  Whisky Auctioneer's [Jack Daniel's Brand guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/brands/jack-daniels)
  and exact Bottle pages were used as independent checks and for the Old No. 7
  Green Label and 2026 Boiler Hill #2 leads.
- Production Series created in this review are `S0685` Distillery Series,
  `S0686` Holiday Select, `S0687` Gold Medal Series, `S0688` Aged Series,
  `S0689` Scenes from Lynchburg, `S0690` Saloon Bottles, `S0691` Before &
  After, `S0692` Single Barrel Special Release, `S0701` Ducks Unlimited, and
  `S0709` International Master Distiller Bottles. Final verification fetched
  both Brand inventory pages: all 155 Bottles have
  Brand `E1079`, distiller `E366689`, and no explicit bottler.
- The Bottle Reference search returned 116 strings. Ninety-nine are assigned
  after this review. The 17 left unresolved are batchless aged releases,
  multi-Bottle sets and a calendar, retailer-pick names without enough cask
  identity, undated Holiday Select, Legacy, McLaren, or Twice Barreled names, a
  non-whisky bitters product, and a Sinatra Select string with two possible
  production targets. These were not guessed or ignored.
- Generic pre-existing records `B12899`, `B24679`, and `B15896` may overlap
  exact Aged or 150th Anniversary releases, and `B16740` and `B44146` may be
  duplicate Sinatra Select records. No merge was made because the generic
  records do not identify a single exact release or separate approval would be
  required.
- Twenty-six Bottle records had an existing image and 129 did not. No existing
  image records both a source page and license. Producer, collector,
  retailer, and auction images were useful identity evidence but did not state
  reusable rights, so no image was copied. Entity `E1079` also has no image; no
  unlicensed logo or product art was added.
