# Longmorn catalog sources

Research date: 22 October 2026. Scope: Longmorn official releases, independent
bottlings, historical Longmorn-Glenlivet labels, and discontinued releases from
UK, continental European, US, and Japanese markets. These sources do not list
every private cask ever bottled.

## Official releases

- [Longmorn collection](https://www.longmorn.com/en/the-collection/): current
  range, with product pages such as
  [18 Year Old](https://www.longmorn.com/en/the-collection/18-year-old/) and
  [22 Year Old](https://www.longmorn.com/en/the-collection/22-year-old/). Older
  product pages sit under `/en/product/<age>-year-old/`.
- Launch news in The Spirits Business:
  [The Distiller's Choice, no age, January 2016](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2016/01/longmorn-introduces-nas-expression/);
  [16 Year Old replaced by 23 Year Old, October 2016](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2016/10/longmorn-replaces-16yo-launches-23yo/);
  [18 and 22 Year Old, February 2024](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2024/02/longmorn-launches-18yo-and-22yo-single-malts/).
- [Chivas Brothers, November 2024](https://www.chivasbrothers.com/2024/11/longmorn-introduces-its-ultimate-expression-and-oldest-single-malt-to-date/):
  the oldest official release.
- Secret Speyside Collection, 2019 (18, 23, and 25 years):
  [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/spirits/2019/chivas-brothers-releases-secret-speyside-collection/)
  and [Pernod Ricard](https://www.pernod-ricard.com/en/brands/secret-speyside).
- Chivas Brothers Cask Strength Edition 17-year-old 50 cl bottles from the
  1986, 1987, and 1991 vintages: exact lots at Scotch Whisky Auctions and The
  Grand Whisky Auction. Batch codes such as `LM17 003` identify the release.
- Old official 10-, 12-, and 15-year-old bottlings for France, Italy (Claretta
  di V. Rosignano), and the US (Hill Thomson & Co.) appear as Whisky Auctioneer
  and Whisky.Auction lots. Hill Thomson's pre-1940 US label gives 91.4 US
  proof, which is 45.7% ABV.

Official no-age and age-only names are a trap. The official 15-year-old exists
at 43% and 45%, and the 23-year-old has more than one launch. A record that
gives only the age cannot be tied to one of them.

## Indexes

- [Whiskyfun Longmorn index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Longmorn.html): about
  285 entries with tasting dates, strengths, and bottlers; the main historical
  cross-check. Entries are leads. One is a 1897 cask sample drawn in 1905,
  not a bottled release.
- [Malt Maniacs Monitor](https://maltmaniacs.com/) bottle pages
  (`bottle.jsp?bid=…`): extra older releases; some ages differ from labels.
- Whisky Monitor NL ([distillery list](https://www.whiskymonitor.nl/huubdist.htm)):
  Dutch tasting records.
- Whiskybase pages were not collected.

## Who bottled what

- Premier Malts was bottled by Malcolm Pride; Jack Wiebers was only the German
  distributor. A bottle number on a Premier Malts label is not the outturn.
- Duthie packed and shipped bottles selected by others: Samaroli (1964/1983,
  "selected and imported by Samaroli") and Corti Brothers (1964, 21 years,
  selected by Darrell Corti; see
  [The Spirits Business, 2018](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2018/01/rare-corti-brothers-scotch-collection-goes-under-hammer/)).
- Captain Burn's is a Ryst-Dupeyron brand
  ([Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/companies/ryst-dupeyron)).
- Montgomerie's belonged to Angus Dundee and ran from 2000 to 2017
  ([Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/brands/montgomeries)).
- The Gillies Club Australia began in February 1977
  ([club site](https://thegilliesclub.com/)).
- Cadenhead "Dumpy" is a bottle shape, not a range. G&M licensed bottlings
  print "Pure Malt" or "Finest Highland Malt"; that wording does not make a
  separate release.
- Scott's Selection lot pages give 1996 as the brand's founding year, not a
  bottling year.

## Traps in old Peated records

- Several sparse imported records store an age equal to 2020 minus the
  vintage (for example 30 years on a 1990 vintage). Treat those ages as
  unverified.
- Do not calculate an age from vintage and bottling year when the label states
  none, and do not store an approximate decade ("1980s", "+/- 1970") as a
  bottling year.

auction or retailer record.

- **SMWS 7.56 "Lip-smackingly gorgeous ballet dancer":** 1984, 25 years,
  refill hogshead per a
