# Kilchoman catalog sources

Research scope: the full Kilchoman back catalog, including publicly sold
single casks, retailer selections, regional batches, Club releases, and
independent bottlings. Research date: 20 September 2026.

## Where to look

- [Kilchoman's whisky archive](https://www.kilchomandistillery.com/xml-file/)
  links current and retired product pages. It is useful for the annual 100%
  Islay and Loch Gorm releases, special cask maturations, and recent ranges.
  It does not inventory every single cask.
- [Kilchoman's news archive](https://www.kilchomandistillery.com/distillery-news/)
  has dated launch announcements and older Club announcements across its
  paginated archive. Read the announcement and label together: some product
  pages have been reused for later editions.
- [Kilchomania's general and small-batch index](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/kilchoman-general-and-small-batch-releases/)
  covers the first releases from 2009 through 2026, regional batches, Comraich,
  Club, festival, and other limited releases. Its links often lead to exact
  label photographs, bottling dates, distribution details, and bottle counts.
- [Kilchomania's casks filled in 2005–2010](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/kilchoman-single-cask-releases-casks-filled-from-2005-2010/)
  and [casks filled in 2011–2020](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/kilchoman-single-cask-releases-casks-filled-from-2011-2020/)
  are the broadest inventories found. The years describe cask filling, not
  release. The older URL ending in `2011-2019` returns 404. Follow the exact
  release pages; the tables contain errors and entries that are not separate
  marketed bottles.
- [Kilchomania's independent bottlings and blends](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/kilchoman-independent-releases-and-blended-malts/)
  provides a separate entrance for releases outside the official Brand.
  Confirm the marketed Brand and bottler independently before assigning them.
- [The Family Collection announcement](https://www.kilchomandistillery.com/distillery-news/kilchoman-family-collection-first-edition/)
  establishes the producer's annual collection beginning in July 2026. It is
  distinct from earlier regional Wills Family Cask Collection selections.

These sources provide broad coverage, not proof that every privately arranged
release is documented. Exact collector pages were fetched sequentially with
pauses; reuse cached pages instead of repeatedly downloading the archive.
Whiskybase was not automatically collected.

## Identity and source traps

- A row marked “Part of” identifies a component of a vatting. Find the
  separately marketed vatting instead of creating a Bottle for each cask.
- Kilchoman's early New Spirit and Anticipation releases were under three
  years old and are outside the whisky catalog.
- Private cask ownership does not establish public sale. Lord Margadale's
  cask 03/2005 explicitly never reached the market. Conversely, cask 42/2006,
  labelled for the Friday Morning Single Malt Club, was sold by The Whisky
  Exchange in summer 2017. Check each private cask's distribution history.
- The individual charity auction bottles from casks 01/2005 and 07/2005
  must be distinguished from gifts and tasting samples. In particular, the
  10cl cask 07/2005 gala-dinner gift is not the 2024 charity auction bottle.
- Split sherry casks can have multiple market labels for the same liquid.
  Cask 316/2006 was bottled on the same day at the same strength for Denmark
  and Whisky Live Japan. Cask 317/2006 was bottled a month apart, on either
  side of its fifth birthday. Compare bottling dates, age, and strength before
  treating country labels as separate releases.
- The age in an index is sometimes a calculated maturation age, not a printed
  age statement. Do not manufacture an age-bearing name from that column.
- Harvest year is not distillation year. This matters especially for 100%
  Islay releases.
- Bottling dates are not release dates. Madeira's 2021 Edition was bottled in
  August 2021 and released in March 2022. Distillery-shop cask 131/2006 was
  bottled in November 2017 and sold in June 2018.
- Regional Small Batch labels commonly print the country, “Small Batch,” and
  a numbered batch. Preserve those names. Importer selection programs belong
  in the edition unless the producer itself markets them as a range.

## Conflicts to preserve

- The producer's 100% Islay 12th Edition product page contains text for the
  13th Edition. Use the dated 12th Edition announcement for its composition.
- Loch Gorm 2023 has conflicting minimum ages in producer and collector text;
  leave the disputed age unknown.
- Original Cask Strength Quarter Cask has conflicting filling-year and age
  details between the producer, index, and collector detail. It needs a label
  check before those fields change.
- The Club 12th Edition collector page gives 57.5% and 522 bottles. The
  [producer's label graphic](https://www.kilchomandistillery.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Newsletter_Club23-02-3-1000x1024.png)
  clearly gives **54.1% and 515 bottles**. Prefer the label.
- Club 5th and 9th Edition outturns conflict with approximate producer counts;
  do not turn an approximation into an exact outturn.
- Cask 244/2006 has a printed filling-year typo. The collector explains that
  2007 should be 2006. Keep the correction with its evidence.
- The distillery-shop cask 12/2007 has a printed age of 10 despite dates
  indicating 11 years, and disputed 54.7% versus 57.4% strength. Those facts
  need separate resolution; do not silently use the index values.
- Several regional batches have explicitly corrected percentages or outturns
  on their detail pages. Belgium/Luxembourg No. 3 prints percentages totalling
  95%; USA No. 8, Total Wine Concierge No. 2, and the Spec's Triskele batches
  have disputed printed counts. Avoid copying the uncorrected number.

## Images

Exact bottle photographs are widely available in the producer and collector
archives. Availability does not establish permission to store them.
[Kilchoman's terms](https://www.kilchomandistillery.com/terms-condition/)
reserve copyright and do not provide a general reuse license. No general
reuse permission was established for Kilchomania's images, including images
credited to other photographers. Keep existing images unless an exact,
permitted replacement is established.

A possible separately licensed distillery photograph is
[Kilchoman Distillery on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kilchoman_Distillery_-_geograph.org.uk_-_5437444.jpg).
Check its current attribution, license, and exact site before using it for the
Entity; it is not a Bottle image.

## Remaining research questions

The oldest generic records named only by vintage and “Single Cask” or
“Private Cask” cannot identify one cask from the name alone. Follow their
original import references and images before assigning a customer or cask.
Private owners, public sale evidence, source pages describing several bottles,
independent Brand attribution, and duplicate market labels require individual
review. The local working inventory records these questions per release;
this source guide does not assert that the complete backfill is finished.

## Additional branches of the archive

Follow both the Machir Bay tour overview and collaborative-vatting overview
linked from the general index. They lead to many releases absent from the
main table. Retailers, bars, clubs, and festivals commissioned these vattings
from 2015 onward. Similar bourbon/sherry proportions or bottling codes alone
do not establish that two commissioned vattings contain the same liquid.
One link for The World of Single Malt's 2020 selection incorrectly leads to
an unrelated single cask; that selection still needs an exact source.

The [MacBeatha series](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/macbeatha-series/)
contains four early house bottlings from other Islay distilleries, released
while Kilchoman's own whisky was maturing. Keep MacBeatha as the marketed
Brand, Kilchoman as bottler, and Bowmore, Bruichladdich, or Caol Ila as the
actual distiller. The tube repeats the first edition's strength; later
bottle labels identify their own strengths. Do not infer stated age from
vintage and bottling years.

The independent-release index also includes collaborative blended malts.
[Journey's Blend](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/journeys-blend-triumph-royal-enfield-2/),
[Spirit of Unity](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/39667/spirit-of-unity),
[Big Peat Christmas 2016](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/36131/big-peat-blended-malt-christmas-edition-2016),
[Great Islay Swim](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/explorers-cask-great-islay-swim-single-cask-nation-2/),
and [KilchArd](https://www.kilchomania.com/en/kilchard-two-distilleries-two-casks-2/)
identify Kilchoman among their contributing distilleries. These are not
Kilchoman-branded single malts. Component ages, filling dates, and peat levels
do not become facts about the entire blend. Glasgow Islay Association's
anniversary blend and Glenalmond College's anniversary blend still need
clearer marketed Brand attribution. The Glenalmond whisky brand is not
evidence of a relationship with Glenalmond College.

## Further checks and limits

- [La Famiglia's retailer listing](https://www.tastefive.de/product-page/kilchoman)
  supports 51.0% for cask 92/2006, rather than the index's 51.1%.
- [Whisky.com's An Geamhradh entry](https://www.whisky.com/whisky-database/details/kilchoman-uniquely-islay-series-an-geamhradh-unpeated.html)
  supports cask 552/2012; the collector detail's 552/2013 is inconsistent
  with its filling date and title.
- The German Tour and Whisky Live Paris labels for cask 332/2006 have
  conflicting strengths. Keep one split-cask release and leave strength
  unknown until the discrepancy is resolved.
- Do not use one market's allocation as a whole-cask outturn. This affects
  cask 447/2007's distillery-shop and Spiriteca labels and cask 483/2014's
  La Maison du Whisky and Croatia labels.
- Some 100% Islay single-cask pages repeat a 50 ppm value that belongs to
  purchased malt. Withhold that figure without evidence for the exact malt.
- The Comraich bar range needs evidence of separately sold labelled bottles
  or samples. Service by the glass and samples supplied only as parts of a
  tasting set do not establish separate marketed releases.
- A festival hand-fill announcement is not proof of the later shop release.
  In particular, cask 1216/2018's remaining stock was announced for later
  sale, but that sale was not established during this review.
- Searches by generic vintage, age, and “Private Cask” returned multiple
  possible casks rather than unique identities. Import references repeating
  those same vague names did not resolve the oldest generic records.
- Spirit of Unity is listed at 40% on Difford's Guide, but exact auction
  records and the collector label identify 46%; prefer the exact bottling
  evidence. Broad search results can help find a release but should not
  replace its label or exact source page.

This review did not establish that every private cask became a public
release. Some source entries describe gifts, retained allocations, stolen
consignments, or plans that had not yet become verified releases. Keep those
questions explicit instead of turning cask ownership into a Bottle record.

The [Whisky.dk Kilchoman listings](https://eng.whisky.dk/shop/kilchoman-whisky-98c1.html)
provide public sale evidence for Kolding Whiskylaug's private casks 102/2007
and 130/2007. Their collector pages alone describe members' allocations.
This is a useful example of why “private cask” should prompt a distribution
check rather than automatic exclusion. The disputed August day does not
change their supported 2017 bottling year.

## Duplicate verification follow-up

A generic “Red Wine Cask Matured” name does not by itself identify the 2017
edition. Its saved import reference repeats the generic name and no assigned
external review resolves the date. Keep that identity separate until an
original label, dated source, or stronger import evidence establishes it.

Existing retailer images also need a fresh visual check before a merge. An
older Spring record displayed a Spring 2010 label despite its 2011 import
references; an older Loch Gorm record displayed the 2026 label despite its
2019 reference. Do not transfer those images to the verified earlier releases.
The Autumn 2009 and Summer 2010 images matched their named editions.

[The Whisky Exchange's Kh1 listing](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/15201/kh1-elements-of-islay)
and [Abbey Whisky's listing](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/products/kh1-elements-of-islay-kilchoman)
identify the marketed Elements of Islay release at 59.7%. The retailer name
is not its Brand. Use the printed “Kh1” expression, Kilchoman as distiller,
and the independent Elements of Islay label as Brand and bottler.
