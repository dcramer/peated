# Laphroaig 15-year-old Unblended — October 25, 2026

Where to find facts about the 1980s Laphroaig 15-year-old bottled under the
`Unblended Islay Malt Scotch Whisky` label, and how to tell it from later
bottlings of the same expression. The identity rule this follows is in
"Who Is The Brand, Series, And Bottler" in
`docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`, next to "A different package does
not make a different Bottle."

## Where the facts live

- The label itself is the best source. The 1980s label reads
  `Unblended Islay Malt Scotch Whisky`, `15 years old`, 75cl, and states the
  strength. Later labels read `Single Islay Malt` and are 70cl.
- [Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/lot/6130541/laphroaig-15-year-old-1980s-cinzano-import)
  titles the Italian bottle `Laphroaig 15 Year Old 1980s / Cinzano Import`. Its
  lot text says the 15 launched in 1985 and was replaced by the 18 in 2009.
  That date is for the expression, not for any one bottle.
- [Rhum & Whisky](https://rhumandwhisky.com/eng/product/laphroaig-15-yo-1980s-cinzano-import-75cl-43)
  lists the Cinzano import as 1980s, 75cl, 43%.
- Whiskybase user lists show two `15-year-old Unblended Islay Malt Scotch
Whisky` entries at 750 ml: one at 43% and one at 40%. No Whiskybase page for
  the Cinzano import itself turned up in search.
- [The Whisky Vault](https://www.thewhiskyvault.com/laphroaig-15-year-old-unblended-uk-eighties-bottling-with-tin-5750-p.asp)
  and [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/19945/laphroaig-15-year-old-bot1980s)
  describe the UK-market bottle: 75cl, 40%, tin, and a large red `15` behind
  the label text.

## How to tell the bottlings apart

- Strength separates the UK bottle (40%) from the export bottle (43%), so they
  are separate Bottles.
- The importer strip (`Importato da Francesco Cinzano`) only marks the market.
  It goes in a Bottle reference, not the edition or the bottler.
- The 75cl size dates a bottle to before 1992 but does not split it by itself.

## Traps

- "1985" in auction and retailer text is the expression's launch year. Do not
  store it as the bottling or release year of a 1980s bottle.
- Rue Pinard's product pages and blog are sales copy. Their claim that the
  Cinzano bottle has a screw cap, unlike later bottlings, is unconfirmed.
- One auction listing spells the importer `Cinazno`.
- Retailer and auction photos of these bottles carry no reuse grant.

## Unknowns

- Whether other importers' strip labels exist for the 43% Unblended bottle.
- Whether the 40% UK bottle was also sold without the red `15`.
- The years each label was in use. No dated producer source was found.
- Which Peated record, if any, is the 1990s–2009 `Single Islay Malt` 70cl
  bottle. The record called "Original Release" carries a 1985 release year
  but shows the 70cl label.
