# Ledaig official back catalog

Research checked on 20 September 2026. Scope: the Ledaig brand's official
bottlings from Tobermory, including discontinued expressions and distillery
shop releases. The broader Ledaig back catalog also includes independent
bottlings, covered in the [companion source guide](2026-09-20-ledaig-independent.md).
An independent bottler recorded as bottler is not evidence that its release
belongs under the Ledaig Brand.

## Coverage and useful sources

No public source found claims to be a complete official release register.
The sources below cover the early vintage bottlings, the older standard
range, the 2010s cask finishes, and the current range. They do not establish
complete coverage of export-only releases or every distillery single cask.

- [Tobermory's Ledaig range](https://tobermorydistillery.com/collections/ledaig-whisky)
  and individual product pages are the strongest sources for current marketed
  names, strength and maturation. The site's product feed also retains some
  sold-out releases. It is not a historical register.
- [Whiskyfun's Ledaig index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Ledaig.html) reaches back
  to the older NAS and age-stated official bottlings and early vintages.
  Separate OB entries from independent bottlers. Approximate bottling dates
  in reviews are not exact release dates.
- [Malt Maniacs' Tobermory/Ledaig register](https://maltmaniacs.com/index.jsp?did=118&distilleryName=Ledaig+single+malt+scotch+distillery)
  provides leads for older strengths and vintage variants. Search snippets
  were accessible but direct retrieval failed. Asterisks and approximate
  dates need label confirmation; derived ages are not label age statements.
- [Whisky News' distillery page](https://www.whisky-news.com/En/distilleries/Tobermory_ledaig.html)
  is another historical entrance. It has only selected releases and contains
  at least one strength discrepancy for the 18-year batch range.
- [Alternative Whisky Academy](https://www.awa.dk/whisky/ledaig/index.htm)
  preserves an old retailer stock list. Its Light and Dark entries use ages
  that have not been confirmed on labels. Several remarks appear copied from
  unrelated entries, so use it for leads rather than final identity.

## Current range and named collections

Producer pages verify the
[10-year-old](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-10-year-old),
[18-year-old](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-18-year-old),
[Sinclair Series Rioja](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-sinclair-series-rioja-cask-finish),
[Castaway](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-castaway),
[Hebridean Moon](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-hebridean-moon),
and [Triple Wood](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-triple-wood).
Sinclair Series is a marketed range, not a bottler. Castaway combines rum and
tequila casks; Hebridean Moon uses second-fill bourbon casks. Do not reduce
these to the imported generic cask value “other.”

The producer also retains
[2008 Amarone](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-2008-amarone-cask-finish),
[2012 Bordeaux](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-2012-bordeaux-red-wine-cask-matured),
and [2012 Bourbon Matured](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/ledaig-2012-bourbon-matured).
The Bourbon Matured bottle is a labeled shop and online release; its single
cask wording does not make it an excluded visitor handfill.

[The Lores of Mull](https://tobermorydistillery.com/collections/the-lores-of-mull)
explicitly presents a distillery-exclusive series, including Ledaig's
[The Swordstone of Mull](https://tobermorydistillery.com/products/the-swordstone-of-mull).
The companion Blue Men release is Tobermory, outside this Brand scope. Both
come from the same distillery; this is not a multi-distillery independent
bottler collection.

## Historical labels and limited releases

Auction listings are useful for exact label identity, but their auction dates
must not become bottling or release dates. Use the actual label when a lot's
structured attributes conflict with its title.

- Whisky.Auction has examples of the older
  [10-year-old](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/191403/ledaig-10-year-old),
  [15-year-old](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/48108/ledaig-15-year-old),
  and [NAS peated bottling](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/157935/ledaig-single-malt).
  [Whisky Antique](https://www.whiskyantique.com/en/ledaig-island-scotch-whisky-7-year-old-70cl-43-cv5289)
  and Whiskyfun corroborate the old peated seven-year release.
- [The Whisky Shop's 1979 lot](https://www.whiskyshop.com/auctions/a81566-ledaig-1979)
  and [HTFW](https://www.htfw.com/ledaig-isle-of-mull-single-malt-1979-whisky)
  support the vintage bottling. Do not infer its age from an approximate
  decade of bottling. Another auction result titled 1979 had a contradictory
  structured vintage of 1974.
- [Christie's 1972 release](https://onlineonly.christies.com/s/christies-wine-online-nyc-collection-rare-whisky/ledaig-32-year-old-1972-93/25943)
  records the old Oloroso release and its stated age. Its listed distillation
  and bottling dates do not neatly reproduce that age; preserve the marketed
  age rather than replacing it with arithmetic.
- [Scotch Whisky Auctions' 1974 cask-strength lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/102-the-64th-auction/106562-ledaig-1974-cask-strength/)
  and [The Whisky Exchange's reduced-strength 1974](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/3045/ledaig-1974-18-year-old)
  establish distinct variants. The historical register also lists a later
  reduced-strength bottling.
- Fine Drams describes the
  [13-year Amontillado](https://www.finedrams.com/ledaig-13-year-old-amontillado-cask-finish-whisky.html),
  [13-year Port Pipe](https://www.finedrams.com/ledaig-13-year-old-2005-port-pipe-matured-whisky.html),
  [19-year Marsala](https://www.finedrams.com/ledaig-19-year-old-marsala-finish-whisky.html),
  and [18-year Batch 3](https://www.finedrams.com/ledaig-18-year-old-whisky-batch-3.html).
  [Caskers' Batch 2 page](https://www.caskers.com/ledaig-18y-year-old-batch-2-single-malt-scotch-whisky/)
  corroborates its strength. Batch numbers identify separate marketed
  editions, not separate Series.
- [Scotch Malt Whisky's 1996 article](https://www.scotchmaltwhisky.co.uk/ledaig1996.htm)
  is a contemporary source for the vintage Oloroso release. Retailers differ
  on its age and date; do not turn a review publication date into a release
  date or silently collapse the 19- and 20-year descriptions.
- The Whiskyphiles' contemporary articles distinguish the 1998
  [PX](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2018/10/22/ledaig-19-years-old-1998-px-cask-finish/)
  and [Oloroso](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2018/10/22/ledaig-19-years-old-1998-oloroso-sherry-cask-finish/)
  finishes. [The producer announcement reported by Scotch Malt Whisky](https://www.scotchmaltwhisky.co.uk/ledaig1998pxcaskfinish.htm)
  corroborates the PX identity. Its marketed age is 19, not the 22 in an old
  generated description. UK allocation is not global outturn.
- [Whisky Saga's 1998 Marsala review](https://www.whiskysaga.com/blog/ledaig-1998-21-yo-marsala-cask-finish)
  covers the later Marsala release.
  [The Spirits Business' 2020 collection announcement](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/2020/08/distell-unveils-eight-limited-edition-single-malts/)
  covers the 2007 PX alongside it, but appears to swap strengths between
  the older Ledaig and Tobermory. Use exact bottle evidence for those strengths.
- [Just Whisky's Moscatel lot](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/ledaig-20-years-old-distillery-exclusive-moscatel-cask-finish-1504444),
  [Scotch Whisky Auctions' Manzanilla lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/218-the-169th-auction/824457-ledaig-21-year-old-manzanilla-finish/),
  and [Ruby Port lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/204-the-156th-auction/746078-ledaig-1997-21-year-old-ruby-port-pipe-finish/)
  preserve exact distillery-exclusive identities and numbered bottle evidence.
- [Whisky.com's Rioja entry](https://www.whisky.com/whisky-database/details/ledaig-rioja-spanish-wine-cask-finish-distillery-exclusive.html),
  [Whisky Saga's Spanish Sherry review](https://www.whiskysaga.com/blog/ledaig-16-yo-spanish-sherry-cask-finish-distillery-exclusive),
  [Master of Malt's older Sherry exclusive](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/ledaig/ledaig-18-year-old-sherry-cask-finish-distillery-exclusive-whisky/),
  and [Scotch Whisky Auctions' Virgin Oak lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/145-the-104th-auction/350939-ledaig-16-year-old-virgin-oak-cask-finish/)
  cover the older shop-exclusive finishes.
- [The Grand Whisky Auction's 2009 Bordeaux lot](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/lot-290193/ledaig-10-year-old-2009-bordeaux-wine-cask/)
  distinguishes it from the later 2012 Bordeaux release.
- Existing stored label photographs corroborate the printed ages on the
  [Port Pipe](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/ledaig-11-years-old-port-pipe-cask-matured-1390226),
  [Ruby Port](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/tobermory/ledaig-21-year-old-1997-ruby-port-pipe-finish-whisky/?sku=142410),
  and [Double Cask](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/tobermory/ledaig-13-year-old-2009-double-cask-matured-whisky/?sku=132991)

- [The Whisky Shop's 1999 PX page](https://www.whiskyshop.com/ledaig-1999-pedro-ximenez-cask-matured)

  conflict; leave that fact unknown without exact label confirmation.
- [The Drinks Business' 2015 Dùsgadh announcement](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2015/05/ledaig-releases-scotch-rarity/?edition=asia)
  is a dated source for the oldest official release.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Sherry Finish lot](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/8184446/ledaig-sherry-finish)
  preserves the discontinued NAS sherry bottling. Direct page access was
  unreliable; its searchable listing corroborates Whiskyfun's historical entry.

## Open identity questions

- “09” has no exact source or reference identifying whether the number is an
  age, a vintage abbreviation, or incomplete imported text. Producer pages,
  Whiskyfun and historical catalog searches did not resolve it.
- The existing “1974 Vintage” cannot be matched to a specific strength and
  bottling year. No inspected source supports its stored 46-year age. Preserve
  its identity until label or original import evidence establishes the release.
- A bare “2008” does not distinguish the official Amarone finish from Marsala
  handfilled bottles. Avoid creating a possible duplicate of the Amarone until
  the existing record can be identified.
- The old plain “20-year-old” does not identify the 40% or 43% bottling.
  Historical registers list both; generated tasting prose resolves neither.
- A retailer lead for a seven-year 40% bottling still needs exact label
  confirmation. It must not be assumed to be the same release as the 43% bottle.
- The old Light and Dark stock-list entries describe four years of maturation,
  but do not establish a printed four-year age statement. They may be the
  existing NAS peated and sherry-finish products rather than additional releases.
- The 2008 Marsala casks sold as handfilled bottles need evidence that they
  were a marketed release rather than visitor-selected fills. Likewise, the
  [2004 Oloroso cask review](https://peatedperfection.blogspot.com/2019/11/ledaig-oloroso-cask-2004-whisky-review.html?m=0)
  explicitly reviews a distillery cask sample; that is not a marketed Bottle.
- The 13-year Port Pipe appears in more than one outturn. A generic expression
  record cannot be assigned one batch's total merely because the strength
  matches. [Diving for Pearls discusses the two releases](https://www.divingforpearlsblog.com/2020/08/ledaig-13-year-old-port-pipe-matured.html).
- Searches also surfaced 18-year Batch 1 and informal mentions of Batch 4.
  Exact official labels need confirmation; Living Souls' separately branded
  Batch 1 dominates search results and is not the official bottling.
- The import phrase “Ledaig 19-year-old” does not distinguish Marsala, Oloroso,
  or PX. It cannot support an automatic match to a specific cask finish.
  Likewise, generic imported ten-year names omit the historical strength,
  an eighteen-year name omits the batch or distillery-exclusive finish, and
  “1998 Cask Finish Limited Edition” omits which finish. Keep these ambiguous
  noncanonical references unresolved rather than assigning a specific Bottle.

## Access and images

Tobermory's HTML product pages and product feed were accessible. Direct web
retrieval failed intermittently for Whisky Antique, HTFW, Scotch Malt Whisky,
Whisky Auctioneer and some retailer archives; indexed results and independent
exact listings supplied corroboration. Diving for Pearls returned a rate-limit
error during a later request; do not repeatedly retry it. Whiskybase was not
scraped or used as the sole source of production facts.

The producer's [terms](https://tobermorydistillery.com/pages/terms-conditions)
reserve image rights and permit extracts for personal, non-commercial use;
that does not grant permission to republish bottle photographs in Peated.
Auction and retailer pages show useful label photos, but this review did not
establish a reuse license for Whisky.Auction, Scotch Whisky Auctions,
Whisky Auctioneer, The Whisky Shop, HTFW, Fine Drams, Whisky Antique, Christie's,
The Grand Whisky Auction, Just Whisky, Whisky.com, Caskers, or Master of Malt.
Existing images were preserved. Missing images need an exact licensed photo or
permission from the rights holder; product photography availability alone is
not permission.
