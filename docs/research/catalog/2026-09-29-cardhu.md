# Cardhu catalog sources

Research dates: September 29–30, 2026. Scope: marketed Cardhu-branded releases
and Diageo owner-collection releases that name Cardhu. Independent bottlings,
blends that merely contain Cardhu, visitor hand-fills, private-cask selections,
and staff-only bottlings are outside this pass.

## Producer sources

The [Malts sitemap](https://www.malts.com/en-gb/sitemap) is the best entrance to
Cardhu's current and retained producer pages. It exposes product pages that are
hard to reach from the current navigation, but it does not prove that the
historical catalog is complete.

## Broad historical indexes

[Whisky Monitor's Cardhu list](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/index.jsp?did=28&distilleryName=Cardhu+single+malt+scotch+distillery),
[Whisky-news](https://whisky-news.com/En/distilleries/Cardhu.html), and
[Whiskyfun's Cardhu index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Cardhu.html) were the main
historical cross-checks. Together they cover old core strengths, Rare Malts,
Special Releases, The Managers' Choice, and several Special Cask Reserve
batches. Each remains a secondary source and can contain approximate dates or
transcription errors.

Whiskybase searches were used only to find likely missing releases and exact
batch codes. Peated must not collect or copy Whiskybase automatically. Each
created historical release was checked against an exact label, auction,
retailer, menu, or contemporary review where possible.

## Historical core range and Pure Malt

- [Whisky Auctioneer's 5-year-old lot](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/8182580/cardhu-5-year-old-john-walker-sons)
  shows the historical five-year age and 40% strength.
- [An historical whisky list](https://ca1-boi.edcdn.com/menus/BCW-Whisky-List-140518.pdf)
  and exact label results establish an eight-year-old at 43%. Search results
  also distinguish old twelve-year-old bottlings at 40%, 43%, and
  [45%](https://lochsofwhisky.com/cardhu-12-year-old-1980s). The differing
  strengths are distinct Bottle identities; ordinary label changes at one
  strength are not.
- [The Guardian's contemporary report](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2003/dec/04/food.foodanddrink)
  describes the short-lived Cardhu Pure Malt controversy.
  [An exact auction lot](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/30-the-seventh-auction/1104-cardhu-12-pure-malt/)
  confirms the twelve-year age, 40% strength, and Pure Malt label. Sources name
  Cardhu and Glendullan as components but do not provide a complete distillery
  list, so a partial blend composition should not be stored as complete.

“The Cummings of Cardhu” and “Speyside” appear on historical presentation and
reference names for the standard eighteen-year-old. No source established a
different vatting, strength, cask, or outturn. These are label or package-name
variants of the same marketed release.

## Gold Reserve and Game of Thrones

[Diageo's Game of Thrones announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/game-of-thrones--inspired-whisky-is-here-in-celebration-of-the-hit-tv-series-300721878.html)
names the product “Game of Thrones House Targaryen – Cardhu Gold Reserve” at
40%. No liquid difference from Gold Reserve was established. Under the current
identity model, the House Targaryen presentation is collection packaging for
Gold Reserve, not a separate Bottle. Its producer and retailer names remain
useful exact references after consolidation.

## Anniversary wine-cask release

The later Malts anniversary article was published in August 2024, and several
retailers describe the bottle as a 2024 anniversary release. A
[contemporaneous French trade announcement](https://www.rayon-boissons.com/spiritueux/cardhu-devoile-une-edition-limitee-pour-son-200e-anniversaire)
was published October 11, 2023 and states that the bottle would be available
from November 2023. That dated availability evidence establishes November 2023
as the release month. Whiskyfun also describes its reviewed bottle as a 2023
bottling. The 2024 descriptions refer to the anniversary year, not first market
availability.

marketed batches. Store the complete printed `Cs/cR` batch code as the edition,
not as a cask number. Exact sources found these batches:

## Distillery and single-cask releases

- [Distillery Exclusive Batch 01](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/lot-281297/cardhu-distillery-exclusive-batch-1)
  establishes 48% and an outturn of 2,502. Other exact sources identify its
  2018 bottling and bourbon, sherry, and Californian red-wine cask mix.
- [Distillery Exclusive Batch 02](https://www.thegrandwhiskyauction.com/lot-491675/cardhu-distillery-exclusive-bottling-batch-02/)
  establishes 48%, an outturn of 4,002, and ex-bourbon and Californian
  red-wine casks. Exact label and catalog results place its bottling in 2023.
- [A Bonhams sale catalog](https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Art/Auction/Bonhams/2018/20181212%20S-24762-0-2%20Whisky%20Sale.pdf)
  identifies the earlier “Available only at the Distillery” limited edition at
  48% with an outturn of 1,800. It does not safely establish a release year.
- [The 1991 Select Cask 9001 release](https://gommonehk.com/products/cardhu-1991-2018-26-year-old-select-cask-9001)
  is a twenty-six-year-old single European-oak cask, bottled in 2018 at 54.5%
  with an outturn of 336.

Distillery-exclusive labeled products offered as finished bottles are marketed
releases. Visitor-filled hand-fill casks are not.

## Diageo owner collections

Rare Malts Selection, Special Releases, and The Managers' Choice are Diageo
collections spanning several distilleries. Under the current identity model,
each collection is the Brand, Cardhu is the Bottle name and distiller, the year
or release descriptor is the edition, and there is no Cardhu-specific Series.

For the 1982 twenty-two-year-old,
[Sotheby's exact lot](https://www.sothebys.com/buy/02d7a654-4c75-40de-a2a3-883178bb2cfb/lots/f13661e7-6e30-4d6a-be62-618e86314787)
identifies it as the 2005 Special Releases bottling at 57.8% and shows bottle
293 of 3,600. [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/85787/cardhu-1982-22-year-old)
corroborates the release identity. Whiskyfun lists 3,000 bottles; the readable
exact bottle numbering and a second exact retailer source support 3,600.

[Abbey Whisky's 2013 release page](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/products/cardhu-21-year-old-2013-release)
identifies the 1991 twenty-one-year-old at 54.2%, bottled in 2013 from
ex-bourbon American oak, with 6,000 bottles.

For the recent Special Releases, the producer pages establish the finishes and
cask-strength presentation. [The Whisky Exchange's exact 2021 page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/61860/cardhu-2006-14-year-old-special-releases-2021)
also supplies the 2006 vintage and refill-American-oak maturation followed by
an ex-red-wine-cask finish.

## Remaining identity questions and exclusions

A generic Cardhu “1997 vintage” record remains unresolved. Its accepted names
do not identify an ABV, age, cask, bottling year, or exact marketed release.
The 1997 vintage also appears in The Managers' Choice and private-cask
releases, so vintage alone cannot settle the identity.

The Manager's Dram fifteen-year-old was staff-only, not a public marketed
release. Visitor hand-filled Cardhu casks and private Casks of Distinction
selections are also outside this scope unless evidence shows that a stable
labeled release was offered publicly.

The 2019 Spirit of Speyside anniversary bottle is a multi-distillery blended
malt in which Cardhu is one component. It is not a Cardhu-branded release.
Independent-bottler Cardhu releases remain under their own Brands and were not
backfilled in this pass.

## Images and access

Producer, retailer, and auction pages supply exact Cardhu photographs, but no
blanket reuse permission was found. Public access is not permission for Peated
to copy an image. Existing stored images and provenance were retained during
duplicate consolidation, including the exact 1982 twenty-two-year-old image;
no new outside image was copied without established reuse terms.

Whisky Auctioneer, The Whisky Exchange, and Master of Malt can block automated
page readers or rate-limit requests. Their exact product pages remained useful
through browser-readable pages and search results. Whiskybase was treated as a
manual discovery index only.
