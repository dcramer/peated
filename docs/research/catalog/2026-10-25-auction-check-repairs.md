# Repairs from the auction check — October 25, 2026

Where to find the facts behind catalog problems that the
[auction production check](../2026-10-24-auction-production-check.md) found
in Scotch Whisky Auctions lots. Each section covers one release family. The
identity rules are in `docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`.

## Ardbeg Ardcore (2022)

- Two strengths: the Committee Release at 50.1% and the Ardbeg Day general
  release at 46%.
  [Whisky Magazine](https://whiskymag.com/articles/ardbeg-launches-punk-rock-limited-edition-ardcore-in-preparation-for-ardbeg-day/)
  and [Spirited Singapore](https://www.spiritedsingapore.com/2022/05/ardbeg-ardcore/)
  state both; Whiskybase lists agree.
- Trap: titles that say "Committee Release" name the 50.1% bottling. "Ardbeg
  Ardcore Limited Edition" fits both releases, so it cannot pick one. Other
  Ardbeg names may mix Committee and general releases the same way.

## Macallan Classic Cut 2017

- 58.4%, cask strength, the first of the annual Classic Cut releases.
  [Abbey Whisky](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/macallan-classic-cut-2017-edition-aw02350)
  and [The Whiskey Wash](https://thewhiskeywash.com/?p=14822). Auction titles
  say "2017 Release" for the same bottling.

## Aberfeldy 16-year-old

- 40% in every source found: the producer's page, House of Malt, The Whisky
  Barrel, ReserveBar, and Scotch Whisky Auctions lots. No source gives 43%.
  "Unravel 16 Years Old" (56.5%) is a different release.

## Longrow single casks

- Longrow is made at Springbank. An official Longrow release lists the
  Springbank distillery as distiller and has no bottler. Some older catalog
  records still carry Springbank as bottler.
- A Longrow 2001 16-year-old single cask (54.3%, 360 bottles) appears at
  auction; no producer page for it was found, so its cask number is unknown.

## Glenglassaugh The Chosen Few

- Each release is one cask chosen by a staff member. The 1976 Ronnie Routledge
  cask is 35 years old, 49.6%, 654 bottles from an ex-sherry butt, bottled at
  cask strength:
  [Whisky Advocate](https://whiskyadvocate.com/Glenglassaugh-35-Year-Old-The-Chosen-Few-49-6),
  [Just Whisky](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/march-2019/70568-glenglassaugh-35-years-old-1976-the-chosen-few-ronnie-routledge-5060193840741.html),
  [The Whisky Barrel](https://www.thewhiskybarrel.com/products/glenglassaugh-35-year-old-the-chosen-few-1st-release).
- The 1978 Mhairi McDonald cask is 33 years old, 275 bottles
  ([Just Whisky](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/glenglassaugh-33-years-old-1978-the-chosen-few-mhairi-mcdonald-1340379)).
- Trap: "The Manager's Legacy" is a separate series. Its Walter Grant release
  is sometimes given as 1976 and 44 years old, and sometimes as 1967; that
  conflict is unresolved. Do not copy its age onto a Chosen Few bottling.
- Unknown: the full list of Chosen Few releases. No producer list was found.

## Wire Works Necessary Evil Finish (White Peak)

- Lightly peated single malt from bourbon barrels, finished in casks that held
  Thornbridge's Necessary Evil imperial stout. Releases in 2022, 2023 (51.6%),
  and 2025 (51.3%)
  ([Whisky for Everyone](https://whiskyforeveryone.com/review-wireworks-necessary-evil-finish-batch-3/),
  [The Whiskey Wash](https://thewhiskeywash.com/whiskey-news/wire-works-necessary-evil-finish-returns-for-the-third-time/)).
- The 2025 label prints `ISSUE 07-…`, 51.3%, `Bourbon/Stout`, and a bottle
  number out of 2457. Reviewers call it Batch 3, the third release; Scotch
  Whisky Auctions and Maltiply title it "Batch #7" from the issue code. Both
  names describe one release.
- Bottle counts conflict: 2,457 on the label, 2,547 in one review, and 2,059 at
  one retailer. The label value was used.
- The Whisky Exchange product page returns 403 to tools.

## Johnnie Walker The Royal Route

- Explorers' Club Collection, travel retail, 1 L, 40%, no age statement, 2013
  ([Drinks International](https://drinksint.com/news/fullstory.php/aid/3388/.html),
  [Difford's Guide](https://www.diffordsguide.com/en-au/beer-wine-spirits/4128/johnnie-walker-the-royal-route)).
  The sources disagree on the month: May or June versus October 2013.

## Tomatin 18-year-old

- The current bottling is 46%, non-chill filtered, natural colour, with a final
  maturation in Oloroso sherry butts; the label and box print "Oloroso Sherry
  Casks". Whiskybase dates the "New Design" label to 2016.
- Older 18-year-old bottlings at 40% exist (Connosr). A 43% figure at one
  retailer looks like a typo against a 46% label. Older generations have no
  separate catalog record yet.

## Not changed

- Yamazaki 12-year-old keeps release year 1984, its launch year, which fits
  the release-year rule for a marketed release.
