# Blair Athol official releases

Research checked on 20 September 2026. Scope: official Blair Athol single
malts, including historic owner collections. Independent bottlings and blends
containing Blair Athol are outside this scope. No source establishes a complete
historical inventory.

## Sources and coverage

- [Malts](https://www.malts.com/en-gb/distilleries/blair-athol) covers the active
  distillery and current visitor offering. Its current shop is not an archive.
  The [20-year-old product page](https://www.malts.com/en/products/blair-athol-20-year-old-single-malt-whisky-70cl)
  and [company announcement reproduced by WhiskyExperts](https://whiskyexperts.net/blair-athol-distillery-stellt-blair-athol-20-year-old-vor/)
  establish the 2025 limited release, 52.8% strength, 1,002 bottles and 1 April
  availability. The product page's component-age prose is inconsistent with the
  stated age; use the label's 20 years, without copying component ages.
- [Just Whisky's old 8-year-old](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/blair-athol-8-years-old-80-proof-1341354)
  shows the older Highland Malt presentation. Its Italian label states 46%,
  while the front uses 80 proof and the box says over eight years old.
  [Lochs of Whisky](https://lochsofwhisky.com/blair-athol-8-year-old-pure-malt-1980s)
  documents the later 40% Pure Malt. Decade estimates do not establish exact
  bottling or release years.
- [Passion for Whisky](https://www.passionforwhisky.com/en/whisky/scotch-whisky/single-malt-whisky/highlands/blair-athol-whisky-distillery/20887-blair-athol-12-years-75cl-italian-import.html)
  documents the old 12-year-old Pure Malt at 46%, imported by Noble Drink.
  Importer and packaging differences alone do not establish separate releases.
- [Just Whisky's bicentenary lot](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/blair-athol-18-years-old-bicentenary-limited-edition-1798-19981502453)
  documents the 18-year-old, 56.7% anniversary release of 1998.
- [The Whisky Vault](https://www.thewhiskyvault.com/blair-athol-1981-flora--fauna-cask-strength-1997-bottling-23377-p.asp)
  documents the 1981 Flora & Fauna cask-strength release bottled in 1997 at
  55.5%. Do not calculate a stated age from the two years.
- [Flora & Fauna's collector guide](https://www.florafaunawhisky.com/collection/extended-collection/)
  distinguishes the original collection from later distillery exclusives.
  Its [2019 Reserve Cask page](https://www.florafaunawhisky.com/collection/2019-release/blair-athol-1993-26-years/)
  supplies a readable label for the Chinese-market 26-year-old, supported by
  the [September 2019 announcement](https://www.sohu.com/a/342499818_120051827).
  The collector's date captions are reversed: the label says distilled
  14 May 1993 and bottled 22 July 2019. The label establishes 58.9%, puncheon
  0001, 546 bottles and first-fill PX/Oloroso seasoning. Reserve Cask Private
  Collection is a named range within Flora & Fauna.
- [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/blair-athol/blair-athol-distillery-only-bottled-2016-whisky/)
  documents the 48% distillery-only bottling of 2016. Its apparent 2016
  distillation date is not reliable evidence. A 3,000-bottle claim found in
  an auction search result remains unverified.
- [Whiskyfun's distillery index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Blair-Athol.html)
  locates the contemporary review of the 55.8% distillery-only bottling of 2010. Its label has no stated age. Later retailer and review pages sometimes
  call it 14 years old; speculation about component ages does not make that a
  label statement.
- [Distiller's 2017 Special Release entry](https://distiller.com/spirits/blair-athol-23-year-2017-special-release)
  and [Whiskyfun's 2017 session](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2017/A-Blair-Athol-session.html)
  establish 23 years, vintage 1993, 58.4%, 5,514 bottles and ex-bodega European
  oak butts. An existing 24-year age claim conflicts with this exact release.
- [The Whiskyphiles](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2020/01/30/blair-athol-2019-distillery-exclusive/)
  and [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/blair-athol/blair-athol-distillery-excusive-batch-1-whisky/)
  cover Distillery Exclusive Batch No. 1, released in 2019 at 48%, with
  6,000 bottles. [The Whisky Shop's Batch No. 2 lot](https://www.whiskyshop.com/auctions/a111094-blair-athol-distillery-exclusive-batch-2)
  establishes 48%, wine barriques, heavily charred American oak and an exact
  outturn of 6,996. Do not round that count to 7,000 or infer a release year
  from a later review.
- [Shang Hsing Wine](https://shw.com.tw/index.php/component/content/article?id=2329:the-whisky-big-nose-blair-athol-1991-25-yo-select-cask-single-malt-scotch-whisky)
  provides the Taiwanese Big Nose selection's readable Casks of Distinction
  label: Select Cask, 25 years, 1991/2017, 58.1%, European oak, cask 0001.
  The shop's broad independent-bottler category does not override the official
  label. The bottle-count text is obscured; do not infer it.
- [C.Gars' Managers' Choice listing](https://www.cgarsltd.co.uk/blair-athol-1995-managers-choice-547-70cl-limited-edition-152570-p-18083.html)
  corroborates the 1995 selection at 54.7%. Its label supplies bottling year
  2009 and cask 5989, without a stated age. Bottling year does not establish
  release year.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Manager's Dram guide](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/series/managers-dram)
  identifies the 15-year-old bottled in 1996 at 59.4% and the 10-year-old
  bottled in 2019 at 57.1%. Their original staff-only distribution leaves
  public-release eligibility under Peated's identity model unresolved; later
  auction availability alone does not prove original public marketing.
- [Diageo Rare Series](https://www.diageorareandexceptional.com/us/rare-series)
  is the entrance for the 2026 collection. Existing exact label images also
  identify the 1991 34-year-old and the Rare Malts 1975 27-year-old bottled
  in April 2003. The latter's recorded 6,000-bottle outturn was not independently
  established by this review and was preserved rather than guessed away.

## Remaining leads and identity limits

English searches using Blair Athol and Blair Atholl, collection names, cask
numbers and exact strengths, plus Chinese-market and collector sources,
did not establish public marketed releases for these Casks of Distinction
leads beyond search listings: 1989/32 years/51.1%/31207; 1989/33 years/48%/31220;
1991/24 years/57.8%/MC605; 1991/28 years/58.4%/2445; 1991/29 years/55.7%/8583;
1991/31 years/53.5%/8624; and 1993/25 years/58.6%/0002. Each needs an exact
readable label and evidence of a public release. Whiskybase search snippets
were leads only; no automated Whiskybase collection was attempted.

Other open questions are the 35-year-old 55.5% commemorative cask 3604, a
20-year-old at 52.0%, a square-bottle 12-year-old at 43%, and the relationship
of older NAS Highland Malt, Special Light and 80-proof presentations to the
documented age-stated releases. Proof conversion or a different box alone
does not establish a different liquid. A 1988 visitor-centre ceramic
presentation also lacks proof of a separate public release.

Visitor handfills, private casks without marketed bottled releases and
not-for-sale tasting samples are outside the identity model. Bell's and
Craig Athol blends are outside this single-malt scope.

## Images and access

Exact bottle images were inspected where readable, including existing stored
images. No new bottle image had established reuse permission. Existing bottle
images lack source/license metadata; their identity can be checked without
inventing provenance. The existing distillery image has a Wikimedia Commons
source and a CC BY-SA license. Commons searches found distillery photographs,
not usable exact images for the missing releases.

[Diageo's website terms](https://legal.diageo.com/en/website-conditions-of-use)
do not grant general republication permission. Just Whisky, Master of Malt,
The Whisky Vault and Shang Hsing Wine show copyright restrictions; no
permissive reuse license was established for Passion for Whisky, Lochs of
Whisky, The Whisky Shop or the Flora & Fauna collector guide. Keep their
photographs as evidence rather than uploading them without permission.

Master of Malt, Passion for Whisky and the collector guide were readable in
a browser when simple page retrieval was insufficient. The Whisky Exchange
and some Whisky Shop pages presented Cloudflare challenges. Whisky Auctioneer
lot access was inconsistent even when its collection guide was readable.
Just Whisky's cached pages were useful, but its live site later showed a
maintenance notice. Malt Maniacs did not return useful content in this pass.
Search snippets from blocked pages were not substituted for inspected facts.
