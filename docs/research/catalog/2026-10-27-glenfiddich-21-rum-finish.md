# Glenfiddich 21-year-old rum finish labels — October 27, 2026

Where to find the facts that tell apart the label generations of Glenfiddich's
21-year-old rum-finished single malt. The rule this follows is "An older bottling
of an ongoing expression can be its own Bottle" in
`docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`: keep the expression name
(`21-year-old`) and put the printed label wording in `edition`.

## The label generations

| Label wording               | When                                                       | Strength              | Sources                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Havana Reserve              | First release, about 2002; Cuban rum casks                 | 40%                   | Scotch Whisky Auctions lots, whisky.auction                    |

## Where the facts live

- [The Drinks Business, August 2016](https://www.thedrinksbusiness.com/2016/08/the-week-in-pictures-186/)
  reports Glenfiddich's launch of new packaging for the 21-year-old
  "Reserva Rum Cask Finish". This is the only dated source for that label.
- [Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/lot/6134511/glenfiddich-21-year-old-reserva-rum-cask-finish)
  and Scotch Whisky Auctions lot pages tell the rename story: Havana Reserve
  first, renamed for the US market, later Gran Reserva, and in 2016 "Reserva
  Rum Cask Finish". Their order for the middle names disagrees.
- The [Glenfiddich US shop](https://shop.us.glenfiddich.com/products/glenfiddich-gran-reserva),
  [Selfridges](https://www.selfridges.com/CN/zh/cat/glenfiddich-gran-reserva-21-year-old-single-malt-scotch-whisky-700ml_R03861295/),
  and [Whisky Shop](https://www.whiskyshop.com/glenfiddich-gran-reserva-21-year-old)
  sell today's bottle as Gran Reserva in the Grand Series, 40%, finished for up
  to four months in Caribbean rum casks.
- [whisky.auction](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/70136/glenfiddich-21-year-old-havana-reserve)
  and Scotch Whisky Auctions lots show the Havana Reserve label and its Cuban
  rum cask wording.

## Conflicts and traps

- Auction descriptions disagree on the order of "Gran Reserva", "Caribbean Rum
  Finish", and a plain "21 Year Old" label between about 2004 and 2016. No
  producer source dates them.
- Most listings give 40%. Some older 75cl bottles list 43%; one source gives
  43.2%. The strength of those bottlings is unresolved.
- Store titles often drop the label wording and say only "Glenfiddich 21 Year
  Old". Such a title cannot pick a generation; check the label or the listing
  photo.

## Still unknown

- Whether the pre-2016 Gran Reserva and today's Grand Series Gran Reserva
  differ in printed wording or strength. Both read "Gran Reserva" at 40%, so
  they share one Bottle until a source shows a difference.
- When Gran Reserva replaced "Reserva Rum Cask Finish" again. Retailers mark
  Gran Reserva bottles "pre-2019", which conflicts with the 2016 launch.
