# Ledaig independent bottlings: historical sources

Research checked on 20 September 2026. This complements the
[official-release guide](2026-09-20-ledaig.md). The target includes marketed
Ledaig releases under independent labels, including unpeated whisky actually
sold as Ledaig. Tobermory is the production site; a release's selecting label
determines its Brand. Unpeated Tobermory and blends merely containing Ledaig
are different catalog targets.

No inspected archive proves worldwide completeness. Coverage includes 1972
and later vintages, old brown-label bottlings, releases sold from the 1980s
onward, and modern independent releases through 2025. This is a source guide
for continuing the backfill, not a claim that every private, export, festival,
or independent cask has been found.

## Producer archives

- [Cadenhead's bottlings](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/) retain sold-out
  releases. Exact pages identify distillation year, age, strength, bottling
  date and cask details. Useful entrances include the
  [2025 Authentic Collection](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/auth-nov25-b6-l15/),
  [2025 Original Collection](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/original-collection-13-b5-l-14/),
  [2008 Small Batch](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/ledaig-11yo-54-0/),
  and [1997 Single Cask](https://www.cadenhead.scot/bottlings/ledaig-20yo-52-8/).
  Neighboring product navigation appears before the product title in extracted
  text. Do not mistake that neighboring distillery for the page's subject.
- [Gordon & MacPhail's whisky pages](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/)
  and sitemap include Connoisseurs Choice, Discovery and archived market
  releases. Exact pages distinguish the
  [2001 at 56.8%](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/21054-cc-cs-ledaig-2001-568)
  from the [USA bottling at 56.3%](https://www.gordonandmacphail.com/our-whiskies/19067-cc-cs-ledaig-2001-563-usa).
  A cask-like number in a URL alone does not prove the printed cask number.
  The retailer's [2005 Hermitage page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/40834/ledaig-2005-hermitage-finish-private-collection)
  preserves the older Private Collection identity, bottling month and outturn.
- [Wilson & Morgan's catalog](https://www.wilsonandmorgan.com/catalogue/)
  retains historical product pages in its product sitemap. The tables include
  cask numbers and exact outturns, often missing from other archives. Examples:
  [1998 at eighteen years](https://www.wilsonandmorgan.com/catalogue/cask-strength/ledaig-18yo-wm241/),
  [2008 at 56.4%](https://www.wilsonandmorgan.com/catalogue/cask-strength/ledaig-12yo-wm334/),
  and [2008 Private Cask](https://www.wilsonandmorgan.com/catalogue/classic-selection/ledaig-2008-wm370/).
  Site categories such as Classic Selection are not automatically bottle
  Series. Preserve cask-number punctuation instead of inventing expanded
  numbers from abbreviated multi-cask lists.
- [Archives' worldwide releases](https://archiveswhisky.com/the-whisky/world-wide-release/archives-whisky-releases-2021/)
  and [USA archive](https://archiveswhisky.com/the-whisky/archives-usa/archives-usa-2019/)
  identify named labels and strengths. The page path, heading and actual
  bottling year can differ. A page called 2021 may describe a 2020 bottling.
- [Elixir Distillers' brand guide](https://elixirdistillers.com/brands)
  explicitly identifies Ledaig as Macbeth's First Murderer. Search the
  character name and the Macbeth Brand as well as Ledaig: an existing record
  may have neither Ledaig in its title nor a complete distillery relationship.

## Independent historical inventories

[Whiskyfun's Ledaig index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Ledaig.html) is a productive
entrance to old Alambic Classique, Cadenhead, Gordon & MacPhail, James MacArthur,
Signatory and smaller independent labels. Follow each link to the actual
tasting entry. Repeated tastings, approximate dates and different packaging
are not necessarily different releases. The index mixes official whisky,
independent bottlings and retailer selections.

[WhiskyNotes' Ledaig archive](https://www.whiskynotes.be/category/ledaig/)
provides a separate inventory and contemporary detail for Asian-market
bottlings, Belgian selectors, modern independent labels and club releases.
Full articles can identify the real label where abbreviated headers do not.
For example, [Liquid Art and Liquid Treasures](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2014/ledaig/ledaig-2004-liquid-art-ledaig-2004-liquid-treasures/)
are different releases and businesses. Liquid Art's Belgian website also
distinguishes it from the similarly named German business. The article's
explicit “launched today” supports a release date; its publication date alone
would not.

[The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/b/40/ledaig-single-malt-scotch-whisky)
retains exact product pages after sale. Its
[Whisky Show 2022 page](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/67785/ledaig-2006-15-year-old-sherry-cask-the-whisky-show-2022)
names Elixir Distillers as bottler and gives the outturn; the selecting retailer
and bottling business must not be conflated. Sample pages can corroborate an
original bottle but do not establish a separate release or a new bottler.

## Conflicts and remaining identity questions

- The Archives first release's cask is written as `90009` by Whiskyfun and
  `900009` by the [contemporary WhiskyNotes review](https://www.whiskynotes.be/2012/ledaig/ledaig-2004-archives/).
  The latter agrees with other contemporary listings; preserve the full
  number rather than silently dropping a zero.
- WhiskyNotes abbreviates Signatory's Le Gus't cask as `751`, while Whiskyfun
  uses `700751`. It also lists `90040` for the 2005 TWE release where Whiskyfun
  uses `900040`. These are identity leads, not evidence of extra releases.
- The 2005 TWE thirteen-year release has a cask number that conflicts with
  WhiskyNotes' header, which repeats its outturn as the cask number. Prefer an
  exact retailer label before changing that field.
- Sestante's 1973 fourteen-year bottles exist at different strengths. A sparse
  Castle Label record without strength cannot be matched safely. Image
  metadata and review text also disagree about the bottling year.
- The Signatory 1974 thirty-year 48% review has an unrelated Benriach image.
  Do not reuse that image or use it to verify this Ledaig's label.
- A generic G&M 1972 brown-label review may describe one of the age-stated
  releases. Age-free text does not prove an extra bottle.
- Sparse Cadenhead, Berry Bros, Wilson & Morgan, Whisky Jury and other imported
  records lack the strength and cask facts needed for exact matching. Names
  containing only a vintage and bottler abbreviation may describe several
  releases. In particular, the Whisky Jury's 2010 bourbon and split Oloroso
  casks cannot all be assigned to one bare 2010 record.
- Mo Òr Collection and Whisky Nerds need a verified label/business identity;
  existing-entity searches did not resolve them. The Nectar's similarly named
  Daily Dram records and the two Stillman businesses need label confirmation.
- The Good Spirits bottling in the Hong Kong review explicitly belongs to a
  different business from Glasgow's Good Spirits Co. Do not use the latter
  merely because its name matches.
- Full Proof's revived 1972 stock may be a relabelled existing release. Robert
  Butler's Mull-a-Mhoine and the Caledonian Collection need their actual
  bottling roles established. The HNWS Taiwan selection likewise needs its
  exact label, rather than assuming an owner's better-known Brand.
- Claxton's 2007 at 53% appears both without a cask and as a China release.
  Do not duplicate it without resolving whether the two accounts describe
  the same octave.
- Old SMWS 42.8 has a well-supported code, age and strength, but a readable
  Society label is still needed for its common title. The James MacArthur
  anniversary's printed wording also needs checking: the review's date span
  is internally inconsistent.

## Access and image rights

Cadenhead and Gordon & MacPhail blocked some direct requests but their normal
public browser pages were accessible. G&M's cookie notice and age gate can
obscure screenshots, and an element screenshot can clip the label below the
viewport. Check that the complete label is visible before relying on it.
Wilson & Morgan and the two review archives were readable with ordinary,
paced requests. Search snippets sometimes exposed pages that the web fetcher
could not retrieve; snippets alone were not treated as comprehensive archives.

Whiskybase database pages were not collected. Search-result appearances were
only leads; Archives' own producer site is a separate source. Do not follow
links into the Whiskybase database to bulk-copy its catalog.

No republishing license was established for photographs from Cadenhead,
Gordon & MacPhail, Wilson & Morgan, Archives, Whiskyfun, WhiskyNotes or the
retailer/auction sources. Their exact label photos can aid research, but their
availability does not authorize uploading them to Peated. Existing stored
images were preserved; missing images need licensed photos or permission.
