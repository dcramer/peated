# Tomatin catalog backfill

Date: 2026-09-30

## Scope

This pass covered the official Tomatin consumer brand and the Tomatin distillery record. It included current releases, official limited releases, travel retail releases, distillery exclusives, and named producer collections.

Independent bottlings distilled at Tomatin and legacy Cù Bòcan records were first inventoried without changing their Brand. A separately approved follow-up then verified those relationships, corrected them where the label evidence was conclusive, and merged proven duplicate Bottles.

## Decisions

- Added missing official Tomatin releases only when the producer or corroborating specialist sources established a distinct bottle identity.
- Completed the French, Italian, Portuguese, Sherry, Five Virtues, Cuatro, and Warehouse 6 collection membership without merging existing records.
- Recorded editions separately from expression names where Peated builds the display name from both fields.
- Marked the distillery active because the producer currently operates its distillery, visitor centre, product shop, and release program.
- Left facts unknown when a source did not state them. In particular, no age was derived only from vintage and release dates.
- Did not copy producer bottle images. The pages establish identity, but no reusable image license was found. The existing licensed distillery image was retained.
- Merged only records proven to describe the same marketed release: the 14-year-old Port expression, both Contrasting Casks releases, Cuatro Fino, Italian Collection Amarone, the official 25-year-old, the official 1967 40-year-old, Cù Bòcan Signature, and Cù Bòcan Creation #8.
- Kept the Cask Strength records separate because the available records conflict on strength and do not establish a common batch or edition.
- Moved legacy Cù Bòcan releases to the Cù Bòcan Brand while retaining Tomatin as distiller.
- Moved verified independent bottlings to their label brands and retained Tomatin as distiller. Corrected historical release facts only when the evidence identified one release. The generic 1990 Whiskybroker row kept its release-specific facts unknown because several candidates share that name.
- Removed generated descriptions and tasting notes that still asserted ages disproved by the corrected label evidence.

## Unresolved work

- The Cask Strength records remain separate because one record points to a different strength and the available evidence does not identify a shared batch or edition.
- The generic 1990 Whiskybroker record is now under the correct label Brand with Tomatin as distiller, but its age, bottling year, strength, cask, and outturn remain unknown because several distinct Whiskybroker releases use the same short name.
- Some older official records remain unresolved because their names are generic or the available evidence does not distinguish packaging, batch, or market variants.
- The producer page for the current UK-exclusive single cask does not state strength or outturn, so those fields remain unknown.
- The Belladrum release page does not state strength or cask number, so those fields remain unknown.
- The producer confirms the early Warehouse 6 vintages as collection members, but the readily available producer pages do not state all release facts for the 1972 and 1977 editions. Their unsupported fields remain unknown.
