# A.D. Rattray catalog sources — September 20, 2026

Source guide for A.D. Rattray's Cask Collection from 2004 through September
2026, Vintage Cask Collection from 2015 onward, customer selections, Warehouse
Collection, House Malt, The Octave Project, and Rattray's Selection. It also
covers the related Stronachie, Cask Islay, Cask Orkney, Cask Speyside, and Bank
Note ranges, including discontinued releases.

Coverage includes the UK producer archive and shop, early Dewar Rattray
listings, and selected US and European customer bottlings. No source proves
complete coverage of private casks or all export markets. The September 18
working inventory supplied leads; it is not evidence for a fact or an existing
Bottle's identity. Recheck source pages before use. Catalog decisions follow
the [Whisky Identity Model](../../architecture/whisky-identity-model.md).

## Producer sources

The [single-cask archive](https://www.adrattray.com/whisky-archive/single-cask-archive)
is the strongest starting point for recent Cask Collection releases. Follow
all its numbered pages, then check the
[current collection](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection) and
[shop](https://shop.adrattray.com/) separately. The archive says it contains
some past releases; it does not claim to document every cask bottled since
the collection began in 2004.

Individual archive pages give distillation and bottling dates, age, strength,
cask number, maturation, outturn, and a label image. Match the whole set of
facts. A distillery and vintage can recur, and a cask can be bottled in more
than one instalment. The label is useful when the heading or specification
contains a typo. Bottling dates do not establish release dates.

The [shop product feed](https://shop.adrattray.com/products.json?limit=250)
provides product descriptions and canonical product handles. It includes
third-party whisky, events, and sample sets as well as Rattray releases.
Check the product's own text before assigning a Brand or Series. Current
shop descriptions also cover recent casks absent from the main archive.

The [Vintage Cask archive](https://www.adrattray.com/whisky-archive/vintage-cask-archive)
has two pages, with specifications embedded in each page rather than linked
from separate products. It covers bottlings from 2015 through 2021. The shop
adds later Vintage Cask releases. Do not apply the regular Cask Collection's
current production statements to every historical Vintage release without
product or label evidence.

Use [producer news](https://www.adrattray.com/our-story/news) for dated release
announcements. An announcement's publication date supports availability only
when its text actually introduces or offers the release. A shop publication
timestamp or auction closing date is not a release date.

## Range and producer evidence

The [Cask Collection page](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection)
describes independent single-cask releases at natural colour and cask strength,
without chill filtration. The featured distillery is the producer; A.D.
Rattray is the Brand and independent bottler. Retain a retailer or importer's
selection wording only where the exact release documents it.

The [Warehouse Collection page](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/warehouse-collection)
describes shop-exclusive single casks, including remaining portions of casks.
It explicitly permits strengths down to 46%, so membership does not prove
cask strength. It does establish natural colour and no chill filtration.

The current [House Malt page](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/house-malt)
covers the Highland, Lowland, and Islay ten-year-old malts at 40%, matured in
bourbon casks. Its account of the Morrison family's former distilleries does
not identify the distiller of each current regional malt. Earlier numbered
House Malts require historical product evidence.

The [Octave Project page](https://www.adrattray.com/whisky-archive/the-octave-project)
explains the division of a cask into PX, Oloroso, Rioja, and rum octaves,
retaining an original portion for comparison. It mentions both sample sets
and 700ml bottles involving Arran, Pulteney, and Bunnahabhain. This contradicts
a blanket claim that Pulteney existed only as samples. The
[July 2018 announcement](https://www.adrattray.com/our-story/news/428-an-experiment-in-innovation)
also explicitly offers 700ml bottles of each finish. It identifies Arran
2011, Pulteney 2007, and Bunnahabhain 2002 as the starting casks. Exact shop
pages establish strengths and ages; sample sets are not separate Bottles.

The [Stronachie page](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/stronachie) identifies
Benrinnes as the source of the recreated malt. Distinguish the revived Brand
from the closed historical distillery. For the discontinued twelve-year-old,
the [February 2014 producer capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20140213214301/https://www.adrattray.com/stronachie)
records its introduction in 2002; an
[exact auction listing](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/142-the-101st-auction/328338-stronachie-12-year-old-1l/)
supports the age, 43% strength, and Benrinnes origin. The litre bottle is a
package variant, not a separate whisky.

The [producer history](https://www.adrattray.com/our-story/our-history) records
the earlier A. Dewar Rattray name. Search both Dewar Rattray and A.D. Rattray
for early casks. The dated producer archive supports Stronachie Sherry Edition
in 2017 and the later Cask Islay and Cask Speyside variants.

## Historical sources and access

[Whisky Monitor](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/) has individual records under
`bottle.jsp?bid=...`, including Dewar Rattray-era releases. Its title and Bottle
Profile can hold age, vintage, bottling year, strength, cask and outturn. It is
a secondary catalog: estimated years and uncorroborated or conflicting facts
need to remain unknown. An example is
[Glen Garioch 1990, cask 4125](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/bottle.jsp?bid=5045).

[Scotch Whisky Auctions](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/) provides exact
lot descriptions and label photographs. Old URLs using a generic auction slug
can redirect to a named auction. Follow the redirect. A browser may show an
age gate before the lot; an empty initial fetch is not evidence that the lot
is gone. Check the label where the title and description disagree.

Wayback captures of the producer and its shop preserve discontinued ranges.
Keep the complete capture URL, including the original product path; a
timestamp alone is not a recoverable source. Some captures returned an
Internet Archive temporary-offline page, sometimes with HTTP 200. Others
returned the Wayback interface without the captured product. Check the body,
retry temporary failures at a low rate, and try the original live URL. A
successful HTTP response alone does not verify a product.

The [December 2019 House Malt capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20191212121520/https://www.adrattray.com/single-malts/house-malt)
lists early numbered casks. Its No.7 and No.8 both identify Glen Moray 2007
cask 5455. That does not prove two different whiskies. No.9 and No.10 conflict
with other supplied historical leads; obtain exact labels before resolving
them. Do not apply today's regional House Malt identities to this older range.

[Whiskyfun](https://www.whiskyfun.com/) distillery indexes provide contemporary
reviews that can corroborate old catalog entries by cask, age, strength, and
outturn. Approximate release years marked with a plus/minus sign are not exact
bottling or release dates. Auction and specialist listings also cover customer
selections absent from the producer archive.

Useful independent sources for the smaller ranges include
[Bank Note Peated Reserve](https://scotchnoob.com/2021/02/08/bank-note-peated/),
[Bank Note 25](https://shop.klwines.com/products/details/1451396),
[Rattray's Selection No.1](https://cocktailchem.blogspot.com/2018/02/), and
[Selection Batch No.2](https://www.caskers.com/rattray-s-selection-batch-2-21-year-old-blended-malt-scotch-whisky/).
These provide usable alternatives to incomplete Pacific Edge links. A blend's
component vintages are not its own vintage. Descriptions of a release year do
not automatically establish a bottling year.

[Whiskybase's Rattray inventory](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/bottler/77376/dewar-rattray)
corroborates discontinued Warehouse and House Malt releases when producer
captures are unavailable. It remains a community catalog: use exact entries,
leave unsupported dates and cask numbers unknown, and do not assume its
bottle-count field distinguishes total production from remaining shop stock.
Search results for “Warehouse Collection” also include unrelated bottlers;
check the named bottler.

Contemporary [Arran PX](https://thewhiskyphiles.com/2018/07/06/arran-6-years-old-px-sherry-octave-finish-the-octave-project-55-4-a-d-rattray-2018/)
and [Bunnahabhain Octave](https://whiskywonderr.wordpress.com/2018/08/11/ad-rattrays-bunnahabhain-theoctaveproject/)
reviews preserve strengths and ages. Combine these with the producer's dated
full-bottle announcement. The fourteen-year-old original Bunnahabhain portion
still needs confirmation as a standalone full-bottle release.

## Evidence conflicts and exact-release sources

- [Dalmore 2003, cask 14742](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection/990-dalmore-2003):
  the page truncates the bottling date to `20.07.202`. Its label clearly reads
  `20.07.2021`, supporting 2021.
- [Cragganmore 1986, cask 1492](https://www.adrattray.com/whisky-archive/vintage-cask-archive), in the Vintage archive: the printed page age of
  33 years conflicts with distillation in June 1986 and bottling in February 2017. Leave the age unknown pending a readable exact label; do not calculate
  and substitute an age.
- [Auchentoshan 1998, cask 100391](https://shop.adrattray.com/products/auchentoshan-1998):
  the shop header says 57.5%, while its detailed specification says 56.9%.
  The exact label confirms 56.9%, which was used.
- [Littlemill 1991, cask 558, for Park Avenue Liquor Shop](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/littlemill-1991-ad-rattray-22-year-old-558-park-avenue-liquor-shop):
  this independently found auction record supports 22 years and 49.2%.
  Its photograph shows A.D. Rattray's Cask Collection and Park Avenue wording.
  The photographed bottle is 750ml despite the page's 70cl heading; volume
  does not create another Bottle.
- [Glenturret cask 891](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection/689-glenturret-1998): the producer heading and downloadable label artwork
  say 1998, which conflicts with the stated age and bottling date.
  [Whiskyfun's contemporary review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2015/A-verticale-of-eight-Glenturret.html)
  identifies the exact 25-year-old cask as 1988/2014, at 45.3% with 308 bottles.
  Use 1988; a label illustration can contain the same error as the page.
- [Miltonduff 1995, cask 2592](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection/695-miltonduff-1995): the page says eight years, but the label says
  eighteen. Use the printed eighteen-year age.
- [Pulteney cask 700751](https://www.adrattray.com/our-whisky/cask-collection/1162-pulteney-2007): the specification's 2017 vintage conflicts with the
  title, release announcement, and label, which identify 2007. Use 2007.
- [Balblair 2011, cask 635](https://shop.adrattray.com/products/balblair-2011): the archive repeats the cask number as the outturn.
  The label and detailed shop specification establish 603 bottles.
- [Strathclyde 1969, cask 010722](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/124-auction/223734-strathclyde-1969-37-year-old-ad-rattray/): preserve the leading zero in the cask number.
- [Glen Moray 1990, cask 4556](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/bottle.jsp?bid=6047): the supplied specialist record combines a
  sixteen-year age with bottling in 2008. Defer the release until a second
  exact source or label settles the conflict.
- [Bowmore 1996, cask 960034](https://www.divingforpearlsblog.com/2017/04/bowmore-18-year-old-1996-ad-rattray.html):
  the contemporary review establishes the eighteen-year release's exact dates,
  59.1% strength, and 464-bottle outturn. The page worked in a browser when
  the text-fetch tool returned a rate-limit error.
- [Warehouse Dalmunach](https://www.whiskyhammer.com/item/82956/Dalmunach/Dalmunach---4-Year-Old-AD-Rattray.html):
  the auction identifies a four-year-old bourbon-cask release at 66.8%.
  The supplied shop URL contains “9-year-old”; a URL slug is not reliable age
  evidence. Use the exact auction description's four years.
- Stronachie 1976: [Whisky Monitor](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/bottle.jsp?bid=11355)
  and [Scotch Whisky Auctions](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/112-auction/153447-stronachie-1976/) disagree on
  outturn, and the auction's “lost distillery” description is misleading for
  a whisky distilled after the original distillery closed. Leave outturn,
  bottling year, and distiller unknown rather than importing those claims.
- [Warehouse Craigellachie sixteen-year-old](https://web.archive.org/web/20210612182655/https://shop.adrattray.com/products/warehouse-collection-craigellachie-16-year-old): the shop's three bottles available
  are remaining stock, not the release's total outturn. The [Orkney twenty-one](https://web.archive.org/web/20250208230432/https://shop.adrattray.com/products/warehouse-collection-orkney-21-year-old)
  explicitly says cask strength despite being below 46%.

### Bowmore and Glenrothes identification

[Bowmore 1991 cask 2075](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/30391/bowmore-1991-dr)
provides an exact cask record for the eighteen-year-old release. The [contemporary Rattray reviews](https://myannoyingopinions.com/category/whisky-by-bottler/a-d-rattray/)
independently identify the 1991 eighteen-year-old cask 2075. Generic
“Bowmore eighteen-year-old” descriptions do not distinguish it from other
casks and need further identifying evidence.

[Glenrothes 1997 cask 717974](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/239759/glenrothes-1997-dr)
identifies the twenty-three-year-old Vintage Cask release bottled in 2021.
[Total Wine's exact product](https://www.totalwine.com/spirits/scotch/single-malt/ad-rattray-glenrothes-1997-brlsl/p/351700700?igrules=true&s=1130)
corroborates the cask and bourbon-hogshead maturation. However, Whiskybase
reports 56%, while [Jack Rose's whisky list](https://www.jackrosediningsaloon.com/s/Whiskey-Book-121524.pdf)
reports 55% for the same cask, vintage, age, and bottling year. Leave strength
unknown until a readable exact label settles it. The existing Peated image
URL did not load, so it could not resolve the conflict. A generic Glenrothes
1997 description does not establish that it is this cask.

## Image reuse

The [trade downloads](https://www.adrattray.com/trade-downloads) offer images
for trade partners promoting the products. They are not a general reuse
license for Peated. The producer's
[terms](https://www.adrattray.com/terms-and-conditions) describe product images
as illustrative but do not grant catalog reuse. Auction photographs likewise
need permission or a suitable license. Use them to read labels; do not assume
permission to upload them.

## Still unknown

### Existing release identities

Searches for a thirty-year-old A.D. Rattray Port Ellen 1982 did not establish
an exact release. Sparse records with suspect ages cannot be repaired merely
by subtracting a vintage from a later year. The suspected earlier age repair
remains unproven.

The sparse existing records still need individual identity evidence. Searching
the producer archive, historical specialist records, and contemporary review
indexes produced the following candidates, but no safe exact matches:

### Historical and customer-release leads

Other unresolved leads include Longmorn 1990 at 54% versus cask 30020 at
55.3%; Linkwood 1996 casks 3928 and 3982 with conflicting strengths; early
Cask Islay vatting numbers; and the generic Glenrothes record that shares a
name group with a specific Vintage release. Its identity remains unresolved.

The old Dewar Rattray site's distillery/vintage-only list, Pacific Edge's
private-cask list, uncertain customer selections, and Ailsa Craig remain
leads rather than verified releases. This review does not establish a complete
history of every private cask. Package-only variants, sample sets, third-party
shop stock, and Clydeside's own releases do not become A.D. Rattray Bottles.

### Octave and personalized releases

Arran Rioja and the Pulteney Octave finishes should be researched as full-bottle
leads, not dismissed as sample-only products. Their exact strengths and label
names were not established here. A personalized label alone does not establish
another release; private-cask selections need evidence of distinct liquid.
