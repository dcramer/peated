# Yamazaki — September 2026

These sources covered different parts of the catalog; none proved a complete
historical Yamazaki inventory.

- Suntory's [Japanese product range](https://www.suntory.co.jp/whisky/yamazaki/product/),
  [distillery history](https://www.suntory.co.jp/factory/blog-d/000127.html),
  [The Essence archive](https://www.suntory.co.jp/whisky/essence/), and dated
  [global announcements](https://www.suntoryglobalspirits.com/news) were strongest
  for the core range, launch dates, named component-whisky releases, and recent
  Tsukuriwake editions. Current producer pages omit most discontinued, private,
  and market-specific Bottles.
- The photographed collector indexes for Yamazaki vintages, including
  [1991](https://yabejojo.jimdoweb.com/yamazaki/yamazaki-vintage-1991/),
  [1992](https://yabejojo.jimdoweb.com/yamazaki/yamazaki-vintage-1992/),
  [1993](https://yabejojo.jimdoweb.com/yamazaki/yamazaki-vintage-1993/), and
  [2003](https://yabejojo.jimdoweb.com/yamazaki/yamazaki-vintage-2003/),
  exposed large Owner's Cask and private-cask families. Readable labels settled
  cask codes and release families, but collector captions sometimes contained
  OCR errors, transposed digits, or calculated facts and needed independent
  checks.
- [Whisky Auctioneer's Yamazaki pages](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/distilleries/yamazaki),
  exact Bonhams and Spink catalogs, and exact Sotheby's and Whisky.Auction lots
  were productive for historical names, cask numbers, strengths, maturation,
  and outturns. Auction dates did not establish release dates, and auction
  photography did not grant reuse rights.
- [Whiskybase's Yamazaki distillery index](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/distillery/75/whiskies)
  and [Spirit Radar](https://www.spiritradar.com/) were broad gap finders, not
  identity authorities. Both mixed marketed releases with package variants,
  samples, blends, independent bottlings, or misattributed Suntory products.
- Japanese retailer archives, contemporary blogs, venue menus, and corporate
  announcements were necessary for domestic exclusives. They were most useful
  when they showed a readable exact label or named the recipient, date, cask,
  and bottle count; copied seller descriptions alone were treated as leads.
- The
  [Wikimedia Commons Yamazaki category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Yamazaki_bottles)
  and [Openverse](https://openverse.org/search/image?q=yamazaki%20whisky) found
  reusable images for a few exact releases. Every live source page, creator,
  license, and visible label still required checking. Most Flickr, retailer,
  collector, and auction photos were unusable because they lacked clear reuse
  terms, showed several Bottles, or did not identify the exact annual release.
- Searches across producer archives, auction catalogs, Japanese-language pages,
  specialist indexes, retailer records, and Peated still left some private
  releases and sparse early labels unresolved. Absence from a broad index was
  not treated as proof that no release existed.
