# Mars distilleries — September 16, 2026

This production review covered every whisky release from Hombo Shuzo's Mars
whisky operation: the Mars brand (`E1169`), Mars Komagatake Distillery
(`E1953`, called Mars Shinshu Distillery until March 2024), Mars Tsunuki
Distillery (`E1969`), Mars Yakushima Aging Cellar, and the closed Yamanashi
(1960–1972) and Kagoshima (1969–1984) malt distilleries. It included the
domestic blends, the Komagatake and Tsunuki single malts, distillery-exclusive
and festival bottlings, export-market single casks, the US importer range, the
Hokkaido Dakekanba blended malts, and independent bottlings that name a Mars
distillery. The verification snapshot was fetched during the afternoon of
September 16, 2026, Pacific time. Re-fetch records before reusing these IDs.

## Method

1. The Peated inventory was the union of every paginated Bottle for Brand
   `E1169`, distillery `E1953`, distillery `E1969`, and name searches for
   Mars, Komagatake, Iwai, Tsunuki, Maltage, Shinshu, and Yakushima. It held
   47 Bottles before the work started: 44 in scope, plus three Ocean Whisky
   Co. blends that Peated links to Sanraku's separate Yamanashi Distillery
   (`E366722`), which is not a Mars site.
2. The outside inventory started from Hombo's own product archive. Every
   product page listed in the site's product sitemaps was fetched in English
   and Japanese and parsed for name, release date, lineup rows (size, ABV,
   bottle count, JAN, price), and the spec block (type, distillation and
   bottling dates, cask and cask number, maturation site, producing site).
   The archive holds 149 whisky pages from 1988 onward, including six unaged
   new-pot spirits.
3. Releases missing from the archive were found through Hombo's news and
   festival pages, Wayback captures of Hombo's pre-2016 product site and news
   pages, the US importer High Road Spirits and distributor Skurnik pages, La
   Maison du Whisky and other European retailer pages, Japanese retailer and
   bar catalogs, dated Japanese whisky blogs, exact Yahoo! Auctions lots, and
   auction-house lots. Two background research passes covered the pre-1993
   era and the distillery-exclusive, export, and independent families; every
   record created from their findings was checked against the cited page.
4. Identity used Brand, distillery, age or NAS, ABV, vintage, bottling year,
   edition, cask number, and Series. Package size (200ml, 500ml, 720ml, 1.8L)
   and gift boxes never created a second Bottle.
5. Writes used explicit production IDs through the authenticated API. Every
   created Bottle was re-fetched and compared with its request. Every patched
   Bottle was read immediately before the patch, and the stored values and
   unrelated fields were compared after it.

## Brand and naming decisions

- Hombo positions Komagatake and Tsunuki as consumer brands inside the Mars
  Whisky house, and importers and retailers market them as "Mars Komagatake"
  and "Mars Tsunuki". Peated therefore uses the distillery Entity as the Brand
  for their single malts, the same model as Yamazaki, Hakushu, Yoichi, and
  Chichibu. `E1953` carries the short name "Mars Komagatake" and `E1969` the
  short name "Mars Tsunuki", so Bottle names read "Mars Komagatake Single Malt
  - 2022 Edition". The first pass of this work had used Mars as Brand with
    Komagatake and Tsunuki Series; that was reversed on the same day and the two
    Series were deleted.
- Under those Brands, `name` is the marketed expression from Hombo's title
  without the distillery word, and it keeps any age, vintage, or cask wording
  that the producer prints: `22-year-old`, `27-year-old`, `1986 30-year-old`,
  `9-year-old Small Batch`, `Single Cask 2014 No.1841 7-year-old`,
  `2022 Edition`, `The First`, `Peated`, `Japanese Spring`, and the named
  lines `Tsunuki Aging`, `Yakushima Aging`, `Komagatake Aging`, `IPA Cask
Finish`, `Double Cellars`, `Nature of Shinshu`, `Asagi Madara no Sato`,
  `The Revival 2011`, and `Malt Gallery`. Export and private single casks
  whose sources give vintage, cask number, and age use the same pattern
  (`Single Cask 2018 #1964 6-year-old`, `Yakushima Aging Single Cask 2016
#2067 8-year-old`). Age wording follows the server's reference
  normalization, which rewrites "N Years" as "N-year-old". Lettered cask
  numbers are written `No.T333`; a first pass had lowercased them through a
  bug in the local rename script, not the server, and was corrected. A generic `Single Malt` name is used only for the 2025
  standard bottling, whose marketed name is exactly "Single Malt Komagatake".
  The same facts are also stored in `statedAge`, `vintageYear`, and
  `caskNumber`.
- Where Hombo's title has a series-like counter the name holds the range and
  the edition holds the counter: `Limited Edition` with `2018`, `2019`,
  `2020`; `Minami-satsuma Art Collection` with `#01` to `#04`; `1986
30-year-old` with `American White Oak Natural Cask Strength`, `Sherry Cask
Natural Cask Strength`, and `Sherry Cask`.
- Mars (`E1169`) stays the Brand for house blends and for ranges that mix or
  do not name one distillery: Iwai, The Lucky Cat, Maltage, Malt Duo, The
  Y.A., Mars Malt Le Papillon, the Bakemono Zukushi Emaki series, Dakekanba,
  Kasei, Kisoyama, and the historical Yamanashi and Kagoshima era releases.
- `edition` copies the descriptor from Hombo's product title: `2022 Edition`,
  `Limited Edition 2019`, `Bottled in 2020`, `The First`, `Peated`, `#01`,
  `Single Cask Ooruri-shijimi`, `Hana`, `Wine Cask Finish`. For the annual
  Asagi Madara no Sato bottlings the title's year is the edition. For the
  Iwai seasonal finishes sold in the US the importer's name is the edition
  (`Haru`, `Natsu`, `Aki`, `Fuyu`). For export and private single casks the
  edition names the release title or the recipient (`Yori-Kiri`, `Japanese
Spring`, `for K&L Wine Merchants`, `Shinanoya 15th Anniversary`).
- Age, vintage, bottling year, ABV, cask numbers, and outturn are always
  stored in their fields, whether or not they also appear in the name.
- Release dates come from Hombo's product-page date, which matches the dated
  new-product announcements. Only the year and month were stored because that
  page date is a publication date, not a proven on-sale day.
- Bottles with peat levels stated in Hombo's Japanese copy carry
  `maltPhenolPpm`.
- Hombo lists blends as "Produced in UK, Japan" so no distiller was added to
  blends; existing distiller links on blends were left as found.

## Source coverage

- Hombo's product archive: https://www.hombo.co.jp/product_en-sitemap.xml
  and https://www.hombo.co.jp/product-sitemap.xml, with pages at
  `https://www.hombo.co.jp/en/product/<slug>/` and
  `https://www.hombo.co.jp/product/<slug>/`. Each page carries the release
  date, lineup table, and a spec block. The English `TYPE` for Mars Maltage
  3 Plus 25 says "Single Malt" while both descriptions call it a pure malt of
  Kagoshima and Yamanashi malts; it was stored as a blended malt. Bottle
  counts written with a tilde (`˜9,900`) were treated as approximate and not
  stored.
- Hombo's new-product news archive
  (https://www.hombo.co.jp/news/new-product/whisky/) runs from January 2022.
  Older notices were read from Wayback captures of
  `https://www.hombo.co.jp/news/news2016.html` through `news2020.html`, and
  the pre-2016 product site from captures of
  `http://www.hombo.co.jp/item/whisky_brandy/<slug>/`, which hold the 2009
  and 2013 Komagatake single casks and the Malt Gallery 200ml single casks.
- Hombo's company history (https://www.hombo.co.jp/company/history/): whisky
  production from 1949, licence moved from Tsunuki to the Kagoshima factory
  in 1957, Yamanashi factory and Iwai-designed still in 1960, Kagoshima
  factory whisky licence re-acquired in 1969, Yamanashi distilling stopped in
  1972, licence moved to the Shinshu factory in 1985, Shinshu paused 1992 to
  2011, Tsunuki and Yakushima opened in 2016, renamed Komagatake in 2024.
- Hombo's site terms (https://www.hombo.co.jp/en/guide/) reserve all rights
  to images and forbid reproduction without permission, so no Hombo product
  image was stored.
- High Road Spirits (https://www.highroadspirits.com/mars) lists the US
  range: Iwai, Iwai 45, Iwai Rye Style, the seasonal Iwai Tradition finishes,
  the Bakemono Zukushi Emaki series, retailer single casks for K&L Wine
  Merchants, Astor Wines, and Sushi Noz, and Kisoyama. Its sell sheets are
  image-only PDFs. Skurnik distributor pages supplied cask numbers and dates
  for Bakemono #03, #04, #07, #08, and #09. Skurnik gives 61% for Bakemono #08
  where High Road gives 59%, so that ABV was left unknown.
- La Maison du Whisky (https://www.whisky.fr/) product pages supplied the
  Japanese Spring trio, the Sumo collection, the Takahiro Kurashima artist
  casks, The Little Big Book and Antipodes trios, LMDW 60 Ans and 65th
  anniversary casks, Kasei, and Kasei White. Uisuki, Dramtime, whiskynotes.be,
  whiskyexperts.net, jwhisky.com, Mizunara The Shop, AFT Liquor, and
  nomunication.jp covered other export casks.
- Japanese festival, bar, and retailer bottlings came from Hombo festival
  pages, jpwhisky.net, nomunication.jp, waiter.jp bar catalog pages,
  kabukiwhisky.com, shinanoya-tokyo.jp, wakamatsuyasaketen.com, PR Times
  releases from Tokyu Hotels and the Whisky Culture Research Institute, and
  exact Yahoo! Auctions lots whose label photos were read.
- Kokubu Hokkaido notices (https://www.kokubu.co.jp/news/) and hre-net.com
  covered the Hokkaido-only Dakekanba blended malts.
- Pre-1993 releases: Yahoo! Auctions lots with readable labels for Maltage
  Pure Malt 8 Years (特級, Shinshu factory, 43%), Mars Old Whisky (43%,
  Shinshu factory), and Mars Rare Old (1級, 40%, Kagoshima); a Sake no Hosoi
  listing for the 特級 Finest Special Old (43%); a dealer record for Vintage
  Satsuma 1984 20 Years (55%, 1,752 bottles, 2004); a dated 2008 blog for the
  ceramic Maltage Komagatake Sherry Cask 10 Years (1,272 bottles); and a

- Whiskybase, Whisky Auctioneer, Master of Malt, The Whisky Exchange, K&L,
  and Astor blocked automated reads. They were not used as evidence.
- The WebSearch budget ran out during the second research pass; later checks
  used direct page reads only.

## Production result

Before the work: 47 Bottles in the fetched scope (44 in scope). After the
work the Mars scope (Brand `E1169`, Brands `E1953` and `E1969`, plus the
Yamanashi and Kagoshima distilleries) holds 288 Bottles: the 44 original
Bottles and 244 created Bottles. 178 of them were then moved from Brand Mars
to the distillery Brands. A final aggregate
check found no two Bottles with the same full name, age, ABV, vintage,
bottling year, cask number, outturn, and Series. Series membership after the
work: Le Papillon 22, Maltage 13, The Lucky Cat 12, Iwai 11, Bakemono Zukushi
Emaki 9, The Y.A. 7, Malt Duo 2.

### Entities

- Created `E367024` Mars Yamanashi Distillery (closed, 1960, Isawa, Yamanashi)
  and `E367025` Mars Kagoshima Distillery (closed, whisky licence 1969, malt
  distilled to 1984, Minamiei, Kagoshima). The server geocoded the Yamanashi
  address to a wrong street; the address and coordinates were corrected.
- `E1953` Mars Komagatake Distillery: status active, region Nagano, site
  address 4752-31 Miyada, short name "Mars Komagatake", alias "Mars Shinshu
  Distillery". It is now the Brand of the Komagatake single malts.
- `E1969` Mars Tsunuki Distillery: status active, region Kagoshima, short name
  "Mars Tsunuki". It is now the Brand of the Tsunuki single malts.
- `E1169` Mars: status active, fuller description, aliases Mars Whisky,
  マルスウイスキー, and マルス.
- `E366142` Hombo Shuzo: status active, country, region Kagoshima, head office
  address, aliases 本坊酒造 and 本坊酒造株式会社, founded 1872 and whisky
  licence 1949 events.
- All six Entities carry Japanese and former-name aliases and dated history
  events (opened, mothballed, reopened, closed, renamed) sourced from Hombo's
  company history page.

### Series

Created `S929` The Y.A., `S930` Iwai, `S931` Le Papillon, `S932` Maltage,
`S933` Malt Duo, and `S934` Bakemono Zukushi Emaki under Mars, and described
the existing `S918` The Lucky Cat. Komagatake (`S927`) and Tsunuki (`S928`)
Series were created and then deleted the same day when the single malts moved
to the distillery Brands. Nature of Shinshu is a Bottle name under the
Komagatake Brand, not a Series.

## Images and provenance

Stock bottle images were added later the same day, at the owner's request,
following the site's existing practice of using product photos from producer,
importer, and retailer pages with the source page recorded. 140 Bottles gained
an image:

- 132 from Hombo's product pages (`imageSourceUrl` is the product page;
  `imageLicense` records "Hombo Shuzo product image, all rights reserved",
  because Hombo's site terms reserve reproduction rights).
- 7 Bakemono Zukushi Emaki releases from High Road Spirits' downloadable
  trade bottle shots.
- 1 from Wikimedia Commons: Tsunuki Minami-satsuma Art Collection #01, CC
  BY-SA 4.0 by Keeezawa, whose label also confirms the 52% ABV.

Each upload was re-fetched to confirm the stored `imageUrl`, source page, and
licence, and three stored images were inspected visually (Bartenders Choice,
Bakemono #09, Art Collection #01); the Bakemono label confirms cask T1710,
distilled January 2020, bottled June 2025, 59%. The 13 pre-existing images from
earlier price imports keep their retailer source pages and no licence. 133
Bottles still have no image: the distillery-exclusive, festival, private,
export, and historical releases whose only photos are on retailer, bar, blog,
or auction pages, and the US-range items without an importer bottle shot.

Entity photos: Mars Komagatake Distillery keeps its Commons exterior (CC BY-SA
4.0). No reusable photo was found on Commons or Openverse for Mars Tsunuki
Distillery, the Yamanashi or Kagoshima sites, or Hombo Shuzo; brand and
company logos are excluded by the entity image guide.
