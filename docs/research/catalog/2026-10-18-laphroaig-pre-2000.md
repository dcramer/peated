# Laphroaig releases before 2000

This review covers official and independent Laphroaig releases marketed before
2000, including older bottles without an exact bottling year. A nineteenth- or
twentieth-century distillation date does not put a later release in this scope.
The archives below provide broad coverage, but none proves that every historical
release survives in a searchable record.

## Productive sources

- [Laphroaig Collector's sitemap](https://www.laphroaigcollector.com/sitemap.htm)
  leads to separate official 10-year-old, 15-year-old, other official,
  Cadenhead, Signatory, Gordon & MacPhail, and other independent indexes. The
  working independent index is `other2.htm`; `other.htm` is not equivalent.
  Exact bottle photographs are often more reliable than the accompanying table.
  Its Italian-import pages mix Gordon & MacPhail bottlings with separate
  Sestante and Intertrade selections. Read the label and research the selecting
  business before assigning the Brand. The archive extends well beyond 1999.
- [Whiskyraritäten Langer's L index](https://www.whiskyraritaeten-langer.de/whiskyselection/whiskysortiment-12/)
  supplies readable labels for discontinued Cadenhead, Gordon & MacPhail,
  Intertrade, Sestante, Carato, Mackillop's Choice, Murray McDavid, and Wilson &
  Morgan releases. Its historical stock list includes multiple packages of the
  same whisky. Search-tool clicks sometimes return a cache miss even when a
  normal HTTP request works. Decorative header images can return 404 while the
  bottle photograph remains available.
- [Whiskyfun's Laphroaig index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Laphroaig.html)
  finds rare vintages, casks, Italian labels, early SMWS releases, and Japanese
  imports. Follow an entry to its dated tasting report and photograph. An age
  in editorial prose may be calculated rather than stated on the label.
  Its [May 2023 historical tasting](https://www.whiskyfun.com/archivemay23-2-Laphroaig-Ardbeg.html#200523)
  is particularly useful for Murray McDavid and Lombard's Jewels of Islay.
- [The Whisky Exchange's wanted list](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/wanted/laphroaig)
  provides an additional inventory of discontinued official and independent
  releases. Its [Italian bottlers guide](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/wanted/italian-bottlers)
  helps distinguish a selecting bottler from an importer. Neither proves exact
  dates for every entry.
- [The SMWS distillery 29 archive](https://www.tin.org/SMWS-29.html) is a useful
  discovery list for the earliest Society casks. Cross-check its dates, names,
  ages, and bottle counts against exact auctions and contemporary descriptions.
  [SMWS's account of its early bottles](https://smws.com.au/legendary-bottlings-the-societys-early-single-casks/),
  [Whisky Show's 2017 dram list](https://whiskyshow.com/london/past-shows/whisky-show-old-and-rare-2017/13119/dram-list/),
  [Whiskybar Plaza's transcriptions](https://www.whiskybarplaza.nl/laphroaig.html),
  and exact Whisky Auctioneer, Whisky Online, Mark Littler, and Whiskybase pages
  provide independent checks. A subtitle found only in the collector table is
  insufficient for a public name.
- [Whisky Club Italia's Leapfrog history](https://whiskyclub.it/news_337-html/)
  transcribes labels and distinguishes the 1987 whisky bottled in 1999 from
  the 1987 whisky bottled in 2000. The [L.A. Whiskey Society's exact bottle](https://www.lawhiskeysociety.com/whiskey/465/Laphroaig-Leapfrog-1987-Murray-McDavid)
  corroborates the earlier cask. The legal dispute explains the Leapfrog name;
  it is not another distillery.
- Auction catalogs and exact lots fill individual gaps. Useful examples include
  [Christie's Cadenhead 1984](https://www.christies.com.cn/en/lot/lot-6053379),
  [Steinfels' historical catalog](https://www.steinfelsweine.ch/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/397-Katalog.pdf),
  [Dun Eideann 1966](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/8215766/laphroaig-1966-dun-eideann),
  [First Cask 2202](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/12167/laphroaig-1967-28-year-old-first-cask-2202),
  and [Bolaffi's Prime Malt label](https://www.astebolaffi.it/pdf/auctions/630.pdf).
  Auction dates are not release dates. Spirit Radar often repeats Whiskybase
  metadata, so agreement between those sites is not necessarily independent
  corroboration.
- Japanese accounts provide evidence for less widely distributed releases.
  [Kingsbury's 1976 cask](https://sarichiiiii.blog.fc2.com/blog-entry-1632.html),
  [Lombard's 1976 bottle](https://rumlovers.net/?p=625), and
  [Prime Malt Selection No. 1](https://whiskywarehouse.blog.jp/archives/1069401660.html)
  have exact bottle descriptions. [Whiskyrific's Clydesdale 1979 photograph](https://whiskyrific.com/2025/05/19/laphroaig-1979-1997-clydesdale-5963-53/)
  clearly shows the vintage, bottling date, strength, and cask.

## Identity and source conflicts

- Signatory's 1966 cask 1089 label says bottled in 1997, although the collector
  table says 1996. The label controls. Cadenhead's 1984 12-year-old at 58.4%
  likewise has a January 1997 bottling date despite some 1996 listings.
- Cadenhead's index includes the 1970 whisky bottled for Broadwell Vintners.
  The visible merchant label establishes Broadwell as the marketed Brand.
  Duthie's physical packing credit does not establish who selected the cask.
- Prime Malt Selection No. 1 names Laphroaig and Munro Watson & Co. on its
  label. The separate 15-year-old Prime Malt attribution is not established by
  the same evidence.
- The Clydesdale Original Scotch Whisky Company is an independent bottling
  label, not the historical Clydesdale distillery or the similarly named
  blended-whisky company. [The label guide](https://www.hetwhiskyforum.nl/_misc/GuideV44e_doc.pdf)
  connects it with Blackadder. Conflicting establishment dates were not used.
- Dun Eideann's 1966/1997 cask has conflicting calculated ages. The age remains
  unknown. SMWS 29.5 has conflicting outturns, so no count was selected.
  Some auction metadata for SMWS 29.10 incorrectly puts the bottling year in
  the vintage field; the title and independent transcription identify 1991.
- [Blackadder's 1979 cask 5965](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/laphroaig/laphroaig-1979-natural-strength-limited-editions-blackadder-whisky/)
  has different full-bottle and miniature counts. Neither count establishes a
  combined release outturn. The Moon Import Sails label's individual bottle
  number is likewise not an outturn.
- Leapfrog's general 1987 12-year-old record cannot be assigned to cask MM 2868
  or MM 4216 from its saved name alone. Only their shared facts can be filled.
  General import references must not be reassigned to an exact cask by guess.
- The same whisky in another size, importer label, ceramic package, or signed
  bottle is not automatically another release. No extra identity was inferred
  from the Sestante triangular package, different positions of the strength
  text, or signed Erskine bottles.

## Antique authenticity

The Berry Bros. 1903 and 1908 labels need more than a stock photograph or an old
auction listing. [Fine Spirits Auction explicitly identifies the purported
1903 All Malt Berry Bros. & Rudd bottle as fraudulent](https://blog.finespirits.auction/fr/news2/le-vrai-du-faux-12).
[Laphroaig Collector also questions the authenticity of its earliest pictured
bottles](https://www.laphroaigcollector.com/L1900.htm) and explains why old glass,
labels, or a test on another bottle cannot authenticate the liquid.

The separate Mackie & Co. 1903 “Hollow of Proig” lead is also unsafe:
[Rare Whisky 101's published investigation](https://media.rarewhisky101.com/media/04ba2854-4072-4418-9ffa-6c5fa2c02abe.pdf)
reports modern liquid. Do not confuse that bottle with the Berry Bros. label,
or treat the existence of either label as proof of a genuine marketed release.
A [positive tasting account of the 1908 bottle](https://whiskyclub.it/news_351-html/)
mentions testing but does not supply the original report. The original release
and vintage therefore remain unverified. Both Berry Bros. candidates should
remain outside the supported historical inventory pending stronger evidence.

## Remaining questions

- SMWS 29.14 appears in the collector table, but targeted code and subtitle
  searches did not find a readable label or independent exact record.
- Blackadder 1990 casks 11703 at 43% and 60.5%, 11704 at 43%, and 11706 at 43%,
  and Mackillop's Choice 1983 cask 1847, remain leads. Targeted cask searches
  found database listings or copied metadata rather than adequate exact-label
  corroboration. Their absence from this backfill is not a claim they do not
  exist.
- Kingsbury's undated Celtic-label 1988 releases and its 14-year-old Valdespino
  finish lack a supported pre-2000 sale date. A later review does not establish
  that date. One similar Kingsbury 1988 bottle is explicitly a 2003 release.
- Two Cadenhead 1967 13-year-old labels show different months in 1980. Their
  shared year, age, and strength do not resolve whether they need separate
  marketed release records. The month distinction was not converted into an
  invented edition.
- The first Highgrove vintages are already represented. [An exact 1989 lot](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/219157/laphroaig-1989-highgrove)
  dates it broadly to the 1990s, while the collector archive leaves ages and
  precise dates uncertain. Do not copy a speculative 1995 date or a calculated
  age into these records. The official 1976 vintage also has conflicting
  circa-1999/2000 descriptions, so its exact year remains unknown.
- The Erskine appeal needs a date review. [Gavin D. Smith's historical account](https://blog.finespirits.auction/en/news/laphroaig-for-collectors-34)
  says the cask was donated in 1999. [The Italian label transcriptions](https://www.laphroaig.it/it/news/news_322.html)
  distinguish initial fundraising labels from bottles issued at the new
  hospital's opening in October 2000. “Erskine 2000 Appeal” is not sufficient
  proof of a 2000 bottling date, and sources disagree on the total outturn.
- The generic Original Cask Strength records lack the stripe, strength, date,
  or reference evidence needed to resolve an exact historical release.
  The Avery's 1973 selection is an independent release; its existing Brand
  assignment needs separate approval to change.
- The private Werner Hertwig cask does not establish a publicly marketed
  release. Rum finished in a Laphroaig cask is not Laphroaig whisky. Later
  releases, including Leapfrog bottled in 2000 and modern old-vintage whisky,
  fall outside the selected period.

## Images and access

The collector photographs carry Still Publishing copyright. Langer photographs
are watermarked; auction, retailer, and reviewer photographs had no verified
reuse license for Peated. They were inspected as evidence and not uploaded.
Existing licensed official-bottle images were preserved. No exact reusable
image was established for the newly researched independent releases.

The public archives were readable without login. Some search-tool page or image
requests returned cache misses; direct HTTP retrieval of the same public files
usually worked. Broken header images were skipped. No account wall, access
restriction, or image license was bypassed.
