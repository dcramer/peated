# Historical Brora releases

Research dates: 20–22 October 2026. Scope: old Brora single-malt releases,
official and independent, from the 1969–1983 vintages through the documented
2024 releases. This pass followed the
[September Brora guide](./2026-09-07-brora.md), which holds the
family-by-family evidence. The September guide overstated completion: this
pass found eleven releases it had missed. This guide does not claim a complete
census of Brora releases.

Out of scope: blends that contain Brora, pre-1969 whisky sold as Clynelish,
cask samples, whole-cask offers, and size or packaging variants of one release.
Searches for the old Ainslie and Glen Brora names found no further Brora
single-malt release.

## Indexes

These lists find candidates. They do not prove that a release exists, and
they do not list every release.

- [Whiskyfun Brora index](https://www.whiskyfun.com/Brora-Index.html): tasting
  notes across official and independent families. Good for dates and
  strengths, with links to dated reviews.
- [Spirit Radar Brora inventory](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/distilleries/brora/):
  wide coverage, including exact cask records such as
  [Signatory 1981, cask 06/656](https://www.spiritradar.com/whisky-db/brora-1981-sv-cask-strength-collection-60.1-percent-750ml/).
- [Whiskybase Brora table](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/distillery/106/whiskies):
  the header claimed 301 bottlings, but the table exposed only 275 entries on
  20 October 2026. The 26-entry gap is unexplained; it does not mean 26
  releases are missing. Some detail pages return 403 or fail to load.
- [Whisky Monitor](https://www.whisky-monitor.com/index.jsp?did=23): 150
  entries that overlap heavily with Whiskybase. Several entries carry dates,
  strengths, or outturns that no label or auction confirms (see Unresolved).
- [Christie's complete Special Releases collection](https://www.christies.com/en/lot/lot-6405825):
  years, ages, and strengths of all 16 annual Special Releases from 2002 to
  2017, including the three separate 30-year-old releases at 55.7%.
- [Buxrud Rare Malts compilation](https://www.buxrud.se/raremalt.htm): Rare
  Malts Selection releases with US-label notes.

Labels, producer material, and exact auction records outrank index entries.
A miniature can be the only marketed form of a release, so do not skip
miniature listings; but a miniature listing that lacks a vintage or bottling
year does not identify a release either.

## Sources by release family

### Le Clan des Grands Malts (Ian Macleod), 1980, cask 823

- [Whisky Auctioneer bottle record](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/learn/explore-whisky/bottles/brora-1980-le-clan-des-grands-malts-22-year-old):
  vintage 1980, 22 years, bottled 2002, 51%, refill hogshead 823, 345 bottles.
- [K&L product record](https://shop.klwines.com/products/details/1358082):
  confirms cask, count, strength, and cask strength.
- [Whisky Live collector menu](https://www.whiskylive.fr/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/WL19-Collectors-sans-prix.pdf):
  lists the same combination.
- [Whisky-news label photo](https://whisky-news.com/Fr/tastingFR037.html):
  shows the Le Clan des Grands Malts branding and the cask-strength statement.
  No source names an owner or country for the label. Shared bottler is not
  evidence of Dun Bheagan membership.

### D&M Connoisseurs Club, 1982/1997

- [Whisky Auctioneer lot 222404](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/222404/brora-1982-single-malt-connoisseurs-club-14-year-old)
  and the
  [2024 Whiskyfun review](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2024/A-few-Brora-to-celebrate-the-Distillerys-first-whisky-in-38-years.html)
  identify the D&M selection.
- Page 61 of the
  [Perfect Collection auction catalogue](https://whiskyexperts.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Perfect-Collection-Catalogue-Final-Digital-Single.pdf)
  shows the label: 14 years, distilled May 1982, bottled April 1997, 58%,
  single cask, cask strength. The PDF is large; download and render the page.
- Preiss Imports appears as importer. An importer is not a bottler. No source
  gives a cask number or outturn.

### Dun Bheagan (William Maxwell), 1980, cask 821

- [Whisky-Online lot](https://live.whisky-onlineauctions.com/pages/auction-details/brora-1980-2004-23-year-old-dun-bheagan-139384-1658753325):
  front and back label photos show vintage 1980, bottled 2004, 23 years, 50%,
  hogshead 821, 324 bottles, no chill filtration. The web reader missed this
  page; a normal download worked.
- [Sylvie's 2017 catalogue, lots 330–332](https://www.sylvies.be/Downloads/veiling191.pdf):
  confirms the release in indexed text; the PDF itself returned 403.

### Gordon & MacPhail Connoisseurs Choice, 1982

- Bottled 1999, 40%:
  [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/26152/brora-1982-bot1999-connoisseurs-choice),
  [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/brora/brora-1982-bottled-1999-connoisseurs-choice-gordon-macphail-whisky/),
  and a
  [Christie's lot](https://www.christies.com/lot/port-ellendistilled-1981-bottled-1999-1-north-brechindistilled-1981-5568786/?intObjectID=5568786&lid=1).
  The 1982/1999 **Spirit of Scotland** release is a different G&M range.
- Bottled 2010, 43%:
  [Scotch Whisky Auctions lot 177-01833](https://www.scotchwhiskyauctions.com/auctions/226-the-177th-auction/863872-brora-1982-connoisseurs-choice/)
  (refill sherry butts),
  [Just Whisky](https://www.just-whisky.co.uk/product/brora-1982-gm-2010-connoisseurs-choice-1470015),

### Other single facts

- First Cask 1982, 21 years, cask 281: bottled 2003, per
  [Master of Malt](https://www.masterofmalt.com/whiskies/brora/brora-21-year-old-1982-cask-281-first-cask-whisky/)
  and [Whiskyfun, December 2025](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2025/A-Brora-quartet-for-our-Christmas.html).
  The retailer gives a January date that conflicts with its February 1982
  distillation and stated age, so the month is unknown. The review's
  276-bottle count has no second source.
- Prima & Ultima Brora 1981, 40 years, is the Third Release, per the
  [producer FAQ](https://cms-diageocms.diageoplatform.com/media/2lgfjmeu/prima-and-ultima-3rd-release-faq.pdf).
- Old Malt Cask DL1186 (1982, 21 years) is not a new cask 386:
  [The Whisky Vault](https://www.thewhiskyvault.com/brora-1982-21-year-old-the-old-malt-cask-2003-bottling-22934-p.asp),
  [Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/whisky-lot/32395/brora-1982-old-malt-cask-21-year-old).
- Dun Bheagan cask 1526 is 46%, not 48%:
  [Whisky.Auction lot 222831](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/222831/brora-1981-26-year-old).

## Unresolved

- **Special Release 2004 (30 years, 56.6%):** one Peated record stores an
  outturn of 1,515, which conflicts with the documented 3,000 bottles and may
  be a bottle number. It also carries history from a generic 55.7% record.
  Which release is it, and does it duplicate the 2004 record? Needs review
  before any merge.
- **Rare Malts 1975, 20 years, 59.1%:** two records, dated 1995 and 1996.
  Buxrud lists both years; Whisky Monitor lists 1995; retailers and Whiskyfun
  use 1996. Separate releases or label variants? Needs a dated label or batch.
- **Douglas Laing 1971/2000, 29 years, 50%:** releases of 285, 274, and 210
  bottles are known. Whiskybase detail pages 5624 and 11625 were unreadable,
  so which outturn each entry means is unknown.
- **1982, 40 years, cask 573, 49.1%, about 136 bottles:** one specialist
  catalog lead. Searches for "Brora cask 573", "Brora 49.1 136", and
  "Brora 1982 573" found no marketed bottle.
- **1978, 43 years, cask 2704, 48.6%, about 119 bottles:** cask sample or a
  Triptych link? No marketed bottle found.
- **1977, 47 years, cask 2845:** the
  [2025 Tempus report](https://tempusmagazine.co.uk/news/brora-distillery-scotch-whisky-new-age-of-peat/)
  describes a whole-cask offer, not a bottle.
- **H.P. de Vreng 1972, 19 years, 40%, reportedly bottled 1991, Serie no. 6:**
  [Whiskybase lead](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/153431/brora-1972-hpdv),
  a [Japanese collector review](https://sarichiiiii.blog.fc2.com/blog-entry-1675.html),
  and a [Dutch set listing](https://www.2dehands.be/v/verzamelen/wijnen/m2441164382-te-koop-zeer-unieke-set-whisky-miniaturen)
  with differing set counts. Needs a readable label or an independent auction.
- **Murgatroyd Private Casks 1978, 25 years, 56.2%, cask MPCHI1244:**
  [Whiskybase lead](https://www.whiskybase.com/whiskies/whisky/307726) only.
  "UD – bottler" there means an undisclosed bottler, not United Distillers.
- **Rare Malts 1972/22 at 54.9% and 59.1%, and 1974/20 at 57.5%:** compilation
  and import entries conflict; no exact label found.
- **Generic miniatures:** Douglas Laing 26 years at 50% (no vintage or
  bottling year) and G&M 1972 at 40% (no bottling year).
