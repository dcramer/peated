# Entity Cleanup From The About Pages Inventory — October 30, 2026

An About-page inventory left a list of suspect Entities: likely duplicates,
blends and brands filed as Scottish distilleries, missing fields, and missing
distilleries. This record covers where the facts for those Entities live. It is
not a full inventory of any Brand.

## Which Records Were Really Duplicates

Producer home pages settled most pairs, because they use both names or give
the shared address.

- [Lark](https://larkdistillery.com/) now calls its site the Pontville
  Distillery. Its
  [2021 ASX release](https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/1662452/Lark_announces_acquisition_of_Pontville_assets_and_capital_raising.pdf?p=pdf)
  shows that it bought that site from Kernke Family Shene Estate, so Shene and
  Pontville are one place.
- [Archie Rose](https://archierose.com.au/) lists 61 Mentmore Ave, Rosebery,
  but that is the Bar & Cellar Door. Production moved to Banksmeadow in 2020
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archie_Rose_Distilling_Co.)).
  Some sources say Botany; it is the same facility.
- [Backwoods](https://backwoodsdistilling.com.au/) writes both "Backwoods
  Distilling" and "Backwoods Distilling Co." on one page.
- [Ahascragh](https://ahascraghdistillery.com/) signs its own site Ahascragh
  Distillery. Trade press also says Ahascragh Distillers
  ([Shelf Life](https://shelflife.ie/?p=61621)).
- [De IJsvogel](https://www.ijsvogel.com/) titles its site Graanbranderij De
  IJsvogel.

Two pairs were not duplicates:

- Heaven Hill's Bernheim plant is in Louisville. The new Heaven Hill Springs
  Distillery in Bardstown began production in April 2025
  ([Drinks International](https://drinksint.com/news/fullstory.php/aid/11940/.html)).
- [Celtic Whisky Distillerie](https://www.celtic-whisky-distillerie.fr/en/) and
  Glann ar Mor share an address, but Celtic Whisky Compagnie
  [created the distillery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glann_ar_Mor_distillery)
  and the company also sells the Gwalarn blend. The company is the owner, not a
  second distillery.

## Blends And Brands Filed As Distilleries

These records came from an old import that treated a label's largest name as a
distillery. The same import guessed stored ages, and they are not reliable:
half of those checked here were wrong. Trust a retailer or auction age over the
stored one.

- Compass Box: the
  [producer page](https://www.compassboxwhisky.com/products/rogues-banquet)
  survives for Rogues' Banquet. Others came from
  [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/?p=751782) (Magic
  Cask, a blended malt),
  [The Spirits Business](https://www.thespiritsbusiness.com/?p=675835)
  (Phenomenology), [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/58489/compass-box-menagerie)
  (Menagerie), and
  [Abbey Whisky](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/blogs/blog/compass-box-the-circus-and-enlightenment-scotch-whisky)
  (The Circus and Enlightenment).
- Velvet Fig: [Whisky Magazine](https://whiskymag.com/articles/wemyss-malts-announces-return-of-velvet-fig/)
  covers the 2014 original and the 2020 25-year-old. A 12-year-old followed in
  2025 ([The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/86464)).
- Spica and Sirius are North Star Spirits labels. Spica is a blended Scotch and
  Sirius a blended malt.
  [Royal Mile Whiskies](https://www.royalmilewhiskies.com/spica-1989-29-year-old-north-star-blend-45-5-70cl/),
  [WhiskyFun](https://www.whiskyfun.com/2018/Further-mixed-pairs.html),
  [Whisky Auction](https://whisky.auction/auctions/lot/139239/spica-1980-40-year-old-cask-strength),
  and [Abbey Whisky](https://www.abbeywhisky.com/products/sirius-31-year-old-1988-north-star-spirits-aw02859)
  hold the facts. North Star's own site says "The Vega Range" for the sister
  label.
- Home Blend: [The Rum Howler](https://therumhowlerblog.com/whisky-reviews/scotch-whisky/wilson-and-morgan-home-blend-35-year-old-80-15-sw-26-wm208/)
  and [My Annoying Opinions](https://myannoyingopinions.com/2017/01/26/home-blend-35-wm/)
  describe different casks of the 2015 Wilson & Morgan release.
- Islay Trilogy: [The Whisky Exchange](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/7539/islay-trilogy-1969-36-year-old)
  and [Whisky Auctioneer](https://whiskyauctioneer.com/lot/6138265/islay-trilogy-1969-murray-mcdavid-36-year-old-mission-cask-strength).
- Burlington Drinks: [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/search/companies?q=the+burlington+drinks+company+limited)
  has The Burlington Drinks Company Limited, 06310070, incorporated in 2007, in
  Witham, England.

## Distilleries

- Levenbank: [Dram Scotland](https://dramscotland.co.uk/2025/02/24/levenbank-distillery-expands-into-whisky-production)

  of 1 May 2025 reports that no more whisky is being made on the site.

## Conflicts And The Value Used

- Home Blend ABV: 47.6% and 46.7% describe different casks, and iDealwine gives
  a third bottle count. ABV stays unknown because the record does not name a
  cask.
- The General: sources describe parcels of 33 years and about 40 years. Age
  stays unknown.
- Islay Trilogy: the label prints only 1969, but the malts date from 1966, 1968,
  and 1969. Vintage stays unknown, and 1969 stays in the name.
- Hiram Walker opened in 1858 or 1859. Its About page shows the disagreement, so
  the founding year stays unknown.
- White Peak cask 23033: Master of Malt says "Released by Nickolls & Perks", and
  Scotch Whisky Auctions says "Bottled for". Treated as the bottler's own label
  (identity model rule 4). The label itself was not seen.
