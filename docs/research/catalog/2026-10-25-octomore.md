# Octomore source guide (October 25, 2026)

Where the facts for Bruichladdich's Octomore releases live, from the first
Futures casks in 2002 to Series 17 in 2026.

## Shape used in Peated

Decided on October 25, 2026, after a dry run:

- Brand Octomore (Bruichladdich's own line), distiller Bruichladdich, no
  bottler.
- A numbered edition is named for its number without a leading zero: `13.1`,
  `8.4`. The two named early editions keep their names: `2.2 Orpheus`,
  `4.2 Comus`. PPM goes in `maltPhenolPpm` and cask wording in `maturation`,
  never in the name.
- Series exist only for the producer's two range names: **Masterclass**
  (8.1–8.4) and **Dialogos** (9.1–9.3 and the Ten Years Dialogos). The
  numbered yearly series ("Series 13") are release-year sets, not Series.
  "The Impossible Equation" is a tagline, not a Series.
- The Ten Year Olds are named `10-year-old`, with the producer's release label
  as the edition: `First Edition`, `Second Limited Edition`, `Dialogos`,
  `Virgin Oak Influence`, `5th Edition`, `Series 15`.
- The #LaddieMP6 casks are named for their distillation year with edition
  `#LaddieMP6` and the cask in `caskNumber`.
- Festival releases keep the existing per-Brand Fèis Ìle Series; whether
  festival bottlings should be Series at all is a catalog-wide question.

## How the producer groups Octomore

- Each year's set is a numbered **series** ("10s series", "Series 13",
  "Octomore 14 Series", "Series 17"), and each bottle is an **edition** with a
  `.1`–`.4` suffix. Shop titles read `Octomore Edition 15.1`; older archive
  titles read `Octomore Edition 01.1 / 131 PPM / Aged 5 years`; the 2009–2011
  site used `Octomore/2_140`.
- The numbering guide explains the suffixes: `.1` Scottish barley and American
  oak, `.2` European oak (travel retail until series 11), `.3` Islay barley,
  `.4` virgin oak from series 7, and a Ten Year Old sometimes in place of the
  `.4` ([A guide to the Octomore numbering system](https://www.bruichladdich.com/blogs/news/a-guide-to-the-octomore-numbering-system)).
- There was no 3.2 and no 5.2: "3.1 appeared solo… a gap for series five"
  ([Octomore: the anti-logic, Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20201216203416/https://www.bruichladdich.com/bruichladdich-whisky-news/new-releases/octomore-the-anti-logic)).
  There is no 15.4; Series 15's fourth bottle is `Series 15 – Ten Aged Years`.
- **Masterclass** is the producer's name for series 8 (`Octomore
Masterclass_08.1` to `_08.4`). **Dialogos (διάλογος)** covers series 9 and
  the third Ten (`Octomore Ten Years Dialogos`). Spirit Radar tags series 10
  and 11 as Dialogos too; producer pages do not.
- **The Impossible Equation** is a tagline for Octomore as a whole, used from
  2018 to 2026 with no fixed members. Whiskybase lists it as a bottling series;
  that is its own notation.
- OBA means "Octomore Black Art". There is no separate "Octomore Black".
- Rock'ndaal is the name of the distillery's Fèis Ìle releases from 2022. The
  2022 Rock'ndaal 01.1 and 01.2 are Bruichladdich-branded blends of
  Bruichladdich, Port Charlotte, and Octomore, not Octomore releases.

## Access

- bruichladdich.com returns 429 to curl and WebFetch after a few requests,
  including the country hosts. agent-browser reads it, including the
  `.json` product endpoints.
- Wayback holds the most history. The capture index returned "temporarily
  unavailable" when queried during another batch; request one capture at a
  time.
- Whiskybase never cleared Cloudflare in agent-browser. Whisky Auctioneer
  returned 406 and Royal Mile Whiskies 403. Master of Malt was read only as a
  search lead.
- Archived Octomore tasting-note PDFs had no extractable text.

## Conflicts and the value used

- 01.1: the label says 131 PPM, but the producer says it was distilled in
  2002, when the first malt measured 80.5 PPM. Keep the label PPM and the
  stated 2002 distillation.
- 07.2: the archive heading reads "07.1 / 228 PPM"; the title, image, and text
  say 07.2 / 208 PPM. Use 07.2 / 208.
- 2004 Futures "The Beast": offer 46%, bottles 60.5%. Use the bottle.
- Polyphonic ABV: producer 54.9%, Whisky Magazine 55.8%. Use the producer.
- OBA Concept: offered February 2017, shipped April, later "launched in May
  2017". Store 2017 only.
- Fifth Ten: a Whiskybase lead says bottled December 2021; producer images
  appear in January 2022. Release year unresolved.
- Rest & Be Thankful 2008 cask: retailers 63.9%, a Whiskybase list 64.1%. Use
  the retailer and label value.
- Valinch 0.1 cask: "refill virgin oak" versus "new oak". Unknown.
- Master of Malt credits the 2022 and 2023 Valinches to Bunnahabhain; that is
  an error.

## Not Octomore Brand releases

- Rest & Be Thankful Whisky Co. bottled Octomore casks independently: 2007
  Sauternes casks at 6 years (bottled November 2014), a 2008 French oak cask
  at 7 years (316 bottles, bottled May 2015), and a 2009 Tempranillo cask.
  "RBTW" is Whiskybase notation for the bottler. No 7-year-old 2007 bottling
  was found.
- That Boutique-y Whisky Company, Dramfool, Darkness, and The Cask Whisperer
  released their own Octomore bottlings.
- The X4 releases are unpeated Bruichladdich. The 2002 "Octomore Birth"
  valinch is a Bruichladdich valinch named for Octomore's first distillation.
- Never released: an Octomore 18-year-old poured at the 2022 Rock'ndaal
  Masterclass, and Masterclass drams that later became OBA Concept.

## Searches that found nothing

No Octomore Fèis Ìle bottling in 2015–2018, 2020, or 2021; no other Octomore
Micro-Provenance cask beyond #LaddieMP6 (#LaddieMP8 was Port Charlotte); no
producer page for Futures 2009, the three Valinches, or the UK single casks;
no market-only Octomore in Taiwan, Japan, or Germany. A Whiskybase lead for a
2012 Octomore Farm 10-year-old at 63.2% was not confirmed anywhere.

## Images

Official producer bottle images exist, live or on Wayback, for every numbered
edition, the Tens, OBA Concept, X4+10, Polyphonic, and OBA Redux; Catalog
Maintenance allows them with the producer's copyright notice. None was found
for the Futures, Event Horizon, the Valinches, or the UK single casks.
Discovery has only an article image and #LaddieMP6 only a photo of sample
labels.
