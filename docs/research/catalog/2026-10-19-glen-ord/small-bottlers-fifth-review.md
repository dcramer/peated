# Smaller independent bottlers: fifth group

Reviewed 21 remaining independent records plus newly created Liquid Library 54.2%. Seventeen bottle updates and Scotch Universe Alien Edition series 1324 were verified. Exact sources and per-record uncertainties are in release-review.csv.

Source labels resolved the Kyoto festival strength to 54.2%, Shinanoya cask 700429 outturn to 314, and Selected Malts production flags. The Selected Malts label does not state an age even though dates let a reviewer calculate five years. Chichibu Reiwa’s 200 lottery units and Scotch Malt Sales’ 58 allocated bottles are not release outturns. Unknown fields remain unknown.

Producer portfolios, contemporaneous announcements, English/German/French/Japanese sources and exact auctions were checked. Mackillop’s Choice 1983/21-year remains unresolved; collector-only ABV/cask assertions do not authorize changing its identity. A separately marketed Ordoculus range is supported by the producer’s August 2026 launch statement, rather than inferred from artwork.
