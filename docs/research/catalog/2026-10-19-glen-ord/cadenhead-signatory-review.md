# Cadenhead and Signatory evidence review

Review covers Cadenhead releases from the 1962 vintage through 2025 bottlings,
and Signatory releases from 1983 through 2026 bottlings. Exact source links and
fields changed appear in changes.csv. This closes the evidence pass for these
families while retaining the questions below. It does not close the whole
distillery catalog.

## Cadenhead

The [producer's historical ranges](https://www.cadenhead.scot/our-spirits/whisky/historical-ranges/)
establish natural colour and no chill filtration for past and present releases.
Authentic Collection and the historical Single Cask range are bottled at cask
strength. The Small Batch range includes reduced-strength 46% releases, so
its name alone does not establish cask strength or the number of casks.

Readable labels establish the stable name Ord for most Cadenhead releases.
The Duthies release uses Glen Ord. Club and Single Cask are distinct producer
ranges; Series 1318 and 1319 were created and their memberships verified.
Single Cask contains 67214, 67225, 67535, 67536, 67538 and 67636.

Two additional releases were confirmed from labels and exact sale records:

- 67849: Ord, 16 years, December 1962 to July 1979, 45.7%. The
  [Sotheby's label](https://www.sothebys.com/en/buy/auction/2021/rare-whisky-cognac-live-the-macallan-anecdotes-of-ages-the-bo-johnston-collection/glen-ord-16-year-old-cadenheads-dumpy-80-proof-2)
  prints both 80 proof and 45.7 G.L.; ABV was read directly, not calculated.
- 67850: Ord Small Batch, 10 years, 2004–2015, 60.9%. The
  [Whisky Antique record and photograph](https://www.whiskyantique.com/en/ord-10-years-old-2004-2015-70cl-60-9-cadenhead-s-small-batch-wo0174i)
  distinguish this from the existing 60.5% release. A January 2016 price list
  corroborates 10 years and 60.9%; its sherry claim was not imported.

## Images, names and references

All starting aliases, references and edit contexts were checked. No Cadenhead
public aliases or stored images were present. Signatory aliases were empty;
the exact references on 65680 and 45792 were retained. Every changed group has
one Bottle and all edits were fetched and verified afterward.

Exact images were used as evidence, including old Cadenhead labels, the two
whic releases and Signatory cask 377. No new image was attached: source pages
and image hosts did not establish permission to reuse their photographs.
