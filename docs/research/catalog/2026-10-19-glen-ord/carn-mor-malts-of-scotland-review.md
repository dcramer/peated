# Càrn Mòr and Malts of Scotland review

Reviewed ten Càrn Mòr and nine Malts of Scotland releases. Coverage includes Càrn Mòr Vintage Collection and Strictly Limited, 2004–2012 vintages and 2009–2023 bottlings; and Malts of Scotland standard, Warehouse, Rare Casks, Innocent Dram and Special releases, 1996–2012 vintages and 2010–2025 bottlings. The full Càrn Mòr producer archive and Malts of Scotland shop search were compared with Peated. The shop search had one page and three Glen Ord products, all already present. These current archives do not prove complete historical coverage.

Readable producer and owner labels resolved the Japan ten-year-old versus retailer eight-year typo and identified the full Dram Brothers Whisky Society & Whisky World Massen edition. Special Range was created from the producer’s named range and assigned to its Christmas 2016 and partner selection. Other uncertain membership was retained. Bottle-level evidence, unknowns and searches are in [release-review.csv](release-review.csv).

Nineteen Bottle updates were applied and checked against fresh production records. Existing age, bottling year and cask facts were preserved where independent evidence remained weak. In particular, MoSFS005 and MoS14050 need exact labels for missing facts, and the Càrn Mòr six-year auction has conflicting title and detail vintages. No additional release was created from those conflicts.

Exact images were inspected for identity, but no reusable license or permission was established. Canonical sources are saved; no images were uploaded. Access failures were respected. No merge, deletion or identity replacement was performed.
