# Smaller independent bottlers: first group

Reviewed 21 releases from Acla, Adelphi, Animal Spirits, Archives, Artful Dodger, Chorlton, Fadandel, Hidden Spirits, Lady of the Glen, Maltbarn, Milroy’s and Or Sileis. This pass covers 1997–2015 vintages, 2010–2025 bottlings and releases, with Swiss, Danish, Japanese, Singapore and Taiwanese selections.

Producer archives and current pages were compared with exact auction records, contemporary distributor lists and owner labels. Acla Classic 1321 and Hidden Spirits Highproof 1322 were created and verified, followed by eleven Bottle updates. Earlier changes for these records remain in the event ledger. [release-review.csv](release-review.csv) records all 21 decisions and retained unknowns.

Acla’s explicit 2022 bottling date overrides the review’s 2023 claim; its producer calls this part of Classic 2023. Hidden Spirits’ Singapore back label establishes 174 bottles and its front label cask GO0922. The Australian ten-year product page has conflicting vintage/strength/distillery metadata and was not used to alter the well-supported 2020/53.3% release. Maltbarn’s archive resolves Whiskyfun’s impossible 2021/2021 nine-year listing as 2012/2021.

Fadandel’s full historical table lists both known Glen Ords but omits the older cask and outturn. Maltbarn’s full archive contains the known nine-year release; the independent 2025 Swiss event newsletter documents the second. Adelphi’s archive says Coming Soon, so its two releases were checked against exact auctions and contemporary importer material. Lady of the Glen’s producer archive and Japanese export listings were compared in the earlier discovery pass. These sources do not prove a complete worldwide historical list.

Animal Spirits’ cask remains unknown because 200260 and 800260 conflict and its readable front label does not resolve them. Or Sileis’ three Taiwanese octave releases remain supported identities, but collector attribution to The Single Cask requires an independent original-label check before a bottler-role change. No role change, merge or deletion was made.

Stored Artful Dodger imagery matches its exact producer bottle and cask; reproduction permission is not established. No images were uploaded. Additional discovery-only leads include Berry Bros. cask 124 (2007, seven years, 57%) and Acredyke Glen Ord 1970/2004 at 52.8%. Exact-code, age/strength, alternative spelling and archive searches did not produce independent corroboration; no records were created for them.
