# Official Glen Ord and Singleton releases

Completed evidence review of 55 original and newly added official records. Per-record evidence, uncertainty and field decisions are in release-review.csv. The review covers historical Ord/Glenordie, core Singleton, Rare Malts, Special Releases, managers’ bottlings, hand fills, travel retail, Forgotten Drop, Epicurean Odyssey and Gourmand releases.

The same age can identify substantially different products: old 38/40% versus Epicurean 38/49.6%; old Masters Casks 40/40% versus Epicurean 40/45.9%; older 35/1284 bottles versus Summer & Winter/999 bottles. Regional allocations are not global outturns. Producer spelling and stable names were retained; redundant cask, age and release details were removed from names where safe.

The generic fourteen-year record 13994 remains unresolved. No merges were made. Vintage and release conflicts on the old 32- and 35-year products remain documented. The producer menu itself prints 55.1% on page 2 and 55.4% on page 4 for the eleven-year hand fill; 55.1% remains a lead requiring an exact label. The 55.4% release has separate auction evidence. Other cask/batch-only hand-fill leads are not automatically separate records.
