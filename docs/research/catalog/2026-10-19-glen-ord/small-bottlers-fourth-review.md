# Smaller independent bottlers: fourth group

Reviewed 27 older independent releases. Twenty-two additional bottle updates and Monnier Oldtimer Collection series creation were verified. Producer catalogues, exact auction lots, original merchant pages and firsthand bottle reviews establish the supported facts in release-review.csv.

Important conflicts remain: Clydesdale CO389 date prose is inconsistent with its fifteen-year label; Classic Cask 1965 has a 40-versus-43 percent owner-list conflict; Liquid Library 50.4 percent has thirteen-versus-fourteen-year retailer text. Existing identity facts were preserved. A separate Liquid Library 54.2 percent release was created as 67853 after duplicate review. Samaroli’s private 2004/2021 cask 97 for Stefano Core was subsequently confirmed by the exact auction page, which explicitly identifies Samaroli as bottler, and created as 67858. It remains distinct from MASAM 2005/2018 cask 97.

The Royal Mile forty-year release is explicitly cask strength, despite its low 40.1 percent strength. Boutique-y Batch 1 and Batch 2 have identical age/strength but different batch identifiers and outturns, so remain separate. No merges, deletions, inferred dates or unlicensed image uploads were made.
