# Five independent bottler families

Reviewed all 30 inventoried releases: A.D. Rattray (7), Blackadder (6), Hart Brothers (6), Berry Bros. & Rudd (5), and Thompson Bros. (6). Coverage spans 1996–2013 vintages and 2010–2026 bottlings, including Three Rivers, Japan imports, Singapore and other export searches, K&L, Aberdeen Whisky Shop, Whiskynet, Le Gust and Dornoch Castle selections.

The full Glen Ord filtered A.D. Rattray single-cask archive returned five releases, all present; vintage and limited archives supplied no further release. Its older 1998 and Three Rivers export bottles were checked separately. Hart Brothers' historical archive includes both 2004 releases; later editions were checked through distributors and original labels. Berry Bros. producer labels supplied missing age, outturn and Glens & Valleys membership. Blackadder producer policy, importer labels and exact auction/merchant records were compared. Thompson producer pages, older reviews and exact auctions were compared with all six existing records.

Twenty-five updates were applied and verified. Bottle-level sources, conflicts and retained unknowns are in [release-review.csv](release-review.csv). In particular, Berry cask Z17/01075's label says 261 bottles while webpage prose says 246; an exact auction corroborates the label. Cask Z16/09090's label states 16 years and 75 bottles, but does not establish its bottling year. Blackadder cask 21's 40-bottle Potstill mention remains an allocation/edition lead pending an exact label.

Thompson's [Mystery Malt Series 6](https://www.thompsonbrosdistillers.com/product/mysterymaltseries6/) lists a 13-year Glen Ord component of the assortment (356 bottles, 46.3%, decharred/recharred cask). Peated's existing 47418 is the generic mystery product with no distillery, offered at the same strength. The producer sells randomly assorted concealed bottles rather than a blended whisky. The generic record was not assigned exclusively to Glen Ord, and no potentially overlapping revealed variant was created without resolving that identity model. Searches of earlier numbered mystery editions did not establish another exact Glen Ord release.

Images were inspected for identity. The existing Thompson 2012 label matches its producer release. No reusable permission was established for new images, so none was uploaded. No merges or deletions were made.
