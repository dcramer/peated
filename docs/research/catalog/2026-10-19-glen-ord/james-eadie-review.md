# James Eadie review

Reviewed all 11 existing records against the producer’s current and past releases, the selecting pub, Danish and German merchants, Tyndrum and auction records. Coverage includes 2008–2015 vintages, 2020–2025 bottlings, Small Batch, Cask Finish, Distilleries of Great Britain & Ireland, and Sweden, Denmark, export, Royal Mile Whiskies, Ensign Ewart and Inverness exclusives.

The Peacock was a misidentified Linkwood release. Its distiller was corrected earlier while preserving its Bottle ID. The other ten records identify Glen Ord. Bottle-level findings and exact sources are in [release-review.csv](release-review.csv). The Swedish outturn, Inverness outturn and Distilleries of Great Britain & Ireland cask-strength status remain unknown after specific searches. No new release was established beyond the compared inventory.

No images were uploaded. The exact Danish label was inspected; producer image requests returning 403 were not bypassed. No reuse permission was established. Aliases, import references, range membership and complete catalog fields were reviewed.
