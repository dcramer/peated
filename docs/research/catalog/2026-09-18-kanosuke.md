# Kanosuke catalog sources — September 18, 2026

Where the information for Kanosuke Distillery (嘉之助蒸溜所, Hioki, Kagoshima;
owner Komasa Jyozo) lives, found during the September 18, 2026 catalog pass.
Scope: every whisky release from the first single malt in June 2021 to
September 2026, the sister-site Hioki Pot Still whisky sold under the
Kanosuke Brand, and independent bottlings that name the distillery. Recheck
each site before use. The Brand, Series, and bottler decisions this pass
settled are the six rules in `docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`.

## Conflicts settled

- Distillery Exclusive #003 ABV: 88bamboo 57%, producer products index 55% (series ABV); producer used.
- 2022 Limited Edition release: secondary sources say June 15; old-site news says the date moved to June 24 after a label ABV misprint.
- K&L/Acorn cognac cask: importer text 18104 at 56%, resellers 21494 at 57%; the label photo reads 18014 at 56%.
- Artist Edition #002 vintage: two independent sources say 2019; no source supports 2018.
- Outturns left unknown: Whisky & Spirits Festival 2026 Yokohama (organizer 240, reseller 252), Casa de Vinos 15th (retailer 196, reference 198), Crane & Sky #02 (organizer 235, label 234), Owners Club for Sushi+Soul (33 on the bottle, 31 per a German shop).
- Modern Malt Whisky Market 2021 "IPA cask finish cask 20049" is Distillery Exclusive #001's cask; the 70 cl and 50% in two listings conflict with the producer's 200 ml at 55%.
- Thompson Bros cask 20463 and The Whisky Crew's "Japanese Trail" share one cask and outturn under two labels.
