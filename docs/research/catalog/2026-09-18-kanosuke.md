# Kanosuke — September 18, 2026

This production review covered every whisky release from Kanosuke Distillery
(嘉之助蒸溜所, Hioki, Kagoshima; owner Komasa Jyozo, `E366135`) from its first
single malt in June 2021 to September 2026: the core range, the annual
Limited Editions, the Artist Editions, the 200 ml Distillery Exclusive and
Component Series bottles, travel-retail and airline editions, distillery and
third-party festival bottles, casks bottled for Japanese and export
customers, the sister-site Hioki Pot Still whisky sold under the Kanosuke
Brand, and independent bottlings that name the distillery. Writes were made
through the authenticated production API during the afternoon of September
18, 2026, Pacific time. Re-fetch records before reusing any ID here.

## Method

1. The Peated inventory was the union of every Bottle for Brand `E1097`,
   distiller `E1097`, distiller `E1775`, and name searches for Kanosuke and
   Komasa: 12 Bottles and 2 Series (`S0373` Brush Stroke, `S0424` The
   Awakening Series) before the work started.
2. The outside inventory started from kanosuke.com: the Shopify product feeds
   in Japanese and English (75 products each; ABV and size are on the product
   page, not in the feed), the news and global-news blogs (2017–2026), the
   company page, and the trade-law page. The site was rebuilt in March 2025
   and dropped most 2018–2024 product posts, so the old WordPress site was
   read on the Wayback Machine: yearly news indexes at `/news/<year>/`, the
   products index, and the `/special/<slug>/` spec pages.
3. Three research passes ran in parallel: the producer archive, export and
   private casks by market, and a collector cross-check from auction, review,
   and news archives. Every record created from their findings was checked
   against the cited page, and the facts on the existing records were read on
   the source pages directly.
4. Identity used Brand, distillery, age or NAS, ABV, vintage, bottling year,
   edition, cask number, and Series. Package size (200 ml, 700 ml), gift
   boxes, and special labels for the same liquid never created a second
   Bottle.
5. Writes used explicit production IDs. Every created Bottle was re-fetched
   and compared field by field with its request (90 checked, no
   differences). Every patched Bottle was read immediately before the patch
   and re-read after it (10, no mismatches). Both deleted Series were fetched
   with zero members first.

## Brand and naming decisions

The Brand-versus-Series questions this review raised were settled in
"Who Is The Brand, Series, And Bottler" in
`docs/architecture/whisky-identity-model.md`, and the records follow those
six rules.

- Kanosuke `E1097` is the Brand and distiller of its own line (rule 1), with
  short name `Kanosuke`. Hioki Pot Still whisky keeps the Kanosuke Brand
  with Komasa's separate Hioki Distillery as distiller (rule 6); its category
  is `single_pot_still` per the categories baseline (pot stills, malted and
  unmalted barley), and Double Distillery (malt plus pot still) is `blend`.
- Official expressions use the producer's title without the Brand: `Single
Malt`, `Hioki Pot Still`, `Double Distillery`, `Single Malt Peated`,
  `Single Malt Kagoshima Exclusive`, `Single Malt Sherry Casks Vatted`,
  `Single Malt IPA Cask Finish`. Dated or numbered descriptors are the
  `edition`: `2021 First Edition`, `2023 Limited Edition`, `Artist Edition` +
  `#004`, `Distillery Exclusive` + `#010`, `Component Series` +
  `Ex-Sherry Cask`, `The Mellow Bar Reserve`, `Distillery Fest. 2025`.
- Casks bottled for a customer are official bottlings with no bottler (rule
  5): name `Single Cask <vintage> #<cask> [<age>-year-old]` as the source
  prints it, and the customer or program in `edition` (`for Isetan`,
  `Chichibu Whisky Matsuri 2025`, `Brush Stroke`, `The Awakening Series`,
  `A Pacific Ocean Collaboration`). Where a retailer sells under its own
  product title, that title is the name (`2020 1st Fill Bourbon Cask #20496`).
- Independent bottler labels are Brand and bottler (rule 4): The Heart Cut
  (name `Kanosuke`, edition `#21`), The Ghost Series (editions `No. 16`,
  `No. 23a`, `No. 23b`, `No. 23c`), Whisky Mew (Drifters label), T&T Toyama,
  Glover Collection.
- The 2025 JAL Limited Edition package and the Conran Shop art label are the
  core Single Malt with a different label, so they became Bottle references on
  `B57458`, not Bottles.
- Owner's Cask bottles that a bar or shop sold to the public were created;
  private or restaurant-only casks were left out of scope. Unaged New Born
  and New Pot spirit, the Lawson highball can, Mellowed Kozuru shochu, hand-
  fill bottles, and non-sale set components were recorded as out of scope.
- Ages were stored only when printed; vintage ranges (2017–2019) stay null.
  Outturns with conflicting sources stay null: Whisky & Spirits Festival 2026
  Yokohama (organizer 240, reseller 252), Casa de Vinos 15th (retailer 196,
  import reference 198), Crane & Sky #02 (organizer 235, label 234). US
  importer "bottles for the U.S." counts are allocations, not outturns.

  `Kanosuke` with short name `Kanosuke`, address 845-3 Kaminokawa,
  Hiyoshi-cho, Hioki, Kagoshima 899-2421, coordinates 31.6037 N 130.3358 E,
